ACCESS IS4S social-skill teaching resources

Use these materials with Communication, Collaboration, Adaptability and Creativity and Innovation. They provide complete classroom cases, individual evidence criteria, teaching routes and practical delivery records. No real community event, operating procedure or private personal information is needed to complete the tasks.

Resource Use
Learner fresh cases “Communication practice C · Send, receive and check a fresh classroom change”, “Collaboration practice C · Make a resource handover together”, “Adaptability practice A · Revise a small pack and carry out the next step” and “Creativity and innovation practice C · Design a clear choose-and-check display”; separate sender/receiver/role/change cards and later practice versions. Distribute the assigned card at the appropriate time.
Instructor key Essential facts, six-component criterion anchors, support distinctions, individual observation records and clearly fictional judgement examples. Keep model answers separate until the relevant attempt is complete.
Practice route cards Core, support and retry choices; all 18 chapter activities located in a practice route for each skill.
Teaching plans Two teaching sessions per skill, sequential cohort-12 observation bookings with one instructor, individual role/tool turns and reserved recovery capacity.
Local delivery and review records Real-contact insert, access/language arrangements, recording choices, scoped review/permission records, pilot and moderation forms.

Published cards are not guaranteed to be unseen. Ask about prior exposure; record familiar practice honestly and choose a different checked source when unfamiliar reception matters. Appropriate access support is recorded without automatically treating it as another person solving the task. Preserve the difference between a group product and each person's observed contribution.

These teaching plans and blank records are ready for local preparation and review. Actual instructor piloting, learner feedback, local Indigenous review, permissions for any proposed local contribution and moderation of real learner evidence remain to be carried out and recorded. Do not fill in those outcomes from fictional examples or assume approval from silence. Learners retain a complete fictional route and choose whether to share personal or cultural knowledge.
