The fictional Community Learning Day provides all facts and quantities. No other course or project is required.

The learner and instructor books include the complete learning sequence. The instructor book also includes the full case, calculation key, observation criteria and a fresh Learning Exchange assessment. Each activity appears by its descriptive name in the reading editions; internal IDs serve only authoring and coverage checks.

The media example includes original SVG scenes, a synthetic Flite narration, MP3/WAV audio, a captioned MP4, SRT/WebVTT captions, transcript, storyboard and flashcards. It is not attributed to a commercial AI generation service or a real speaker. The teaching sequence explains how to use available AI tools to plan, create and audit equivalent media.
