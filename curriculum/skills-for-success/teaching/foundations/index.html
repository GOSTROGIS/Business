Foundation teaching and fresh checks

These ACCESS IS4S teaching resources develop the first five skills through direct instruction, practice and fresh checks. They are local classroom checks, not standardized assessments, official proficiency levels or occupational qualifications. All people, transactions and figures in the cases are fictional.

For each skill, begin with the practitioner notes. The route card connects a short starting observation to an optional entry bridge, core teaching, a fresh check and further practice. Give learners only the selected learner-checks form during a check. Keep the practitioner key and the other form separate. Form A and Form B are designed to sample comparable demands; their equivalence has not been established through testing. Record prior exposure and use a different form if its solution is already familiar.

The bridge pages deliberately include demonstrations and feedback. They are teaching resources, so do not score a learner's copied demonstration as a fresh independent performance. The check pages contain the necessary source information and tasks without completed models. Use CK-RD, CK-WR, CK-NU, CK-DG and CK-PS identifiers to distinguish these fresh checks from earlier practice.

Choose supports with the learner. An agreed calculator, keyboard alternative, large print, speech-to-text or other access tool does not automatically mean a practitioner supplied the answer. Record the tool and the particular action being assessed. Record task-solving help separately, with its words or action. Use Not observed, Not yet demonstrated, Demonstrated with support, or Demonstrated independently against each criterion. Give feedback and another opportunity. Do not average these descriptions into a national skill level.

A learner can choose the fictional cases without explaining identity, personal circumstances or cultural knowledge. No case requires contact with an outside person, a real purchase or disclosure of credentials. Specific local knowledge is included only through the appropriate relationships and permission; the materials do not invent Nation-specific teachings.

The criteria concern the stated actions, not every component of a whole skill. The teacher checks timing, access and usefulness through an actual pilot; no completed pilot or validated score gain is claimed.
