# Visual index — ACCESS IS4S

[Preview illustrated pages](visual-sampler.pdf) · [Learner book](learner-textbook.pdf) · [Practitioner guide](practitioner-guide.pdf)

**Edition 3 · 29 October 2026.** The visual library contains **43 active, editable SVG graphics**: 41 used in the Skills for Success books and two in the roadbuilding lessons. Four fictional illustrations add human context. The diagrams show quantities, decisions, processes and relationships in the ACCESS cream, ink, red and gold palette. Each linked SVG opens separately for inspection or editing.

## Explore the visuals

![A planning notebook, tablet, revision note and pencil form a connected learning scene beside an index of all nine Skills for Success.](assets/diagrams/cover-nine-skills.svg)

The cover brings planning, trying and reviewing into one learning scene.

![Three eighths becomes six sixteenths; adding five sixteenths gives eleven sixteenths. Regrouping four and one eighth as three and eighteen sixteenths makes subtraction of two and three sixteenths visible, leaving one and fifteen sixteenths.](assets/diagrams/edition3-fractions-regrouping.svg)

Fraction strips connect equivalent parts, regrouping and a check using addition.

![A cycle connects a prediction and reason, a small trial under recorded conditions, observable actions and help needed, and a decision to keep, adjust or retest. Opening two folders is an observation; a label being hard to find is a possible explanation.](assets/diagrams/edition3-prediction-trial-cycle.svg)

A small trial turns a proposed solution into evidence for the next decision.

![A starting task branches to foundation practice or the core task. Both paths lead to a fresh check, then a choice to practise a part, use the skill again or transfer it to another task.](assets/diagrams/edition3-learner-route.svg)

The chapter route makes room for different starting points, useful support and another attempt.
