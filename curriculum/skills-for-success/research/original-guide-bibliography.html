The original bibliography is the authority for layout and wording. Wrapped URLs, typographic artefacts and misplaced cross-reference notes can occur in extraction and in the supplied source. They are not silently corrected here. The machine-readable inventory retains the complete text of each source page and extracted external URI annotations for inspection.

This index preserves research leads. Before relying on an outside work, retrieve the relevant edition, read the needed passage, check its population and context, and record what was actually accessible. Current, independently checked sources are identified separately in the practitioner chapters and project research.
