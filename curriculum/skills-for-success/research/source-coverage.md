# Coverage of the full 148-page supplied SkillPlan guide

This crosswalk accounts for the entire user-supplied **Skills for Success Get Started Guide for Practitioners**, revised March 2024. It distinguishes material rewritten as original ACCESS IS4S teaching content from historical material and external references retained as an index. It does not claim that every outside publication cited by SkillPlan was retrieved, verified or adopted.

The source is the [supplied PDF](../../asphalt-paving/sources/SFS-Get-Started-Guide_FINAL_MAR2024.pdf). Its SHA-256 is `32dad4699b1ef1ab281ad4bdf95bef310048741703fc401094c57f6c62e33caf`. PDF pages and printed folios correspond where a folio is shown; the cover, contents and section-three divider have no extracted printed folio. All references below use PDF page numbers.

The [machine-readable inventory](sfs-inventory.json) includes all 148 page records, complete extracted source text, exact six-component labels, source strategy titles, activity summaries, appendix sequences, handouts, six professional-development listings, 122 parsed bibliography entries and 194 unique external URI annotations. Its relative paths resolve from this research directory.

**Coverage totals:** 148 of 148 pages; 31 nonoverlapping page subsections; six overarching approaches; nine skills; 54 components; 27 instructional strategy groups; 27 grab-and-go patterns; three appendix lessons; six source handouts; six historical professional-development listings. The new learner activities provide 18 numbered opportunities per skill (162 total), with answer guidance. These are inventory counts, not evidence of equivalent instructional depth, learner competence or program effectiveness. The treatment labels below identify concrete adaptations, narrower coverage and deliberate replacements; they do not certify equivalence to the source or to an official proficiency assessment.

## Deliberate corrections and limits

- Historical national statistics and economic projections are identified as historical; they are not republished as current findings or deficit claims about Indigenous learners.
- Document Use is integrated across the nine skills. Continuous learning is transversal; historical and current frameworks are not forced into a one-to-one replacement chart.
- The Numeracy component-six heading error on p. 63 and connection-label issue on p. 70 are corrected using the source component list. Digital p. 77’s copied numeracy teaching tips are replaced by relevant digital-safety instruction.
- Eye contact, stillness, accent and emotional display are not universal assessment criteria. Teaching observes understanding, contribution, selected strategies and supported performance.
- Punitive or deficit-framed examples are replaced with original, bounded classroom cases. Cultural knowledge and creative work are not treated as freely reusable resources.
- Measurement uses suitable safe objects and supplied fixed values; body measurements, traffic-area tasks and tape-extension contests are not carried forward. Illustrations are not treated as physical measuring instruments.
- Cause maps and why questions identify hypotheses and possible system conditions; they do not prove a single cause. Creativity tests and small improvement trials support limited conclusions.
- Writing evidence can use local drafts; no external message, account creation or disclosure is required. The email lesson supplies its own original instruction and model.
- All proposed teaching durations, fictional facts and local feedback descriptions are distinguished from program requirements, current technical standards and official proficiency ratings.

The inventory’s source-issues array retains the page-specific rationale. Current online research supports selected updates; it does not transform the whole original bibliography into a verified evidence base. Local review, learner feedback and any required community permissions remain specific to the eventual delivery context.
