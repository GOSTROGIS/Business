Two substantial companion books expand the complete 148-page Skills for Success Get Started Guide for Practitioners, revised March 2024, into original ACCESS Indigenous Skills 4 Success teaching prose and practice. The nine Skills for Success are taught in BC learning, work and community contexts, with asphalt paving as the starting trade connection. The colour, type and badge follow the ACCESS Numeracy Hub.

Visual teaching edition

The books now lead with 38 SVG assets, including a visual map and two case diagrams for every skill. Across the books there are 67 SVG placements and four people-focused illustrations. Annotated notices, proportional time and cost diagrams, dimensioned layouts, file-version views, equipment sketches, feedback loops, prototype comparisons and lesson diagrams make the reasoning visible. A figure from the supplied guide is also reused with attribution. Preview the redesign.

Worked visual checks provide model responses after practice; use the changed cases for independent evidence. On narrow screens, scroll a diagram within its frame or use its full-size link. PDFs retain vector clarity when enlarged.

What is included

Book Contents
Learner textbook Nine full skill chapters, all 54 components, worked examples, complete fictional documents and datasets, 162 numbered skill activities, two integrated projects with 12 further activities, a glossary and a learning portfolio.
Practitioner guide Framework and transition from Essential Skills; six teaching approaches; nine skill teaching chapters; three complete 90-minute lessons with handouts and answers; assessment and practitioner development; answer guidance for all 174 learner activities; online research, source coverage and the original bibliography index.

The practitioner guide reworks the full instructional scope of the supplied guide. It preserves the original bibliography as an attributed index rather than implying that every listed publication was independently read. The coverage crosswalk accounts for all 148 source pages, 54 components, 27 instructional strategy groups, 27 grab-and-go patterns, three appendix lessons and professional-development resources. Content equivalence is demonstrated through this mapping, not by padding the new books to an identical page count.

Start learning

  1. Use Start here to choose a useful goal and agree supports.
  2. Start with Reading, Writing or Numeracy, or choose the skill connected to your goal.
  3. Follow the explanation and worked example, complete an activity, then review it using the practitioner answer guidance.
  4. Bring the skills together in the two integrated projects, and retain evidence in the portfolio.

Sources and BC Indigenous direction

The original 148-page SkillPlan PDF, asphalt program outline and program profile are preserved unchanged in the source folder.

Online research records current federal Skills for Success pages and verified BC education sources, alongside access limits. Source metadata, the complete original-guide inventory and bibliography index make the research traceable.

The teaching voice recognizes adults' existing strengths, individual agency, contemporary Indigenous experiences and responsibilities to people and place. Learners can use fictional cases or choose suitable examples from their lives. No one is required to disclose personal history or cultural knowledge. Local Nations and community partners direct Nation-specific content, language, protocols and knowledge. The books do not invent teachings or claim Indigenous community endorsement.

This is an instructor-review and pilot edition, not an approved certification instrument. Feedback describes evidence from a task and the support used; it does not assign official proficiency levels. Digital, Writing and interpersonal evidence retain their own task requirements. Current workplace rules and equipment instruction belong in the supervised trade program.

Rebuild and review

Editable Markdown is the source of truth. The committed HTML pages support reading in a browser when served locally or hosted as static files; GitHub displays their source. The PDFs can be downloaded directly from GitHub.

python -m pip install -r curriculum/skills-for-success/tools/requirements.txt
python curriculum/skills-for-success/tools/build_books.py
python curriculum/skills-for-success/tools/verify_books.py --require-generated

Chromium must be installed. Set IS4S_CHROMIUM_PATH to an alternate Chromium executable if needed. Use --html-only to build without PDF export. The builder serves repository files briefly on localhost to render them, uses bundled fonts, and converts PDF links to repository URLs. No external service is required. The book manifest controls the chapter order and the visual manifest controls managed figure blocks; design provenance and font licences accompany the assets.

See validation results for the actual build, content review and artifact checks. Pilot feedback should be recorded against an activity ID, the observed issue, the proposed revision and who reviewed it; do not store identifiable learner work in the public repository.
