ACCESS IS4S book design

The palette, badge and typography follow the ACCESS Numeracy Hub: warm cream #F7F4F1, ink #1A1512, ACCESS red #D2181F and gold #FFBC00. Archivo headings, IBM Plex Sans text and IBM Plex Mono figures are bundled locally, with Fontsource provenance and licences in fonts/. The badge is preserved unchanged.

The textbook layout is original. No Nation-specific artwork, cultural pattern or source-guide artwork is added. The design uses hierarchy, worked-example panels, tables, numbered tasks and space for reading and annotation. Source material is attributed in the books and research ledger.
