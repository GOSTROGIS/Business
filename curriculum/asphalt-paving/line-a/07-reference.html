Record evidence without overstating it

For each selected activity, record: activity ID; date; task and document version; what you did; support used; result; check; feedback; revision; and next practice. Mark the observed skill. A group product needs an individual contribution record. An accurate calculation needs units and assumptions. A digital claim needs actual tool use. A future application is a plan until it has been observed.

Sources and continuing questions

The research register provides public titles, issuers, dates, URLs, retained short excerpts and retrieval hashes. It distinguishes dated factual evidence from invented classroom inputs and records questions that need current local or technical confirmation. Use the register to check a claim, then keep the question and the evidence together. A link or a confident statement alone is not the complete answer.
