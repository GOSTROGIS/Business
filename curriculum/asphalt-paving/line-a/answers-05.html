Unit 5 · Answer and teaching guidance

Use 120 minutes: project/document register 15, three-view vocabulary 25, modelled quantity 20, independent check 20, revision 25 and handover 15. Provide the complete P-01/Q-01/SP-01 text, calculator, blank paper and the cross-section visual. No physical measurements of the printed image are valid inputs. Offer unit cards and a partly completed quantity table when needed. Record support.

Task Expected evidence
LA5-01 Private facility access; Cedar Learning Society owner; Valley Surface Ltd. contractor; community fund contributor. Use/category does not by itself adopt a specification. Actual signed contract and authorized current documents determine applicability.
LA5-02 Plan: 50 m horizontal length/6 m width. Profile: elevation change along centreline. Section: 40 mm calculation-layer thickness. 100.50 m is an elevation relative to the illustrative datum, not a layer depth.
LA5-03 Crown raised transverse form; shoulder road edge area; open ditch drainage channel; curb/gutter contained edge/drainage arrangement; sidewalk pedestrian route; runoff surface-water movement; superelevation cross-slope arrangement on curves; grader equipment. Figure LA-6: granular base supports the asphalt surface; subbase is an additional supporting layer where specified; prepared subgrade is the prepared ground beneath the pavement structure. Other structures exist; the drawing supplies no P-01 design dimensions. Questions may concern outlet, base preparation, approved slope, services or required materials. Do not supply invented design answers.
LA5-04 Thickness 0.040 m; area 300 m²; volume 12 m³; model mass 24 t. Records 8 + 8 + 7.6 = 23.6 t, 0.4 t below the model quantity. Profile rise 0.50 m/50 m=1%. Neither record totals nor an invented factor establish actual placed quantity, thickness, density, acceptance or payment.
LA5-05 Revision C area 360 m²; volume 14.4 m³; model mass 28.8 t. Increases 60 m², 2.4 m³, 4.8 t. Existing record total 23.6 t is 5.2 t below the revised model quantity. Profile rise 0.60 m/60 m=1%. Keep the old record and identify which revision each calculation uses.
LA5-06 Handover includes current revision C, 14.4 m³ geometric model, 28.8 t at fictional 2.00 t/m³, 23.6 t recorded, 5.2 t arithmetic difference, and the limits on a real order or acceptance decision. Each person demonstrates both checks over the two cases.

Model quantity note: “P-01 revision C shows a 60 m by 6 m rectangle with a 0.040 m calculation layer. Area is 360 m² and geometric volume 14.4 m³. At the supplied fictional 2.00 t/m³ factor the model mass is 28.8 t. The unchanged classroom records total 23.6 t, a 5.2 t difference. I checked units and recalculated from the new length. These model values do not establish an actual order, approved density or acceptance result.”
