Unit 4 · Answer and teaching guidance

Suggested 90 minutes: role mapping 15, safety case 20, licences 15, handover 10, schedule 15 and development/enquiry 15. Allow spoken planning before authored writing. Keep every role-play in the classroom; provide no simulated machine signals. Evaluate observable actions and source distinctions rather than confidence, accent or posture.

Task Expected evidence
LA4-01 Broad functions and meaningful dependencies: haul supplies mixture; paver places; screed helps control the placed layer; raker supports handwork/finishing; roller compacts; milling removes/profiles existing material. A handover identifies status or information, not an invented signal. W1 authorizes no site access or operation.
LA4-02 Equipment-name knowledge is insufficient workplace-specific orientation. Learner asks for supervisor/contact, relevant hazard and emergency information, task instruction/demonstration and the appropriate communication arrangements. The employer's obligation is not transferred to the learner.
LA4-03 Admission: provider's current intake requirements. Licence: ICBC/applicable legal requirements for the actual vehicle/activity. Competence: appropriate training and assessment records. Assignment: responsible employer/site authorization. Class 5/7 in a provider prerequisite cannot authorize every truck or machine.
LA4-04 Example: “The role map is ready for review, but licensing is still pending. We can print the classroom explanation with that question marked open and ask the responsible person before adding an authorization claim.” Observe the receiver's check-back and each contribution.
LA4-05 18:00–24:00 is 6 h; 00:00–02:00 is 2 h; elapsed 8 h. Subtract 0.5 h = 7.5 paid hours in the fictional record. Add Wednesday 6 h = 13.5 h. Questions may concern overtime, cancellation/travel pay, breaks, fatigue controls or actual schedule arrangements. No legal entitlement follows from these invented inputs.
LA4-06 Collective bargaining is negotiation between an employer and workers represented by a union about employment terms; the applicable parties, agreement and dates must be checked. Forecast demand and attendance do not guarantee hiring, continued work or qualification. Questions identify the actual agreement/terms and role-specific pathway; the goal includes a task, evidence, support, check date and responsible reviewer. A saved/reopened file is separate from a paper enquiry. Do not require sending, personal employment disclosure or joining any organization.

Before workplace delivery, a qualified reviewer must confirm role descriptions, current legal/licensing application, provider pathway, workplace safety program and employment/collective-agreement context. This unit supplies the classroom cases and answer criteria; that review is an edition-status requirement, not a request for learners to devise actual operating procedures.
