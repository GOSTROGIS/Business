The new classroom practice time has a different purpose and must not be substituted into a regulator or provider schedule without agreement. The 140-hour provider statement is now verified as a published statement; its relationship to the historical 120-hour profile remains a pathway question.

The IUOE page includes course-completion and credential/endorsement language alongside other training references. Retain the wording in the evidence record and obtain written clarification from the responsible provider/regulator before describing the current qualification pathway. A Class 5/7 provider admission condition is separate from the driver's licence or authorization required for a particular workplace vehicle/activity. Do not use a failed guessed webpage request as evidence that a regulator standard has changed.
