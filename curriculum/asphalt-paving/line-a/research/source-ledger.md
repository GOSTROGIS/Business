## Retrieval and scope limits

Failed or unsuitable retrievals are recorded in retrieval-attempts.json. The attempted FHWA life-cycle framework links returned 404; a guessed ROSAP identifier returned an unrelated Mayday report, which was inspected and excluded. Neither source is represented as read evidence for life-cycle results. Unit 1 therefore supplies a sourced recovery-pathway comparison and a complete fictional evidence-appraisal case, not a fabricated asphalt/concrete carbon ranking.

The Statistics Canada table notes count statistical locations, not unique enterprises; prohibit calculating total employment from the size bands; and advise against treating the counts as a time series. The published table title and machine reference-period field are both retained. NAICS 237310 is highway, street and bridge construction, wider than asphalt alone.

The supplied 2006 *Design Build Standard Specifications for Highway Construction*, [clause 502.03.01, PDF 297 / printed 1 of 22](../../sources/design_build_standard_specs_2006.pdf#page=297), explicitly defines **End Product Specification (EPS)**. The definition assigns construction-process quality control to the contractor and specified end-product audit sampling/testing for acceptance/rejection and payment adjustment to the Ministry. This is confirmed document evidence, not an inferred expansion. The historical outline A1 label’s intended relationship to this specification and the current provider context still require confirmation. Expanded-polystyrene geofoam is a different meaning in a different subject. This supplied-document reference is separate from the 20 retrieved public-web evidence records; it does not increase that count.
