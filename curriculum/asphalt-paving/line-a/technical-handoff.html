Edition: Asphalt paving · Roadbuilding foundations, 29 October 2026. Current status: classroom units authored; independent qualified technical review, ACCESS delivery agreement, local co-design and classroom pilot not yet recorded. No external messages have been sent as part of this preparation.

What the reviewer receives

The six units contain learner readings, complete fictional inputs, worked models, individual and group tasks, separate keys and local evidence criteria. The research register preserves current public-source facts, dated technical references and unresolved questions. The 68-row competency map distinguishes authored Line A work from later occupational scope. Classroom outputs can support a learning record; they do not substitute for workplace practical assessment or equipment endorsement.

The supplied outline assigns 3% of historical technical time to all of Line A. Applying that share to 120 hours gives 3.6 hours, not 3.6 hours for A1 alone. The new classroom practice time has a different purpose and must not be substituted into a regulator or provider schedule without agreement. The 140-hour provider statement is now verified as a published statement; its relationship to the historical 120-hour profile remains a pathway question.

The IUOE page includes course-completion and credential/endorsement language alongside other training references. Retain the wording in the evidence record and obtain written clarification from the responsible provider/regulator before describing the current qualification pathway. A Class 5/7 provider admission condition is separate from the driver's licence or authorization required for a particular workplace vehicle/activity. Do not use a failed guessed webpage request as evidence that a regulator standard has changed.

Pilot evidence

Before a wider release, record the cohort/context, actual durations, access arrangements, confusing terms, activity evidence, calculation errors, learner feedback and revisions. Ask for consent before retaining identifiable work. Use ordinary learning evidence rather than pressure to disclose personal experience. Record which activities were actually observed and which need another opportunity. The facilitator should have the answer guide and a route to qualified technical clarification.
