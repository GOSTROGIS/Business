1.4.2 Surface Course

Aspbalt pavement is a general term applied to any pavement that has a
surface constructed with asphalt (see Figure 1.6). Normally, it consists of
a surface course (layer) of mineral aggregate coated and cemented with
asphalt, and one or more supporting courses, which may be the following:

¢ Asphalt base, consisting of asphalt-aggregate mixtures

¢ Crushed stone (rock), slag, or gravel

¢ Portland cement concrete

unbound (gravel or crushed stone) layers above the prepared subgrade.
