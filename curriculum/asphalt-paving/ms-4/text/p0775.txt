15.4.2 Basic Considerations

A properly designed and constructed asphalt pavement can provide a
long-lasting surface. The surface must be designed to meet the needs of the
users and to accommodate local site conditions and materials. The existing
terrain, drainage, and pavement uses of the facility must be addressed in
the design phase. The basic construction must provide the following:

e A stable, well-compacted subgrade

° A free-draining, compacted, crushed-stone base

° A compacted HMA layer 3-4 inches (75-100 millimeters) thick that is

placed in two lifts

* For running tracks and tennis courts, a special surface such as a liquid-

applied acrylic for tennis courts or a rubberized surface for running
tracks. For a walking path, no special surface will usually be required.
