AASPHALT IN PAVEMENT
IVI AINTENANCE

This chapter provides an overview of the methods used to maintain
hot mix asphalt pavements. Pavement maintenance is the work
performed to keep a pavement as close as possible to its as-built
condition, under normal traffic and weather conditions.
