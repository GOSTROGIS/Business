Decision Matrices

Another format used for defining treatment rules is a decision matrix.
Decision matrices are similar to decision trees in the way they distinguish
between treatment options, except that the decisions are presented in
a tabular format rather than in a tree structure. The information from
Figure 11.27 is illustrated in a matrix format Table 11.1.

Other Tools

Depending on the type of pavement management system used, other
tools may be used to determine when various treatments are feasible. Some
pavement management systems require the rules to be defined in terms of
“if-then” statements that can be easily interpreted by the computer during
the analysis. Regardless of the format used, the treatment rules should
reflect current agency policies and practices as much as possible so the
treatment recommendations make sense.
