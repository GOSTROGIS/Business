Equipment Common to
Both Types of Facilities

Regardless of the proportioning and mixing style, both types of facilities
have common equipment components that operate similarly and have
essentially the same functions:

° Liquid binder storage tanks

° Aggregate and RAP stockpiling

¢ Virgin aggregate and RAP feed bins

e Aggregate and RAP transfer conveyors
Aggregate dryers
* Dust collection equipment (from aggregate dryer)

Collected dust return equipment
Mineral filler additive storage and positive feed controls

¢ If required by specifications—lime additive bulk storage and positive

feed controls

¢ Equipment for the addition of fibers

¢ Finished product storage silos and load-out equipment

Plant automation is similar in both—and typically computer controlled—
although blending automation is different because of the differences
between the batch and the drum-mix processes. Most automated plant-
control systems, whether batch oriented or drum-mixer, provide for
multiple stored-mix formulas and automatically handle all the blending,
metering, and mixing. All computer-controlled plants can provide cumu-
lative material totals for a daily production report, which aids inventory
control.

In both plant types, truck load-out and ticketing systems are also com-
puterized and provide not only weight figures but also detailed project
information on the load ticket.

These common equipment groups are discussed below, along with key
optimum operating practices for controlling the process and producing
consistent high-quality mix with these common components. Specific
operational differences between the batch process and the drum-mix
process are addressed later in the section.

7.2.1 Liquid Asphalt Storage

Liquid asphalt binder is generally stored inside insulated and heated
tanks (see Figure 7.5) adjacent to the other plant equipment. Multiple
storage tanks are common at stationary facilities and are not uncommon
with portable plants. This allows the producer to keep different grades of
asphalt binder properly separated.
