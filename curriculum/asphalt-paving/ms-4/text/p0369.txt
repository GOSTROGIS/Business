Different screen sizes can be used to fractionalize the material into dif-
ferent sizes, but most modern batch screens are set one time, representing
the common mixes being made and are not often, if ever, changed. Typical
screen sizes for plants at the time of this writing are No. 4 to No. 6 over the
No. 1 bin (to create a sand-by-dust-type material), “7+ inch over the No. 2 bin
(to create a %-inch-by-No. 4 or No. 6-type material), % inch over the No. 3
bin (to create a nominal % inch or %6-inch-by-/s-inch-type material), and
1-1% inches over the No. 4 bin (to create a nominal +34inch material—
that being retained on the %-inch deck, but passing the 1-1% inch screen).
Materials larger than 1~1% inches are rejected out of the tower into the
oversize chute.

Charts are available from batch-plant manufacturers that provide guid-
ance on maximum screen capacity (before the material overflows) based
on a given size opening. Slotted or z-wire screens can be used to improve
throughput capability over standard square-screen types.
