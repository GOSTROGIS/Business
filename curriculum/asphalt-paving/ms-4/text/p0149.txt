Usually, the crushed product is screened to produce the desired aggregate
sizes. Sometimes, for cost efficiency, the crushed product is used as it comes
from the crusher with little or no screening. This is referred to as “crusher-
run” aggregate and, in some instances, can be used satisfactorily in asphalt
pavement construction, especially if the graduation of particle sizes is rea-
sonably distributed. Extra care must be exercised, however, to maintain a
well-blended product and avoid segregation.

The process of crushing aggregate, whether stone or gravel, generates
crushed particles of all sizes. Particles smaller than 0.02 inch (6 milli-
meters) in diameter are usually separated from the larger crushed mate-
rial. This fine-aggregate material is known as crushed sand, manufactured
sand, crusher fines, and crushed fines. Many times, this material is used
in the fine-aggregate component of asphalt paving mixtures. The crushed-
sand aggregate from limestone may be further processed to a maximum
size of 0.02 inch (0.6 millimeter) in diameter for use as mineral filler in
asphalt paving mixtures.
