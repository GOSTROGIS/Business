One method of determining the binder content is to mix up small batches
with differing percentages of binder. Place the samples in Pyrex pie plates. Place
the plates in an oven heated to mixing temperature. After 24 hours, observe the
amount of drain off. The percentage of binder that shows only a slight drain off
would be the proper binder content for that gradation of aggregate.
