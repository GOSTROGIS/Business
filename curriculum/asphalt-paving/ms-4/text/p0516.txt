Some agencies establish “adjustment” criteria in their specifications. For
those projects, if results of quality-control testing fall outside the criteria, the
producer is required to make an adjustment to bring the material closer to the
middle of the band. These specifications generally also have a wider criterion
that would require the producer to stop production and adjust the mixture
before restarting production.
