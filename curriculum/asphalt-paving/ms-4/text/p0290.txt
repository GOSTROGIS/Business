The CBR is determined from the corrected load values at 0.1- and 0.2-inch
(2.5 and 5 millimeters) penetrations by dividing the loads at 0.1 and 0.2 inch
(2.5 and 5 millimeters) by the standard loads of 1000 and 1500 pounds per
square inch (6.89 and 10.34 megapascals), respectively. Each ratio is multi-
plied by 100 to obtain the CBR in percentage. The CBR is usually selected at
0.1 inch (2.5 millimeters) of penetration. If the CBR at 0.2 inch (5 millimeters)
of penetration is greater, the test should be rerun. If check tests give similar
results, the CBR at 0.2 inch (5 millimeters) of penetration should be used.
