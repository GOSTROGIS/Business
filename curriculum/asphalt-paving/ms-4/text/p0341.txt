This chapter provides information on the manufacturing of hot

mix asphalt using batch and drum-mix plant facilities. The common
features and differences of the two mixing plant types are discussed.
Controlling the aggregate gradation and asphalt content is detailed.
Mixing and coating with both type plants is covered. Incorporating
RAP into hot mix with both plant types is discussed. Finally, a
trouble-shooting guide is provided.
