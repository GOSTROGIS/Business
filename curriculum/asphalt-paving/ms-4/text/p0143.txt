CHAPTER

MINERAL AGGREGATES AND
Mixture ADDITIVES

This chapter provides information on aggregate as a component
of asphalt mixtures and treatments. The sources of aggregates

and production and processing practices are described. Aggregate
properties that influence asphalt performance are covered. The
basics of using reclaimed asphalt pavement (RAP) are summarized.
Finally, information pertaining to aggregate additives is provided.
