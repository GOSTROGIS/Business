A shiny black surface indicates too much asphalt. Adjustments should be
made by experienced personnel in 0.2 percent increments of emulsion
content.

The inspector should be aware of any large maintenance patches in
the existing roadway. It may be necessary to remove those sections
prior to CIPR and replace them with either HMA or RAP. Maintenance
patches are usually manufactured and placed by maintenance forces
using cutback asphalts. To improve workability, excess asphalt is some-
times used in these patches. If such patches are left in the roadway and
not removed, it may be necessary to adjust the amount of asphalt being
added to the process as the CIPR machine moves through the patch.
Quality-control testing should consist of taking random samples of
the CIPR in accordance with the agency’s guidelines—or at least two
samples per mile (one per kilometer). These samples should be tested
for aggregate gradation and asphalt content.

The ride quality should be checked daily by driving through the project.
Ride specifications are not generally incorporated into CIPR projects.
The steps discussed in Chapter 8. “Hot Mix Asphalt Paving”— for insur-
ing a smooth pavement surface—also apply to CIPR projects.
