9.2.1 Terms and Definitions

The followings terms and their definitions are used in this chapter:

Width of mat

416

Compaction is the process of compressing HMA into a smaller,
denser volume.

Density is a physical measurement typically expressed as a percent-
age of maximum theoretical density or as a percentage of laboratory
density. Density is what is achieved through the compaction process.
Lift. An HMA pavement consists of a series of lifts or layers of HMA that
will constitute a surface, intermediate, or base course. Each course may
have multiple lifts or layers. For example, a 6-inch HMA base course may
have two lifts 3 inches thick or three lifts 2 inches thick.

Time available for compaction is the time it takes for the mat to cool
below the point where density is usually obtained.

Pass is defined as the roller passing over one point in the mat one
time.

Coverage is defined as the roller making enough passes to cover
the complete width of the mat being placed. Repeated coverages are
applied until the target density is achieved. This is demonstrated in
Figure 9.1, which illustrates the pass and coverage.
