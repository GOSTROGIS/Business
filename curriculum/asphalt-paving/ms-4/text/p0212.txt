Effective Specific Gravity of Aggregate

Effective specific gravity (G,,) is the ratio of the mass in air of a unit
volume of a permeable material (excluding voids permeable to asphalt)
at a stated temperature (see Figure 4.7) to the mass in air of equal den-
sity of an equal volume of gas-free distilled water at a stated tempera-
ture. In other words, the effective specific gravity includes the volume of
the water-permeable voids in the aggregate that cannot be reached by
the asphalt.
