Mixtures prepared with emulsified asphalt do not require aggregates to
be as dry as those used in mixtures prepared with cutback asphalt. Such
emulsified-asphalt mixtures reduce the amount of manipulation or extent
of air drying required before mixing the aggregate with asphalt.

Cold-laid asphalt mixtures prepared for maintenance use and stockpiled
over a period of time are usually prepared with a lower-viscosity grade of
MC or SC cutback asphalt.

15.1.6 Types of Mixes

Open-Graded Mixes

cold mixes. Most of the research and development work on these mixes has
centered in the northwestern states, where they have been successfully used
as bases and surface courses on many miles of federal, state, and county
