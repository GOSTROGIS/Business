Select Initial Asphalt Binder Content

When the aggregate blends have been chosen, the next step is to evaluate
the trial blends by compacting the specimens and determining the volumetric
properties for each trial blend. For each blend, a minimum of two specimens is
compacted using the Superpave Gyratory Compactor (SGC). The trial asphalt
binder content can be estimated based on the designer’s experience with simi-
lar materials. If the designer does not have experience with the particular aggre-
gate source, the trial binder content can be determined for each trial blend by
estimating the specific gravity of the blend and using a calculation procedure
detailed in AASHTO R 35. SP-2 lists typical trial binder contents for aggregate
blends having a combined bulk specific gravity of approximately 2.65.

Superpave Gyratory Compactor

The Superpave mix design procedure uses the Superpave Gyratory
Compactor (see Figure 4.12) to compact the specimens. The basis for the
SGC was the Texas gyratory compactor, which was modified to use the
compaction principles of a French gyratory compactor. The Texas device
accomplished the goal of achieving realistic specimen densification, and
it was reasonably portable. Its 6-inch sample diameter (ultimately 150 mil-
limeters on an SGC) could accommodate mixtures containing aggregate
up to 50 millimeters maximum (37.5 nominal) size. The developers of the
Superpave system modified the Texas device by lowering its angle and
speed of gyration and adding real-time specimen-height recordation.
