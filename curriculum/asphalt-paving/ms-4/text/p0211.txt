Maximum Specific Gravities of Mixtures with Different Asphalt

Contents

In designing a paving mixture with a given aggregate, the maximum specific
gravities (G,,_) at different asphalt contents are needed to calculate the percent-
age of air-voids for each asphalt content. While individual maximum specific
gravities can be determined for each asphalt content, the precision of the test is
best when the specimen is at, or over the optimum asphalt content. The Asphalt
Institute recommends measuring the maximum specific gravity in duplicate or
triplicate on individually batched specimens at one trial asphalt content. The
end result of the maximum specific gravity test is to determine an accurate
G,, (Effective Specific Gravity of Aggregate) as described in the following sec-
tion. Many agencies have different procedures to determine G,. The designer is
encouraged to be familiar with methods in their respective localities.
