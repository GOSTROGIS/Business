11.4 Pavement Preservation

Program

As discussed earlier in this chapter, a pavement preservation program
can be a cost-effective way to manage a pavement network when a blend
of preventive maintenance, rehabilitation, and reconstruction treatments is
used appropriately. This section of the chapter describes the components
of an effective pavement preservation program in more detail and the
manner in which a pavement preservation program is supported using a
pavement management system.

11.4.1 Establishing a Pavement
Preservation Program

The decision to establish a pavement preservation program can be difficult
for an agency because it often requires a change in the way an agency does
business. Traditionally, agencies respond to pressure from outside forces
to continually allocate funds to the roads in the worst condition. However,
when a strong pavement preservation program is in place, in addition to
funding roads in poor condition, the agency dedicates some of its funds to
functional improvements on roads that are not substantially deteriorated in
order to delay the need for more costly treatments. It requires support from
the top of the organization to commit to this type of program and to remain
committed in the face of outside pressures to do otherwise.

Use of Pavement Management to Support Pavement
Preservation

The use of pavement management systems to demonstrate the benefits
associated with a pavement preservation program was discussed earlier
in this chapter. To fully support a comprehensive pavement preservation
program, a pavement management system must include the following
considerations in its analysis:

° A method of assessing pavement conditions that includes the types of
deterioration that normally trigger the selection of preventive mainte-
nance and rehabilitation treatments

° Pavement performance models that can differentiate the performance
of a pavement section with and without different types of treatments

* Treatment rules that can be used to determine when different types of
treatments are considered feasible

* Models that quantify the effects on pavement condition after differ-
ent types of treatments have been applied. These models are used to
forecast future conditions under various scenarios.
