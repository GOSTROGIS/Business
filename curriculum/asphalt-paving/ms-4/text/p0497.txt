10.2.5 Asphalt Binder Sampling

Asphalt binder can be sampled from delivery truck tanks, from the plant’s
asphalt storage tank, or from delivery lines from the tank to the asphalt
mixing plant. Samples from the delivery trucks are more representative of
the binder delivered by the supplier, but samples from the storage tank or
the delivery lines to the hot plant are more representative of the binder that
is incorporated into the mix. The procedure involves opening a valve that is
usually located near the bottom of the tank. Sample containers must be new,
clean, and dry. At least one quart of asphalt should be allowed to flow out
of the valve and discarded before filling the sample container. It should be
sealed immediately after sampling, wiped with a clean dry cloth, and properly
marked to identify the material for shipping to the laboratory.

Appropriate personal protective equipment should be worn around the
hot asphalt. Generally, representatives of the asphalt supplier or HMA pro-
ducer will obtain the samples because of safety and insurance concerns.
The quality-control or acceptance technician then takes immediate posses-
sion of the sample.

Procedures identified in AASHTO T 40 or ASTM D 140 should be followed
when sampling asphalt binder.
