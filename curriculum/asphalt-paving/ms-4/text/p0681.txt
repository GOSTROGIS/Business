compaction problems. After placement of the mix, compaction is accomplished
with a conventional roller train using the compaction procedures discussed in
Chapter 9, “Hot Mix Asphalt Compaction.”

Remixing

Remixing consists of heating the roadway to a depth of 1%2-2 inches
(7.5-50 millimeters); scarification and collection of the softened material
into a windrow; mixing the material with virgin aggregates, a recycling
agent, and/or new HMA, if required, in a pugmill; and laying the recycled
mix in a single homogeneous HMA layer. This method is used when the
repaving procedure is not sufficient to make the necessary corrections to
an existing roadway. It can be used to make changes in aggregate gradation,
improve skid resistance, and adjust the mix to improve rutting resistance.
