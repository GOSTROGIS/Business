PETROLEUM ASPHALT

This chapter discusses liquid asphalt binder (also called bitumen)
as a material. It is derived from petroleum processing. Properties,
characteristics, and commonly used tests and specifications are dis-
cussed. In the interest of brevity, all the test details and procedures
are not given here but can be found in other sources.
