Multi-unit Trains

A multi-unit train consists of a milling machine, a portable screening
and crushing unit, and a portable pugmill mixer (see Figure 13.22).
The milling machine controls the depth of the pavement being milled.
The RAP is picked up by the milling machine and deposited into the
screening and crushing unit. The material is passed over a screen deck,
and any oversized material is returned to a crushing unit for resizing. After
the oversized material has been crushed, it is sent back over the screening
unit. The maximum size of the RAP is controlled by the opening size of the
bottom two screens. Typically, the maximum particle size is 1-1% inches
(25-37.5 millimeters).

The RAP moves from the processing unit to the pugmill where the
asphalt emulsion is added and mixed. The weight of the RAP is moni-
tored as it enters the pugmill. The amount of emulsion is controlled by
a computerized metering system. The RAP and emulsion are mixed in a
twin-shaft pugmill. The addition of a crushing and screening unit provides
better gradation and mixing control than the single and double-unit pro-
cesses, and it results in a more uniform asphalt-treated base course.
