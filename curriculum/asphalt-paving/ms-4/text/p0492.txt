Hot Bin Sampling

For batch plants, aggregate samples for quality-control are often taken
from the hot bins (Figure 10.4). Proper sampling devices that cut through
the entire discharge stream are needed to achieve representative samples.

10.2.4 HMA Mixture Sampling

Sampling methods are available for sampling HMA at many points along
the construction process, including at the plant, from delivery trucks, from
windrows at the job site, and from the paving grade behind the paving
machine. For all these sampling methods, the procedures described in
AASHTO T 168 or ASTM D 979 should be followed. Samples of the com-
pacted HMA materials can also be taken by cutting cores from the cooled
pavement following methods described in ASTM D 5361 or AASHTO T 168.
