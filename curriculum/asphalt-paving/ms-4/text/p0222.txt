The requirement for maximum loss value for the soundness test will
typically range from 10 percent to 20 percent for five cycles. A wide range of
criteria for maximum allowable percentage of deleterious particles exists.
Values range from as low as 0.2 percent to as high as 10 percent.

Gradation

Superpave utilizes the 0.45 power gradation chart to analyze the agere-
gate gradation and to define the maximum density line. The maximum den-
sity line represents a gradation where the aggregate particles fit together
in their densest possible arrangement. The development and use of this
chart is discussed in Chapter 3. Superpave uses the maximum and nominal
maximum size of the aggregates to categorize mixtures and define grada-
tion requirements. These are defined as follows:

* Maximum size: One sieve size larger than the nominal maximum size

* Nominal maximum size: One sieve size larger than the first sieve to

retain more than 10 percent

4.3.3 Design Aggregate Structure

The term used in Superpave to describe the distribution of aggregate
particle sizes is design aggregate structure. A design aggregate structure
that lies between the control points meets the Superpave gradation require-
ments. Superpave defines six mixture gradations based on their nominal
maximum aggregate size (see Table 4.3). The SP-2 manual provides the
numerical gradation limits for the five Superpave mixtures.
