With static steel-wheel rollers, the operation should progress with the
drive wheei forward in the direction of paving. If the breakdown pass of
the roller is made with the tiller wheel forward, the pushing force and the
weight is slightly ahead of vertical, causing material to push up in a wave
in front of the wheel. The greater weight of the drive wheel carries out the
compaction, while the turning force tends to tuck material under the front
of the wheel as shown in Figure 9.11.
