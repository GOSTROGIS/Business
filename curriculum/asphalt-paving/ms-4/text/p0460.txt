9.3 Compaction Equipment

Compaction is performed using any of several types of rollers that apply
force (either static or dynamic) to the mat, reorienting aggregate particles
to reduce the volume occupied by the mixture.

Self-propelled rollers are by far the most common type of compaction
equipment used for HMA construction. Towed-type rollers should not be
used for HMA compaction, but hand-operated walk-behind rollers, vibrat-
ing plates, or even hand tamps are often used in small areas inaccessible
to larger rollers. Typical self-propelled compaction rollers include these
three types:

* Static steel-wheel

¢ Pneumatic-tire

° Vibratory
