If mixing flights are not cleaned periodically, very hard pieces of asphalt
and fines, which build up on the tips and flights, can break loose and find
their way into the mix. Mixing effectiveness can also be affected. If mix-
ing flights are allowed to build up with material between the flights to the
point where flights are barely visible, segregation can occur in the mixing
process. Also larger aggregate particles can make it through the mixing
area without being adequately tumbled and coated.
