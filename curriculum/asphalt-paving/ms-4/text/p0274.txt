The samples are tested at or near the maximum pavement temperatures
(generally defined as the high temperature grade for the asphalt binder to
be used in region where the HMA pavement is being built).

The wheel load is 100 pounds (445 Newtons) and the hose pressure is 100
pounds per square inch (690 kiloPascals). Figure 4.39 shows the typical APA
rut-test results. It indicates that specimens deform rapidly at the beginning
of the test. The amount of permanent deformation per cycle decreases and
becomes quite steady after a certain number of load cycles.
