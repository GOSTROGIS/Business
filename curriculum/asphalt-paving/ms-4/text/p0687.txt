Asphalts Used for Cold-Mix Recycling

Various asphalt binders have been used for cold central plant recycling
and for cold in-place recycling. Typically cationic, anionic or high-float
medium setting (HFMS) emulsions, or polymer-modified versions of these,
have been used. A recent development uses a foamed asphalt process in
a central mix plant. To increase the initial strength and resistance to water
damage, small amounts (1-1% percent) of either lime or cement can be
added to a mix using an asphalt emulsion. They can be added dry or as
slurry Gime, cement, and water).

13.5.3 Stationary Facilities

Central plant cold recycling can be accomplished in a standard batch
plant or other plant that may be located at a central mixing location, or it
can be processed at the job site using a mobile mixing plant. The produc-
tion of high-quality cold recycled mixes requires attention to detail. The
nature of the plant varies according to the size of the project (quantity of
mix being produced). The plant should consist of (1) a twin-shaft pugmill
equipped with emulsion and water spray bars, (2) trailers for water and emul-
