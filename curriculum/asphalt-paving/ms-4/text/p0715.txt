These applications can work successfully when the existing pavement is
in good structural condition (good load transfer, intact slabs). While it is not
unusual to see a single lift of HMA used, two or more lifts are recommended
where feasible to provide sufficient cover over the interlayer and to allow
for the smoothest finished surface.

While an interlayer or saw-and-seal can be used effectively to reduce,
delay, or control reflection cracking resulting from thermal movements,
these treatments are not recommended where the load transfer across
joints or cracks is poor. Fractured slab techniques that either reduce or
eliminate slab action, such as cracking and seating and rubblization, are
commonly used to address reflection cracking in structural HMA overlays
of Portland cement concrete pavement (PCCP).
