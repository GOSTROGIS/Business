SpeEcIAL Uses oF
ASPHALT AND HMA

This chapter describes the many special uses of HMA and asphalt.
The details of preparing and using cold mix asphalt mixes are
covered. Using asphalt in hydraulic applications and for railway
track beds is described. Information is provided on how asphalt
is used in athletic and recreational facilities. Asphalt roofing is
summarized and mitigating pavement noise with selected asphalt
mixes is discussed. The special requirements for asphalt for race
and test tracks are covered. New applications of asphalt in porous
pavements and in warm mix technology are covered.
