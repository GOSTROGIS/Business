8.1. Pre-paving Conference

The key to a successful paving project is good communication between
the paving crew, plant personnel, and the agency or owner. This communi-
cation generally begins with the pre-paving conference. At this conference,
the contractor’s personnel should explain their project plans and establish
a meaningful schedule to meet the agency’s or owner’s expectations.

The details of the project should be covered so that all the affected par-
ties understand the “who, what, why, when, and how.” The pre-paving con-
ference can be as simple as the contractor’s representative meeting with
the owner on the project site to explain his plan to pave the project—or as
complicated as an organized meeting with all interested parties (including
the owner, the designer, the inspectors, personnel from the testing firms,
the contractor’s plant and paving personnel, utility representatives, etc.).
No matter what form this pre-paving meeting takes, it is very critical and
will save time, money, and confusion provided it is held well before HMA
placement begins.

The discussion of the project details allows everyone involved with the
project to identify concerns they may have with the proposed schedule
and the possible impacts on pavement users (in terms of lane closures),
traffic patterns, on businesses adjacent to the project, and local or regional
events such as major sporting events.

The most important item to be discussed at the pre-paving conference
is safety—of the construction personnel as well as of the traveling public.
Clear responsibility for the maintenance of traffic-control devices, such as
pavement marking and flagging, should be delineated. Attention to proj-
ect safety is a moral, legal, and financial responsibility of the contracting
owner or agency, the contractor, all subcontractors, and suppliers.

Testing and sampling methods and frequencies should be reviewed at
the pre-paving conference to ensure that everyone involved in the project
understands the following:

¢ The purpose of the testing

° The frequency of the testing

° Who will conduct the tests

¢ How and to whom the test results will be reported
