The SGC is a mechanical device comprising the following components:

° Reaction frame, rotating base, and motor

° Loading system, loading ram, and pressure gauge

° Height measuring and recordation system

* Mold and base plate

e A specimen extruding device

A loading mechanism presses against the reaction frame and applies a
load to the loading ram to produce a 600-kiloPascals compaction pressure
on the specimen. A pressure gauge measures the ram loading to maintain
constant pressure during compaction. The SGC mold (see Figure 4.13) has
an inside diameter of 150 millimeters, and a base plate in the bottom of
the mold provides confinement during compaction. The SGC gyrates at a
constant rate of 30 gyrations per minute with the mold positioned at an
external compaction angle of 1.25 degrees. The internal angle is calibrated
using procedures discussed below.

Specimen height measurement is an important function of the SGC.
Using the mass of material placed in the mold, the diameter of the mold,
and the specimen height, an estimate of specimen density can be made at
any time throughout the compaction process. Specimen density is com-
puted by dividing the mass by the volume of the specimen. The height is
measured by recording the position of the ram throughout the test. Using
these measurements, a specimen’s compaction characteristic is developed.
