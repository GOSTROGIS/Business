Warranty periods range from 2 to 20 years, but 5 years is typical for
pavement warranties in the United States. Mitigation measures for devia-
tions from acceptance criteria can be agreed to before beginning construc-
tion if they are not addressed in the specifications.

10.10.2 Monitoring Performance
Parameters

Specific methods used to monitor performance are included in the spec-
ifications, along with requirements for when and how often measurements
are to be taken. The owner generally makes these measurements, and it
is critical that clear acceptance criteria and detailed descriptions of conse-
quences of noncompliance are understood by all parties.

10.10.3 Dispute Resolution

Many warranty specifications have alternative dispute resolution terms
for resolving disagreements. A method commonly used involves selec-
tion of a panel to evaluate and render an opinion on the specific item of
the disagreement. The parties with the disagreement first must meet and
clearly establish what the points of disagreement are. This keeps the panel
on-track with the correct issues and reduces the amount of time needed to
come to resolution. Each party selects one person who is independent and
unaware of the dispute but has special knowledge or expertise relative to
the issues disputed. A geotechnical engineer may be selected, for example,
if the dispute is related to soil conditions. After each party selects one
person, the two selected panel members meet and select a third. All three
panel members agree to be unbiased and their costs are shared evenly.
Information describing the disagreement and the position of each party
is presented to the panel, which has an established time for evaluating
the information. The panel then renders a decision, including any needed
corrective action and an allocation of cost responsibility.
