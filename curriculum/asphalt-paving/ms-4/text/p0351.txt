7.2.0 Virgin Aggregate and RAP
Cold-Feed Bins

Feed bins for virgin aggregate (see Figure 7.6) and RAP (see Figure 7.7)
are similar for both batch and drum-mix plants.

While vibratory pan feeders and plate-style feeders were used in the
past, virtually all plants, both batch and drum/continuous, now use con-
veyors with variable-speed motors located below feed bins to regulate the
flow of materials to the drying drum.

Variable-speed belt feeds are consistent and repeatable, and therefore
easy to calibrate. The ton-per-hour output can be determined at different
belt speeds, and then the required speed setting can be calculated for a
given recipe and given production rate. The feed-bin calibration process
is the act of measuring and recording output at different speed ranges for
a specific gate opening. This process is usually computerized on today’s
modern plants. Gate openings should be proportional to the amount of
ageregate being added to the plant to ensure uniform loading of the belt.
