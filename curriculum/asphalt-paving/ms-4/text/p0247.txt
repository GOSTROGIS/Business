4.6.2 Mix Design

Determine Combined Aggregate in Recycle Mix

The combined gradation for the HMA mix using RAP must meet the
specifying agency’s requirements. To accomplish this, the contractor will
blend the aggregates as discussed in Chapter 3. The gradation to be used
for this analysis should be the extracted aggregate gradation.

Asphalt Demand of Combined Aggregate

The asphalt demand for the HMA mix using RAP will be determined
using one of the standard design procedures: Superpave, Marshall, or
Hveem. The final asphalt content will consist of the asphalt in the RAP
and the virgin asphalt to be added during the production process. A major
advantage of the use of RAP is that it reduces the need for new, virgin
asphalt binder.
