Remove Exposed Joint-Sealing Material

Joint sealant materials such as rubberized asphalt and low modulus silicone
are usually loosened during rubblization but often left in place. Any wood
joint fillers should be removed before rubblizing the PCC pavement.

Roll the Rubblized Concrete Pavement

The PCC pavement must be rolled after rubblizing. Rolling tightens the
surface by seating loose particles as well as by smoothing the surface in
preparation for paving. This rolling is not intended to achieve additional
densification of the rubblized material or underlying layers. Excessive roll-
ing could reduce particle interlock and cause a weakening of the rubblized
layer.

When the rubblization is done with an RPB, the rubblized concrete
should be rolled with two to four passes of a smooth tandem vibrating
roller Gninimum of 10 tons; 9,000 kilograms) for surface smoothing and
further alignment of the fractured concrete pieces. The roller should be
operated at the highest-frequency setting available at a speed that will
produce at least 10 impacts per foot (33 impacts per meter).

When the rubblization is done with a multiple-head breaker, the rubblized
concrete should be rolled with two passes of a vibratory steel-drum roller fit-
ted with a special “Z” pattern grid on the drum face (see Figure 14.13). The
grid roller should weigh a minimum of 14 tons (12,700 kilograms). This pro-
vides a uniform surface and reduces the size of the surface pieces. The grid
roller is followed by a 25-ton (22,680 kilograms) pneumatic-tire roller and
often a tandem vibratory roller for final seating.
