Sand Mixes

Except for aggregate gradation, the same basic principles apply for
production of sand-emulsion plant mixes as for dense-graded, coarse-
aggregate emulsion mixes. Sand mixes may be used for either base or
surface courses.

The addition of 1 to 2 percent Portland cement will aid in the develop-
ment of early initial strength. Thorough mixing is essential to uniform
distribution of the cement throughout the mixture.

A wide variety of fine aggregates throughout the country have produced
satisfactory results. Blending of two or more aggregates may be necessary
to produce the desirable mix characteristics. Typical gradations are listed
in Table 15.4.

The emulsified-asphalt content usually varies from 6 to 10 percent. Emul-
sion grades SS-1, SS-1h, CSS-1, CSS-Ih, or HFMS-2s may be used, along
with a laboratory-determined amount of mixing water added to the sand.

15.1.7 Proportioning Materials

There is no universally accepted asphalt-emulsion-aggregate mix design
method for dense or open-graded cold mixes. Nearly all mix design meth-
ods are modifications of the Hveem (ASTM D 1560 and 1561 and AASHTO
T 246 and 247) or Marshall (ASTM D 1559 or AASHTO T 245) test meth-
ods. When laboratory equipment is not available, formulas may be used
for approximating the asphalt content for initial mixing.
