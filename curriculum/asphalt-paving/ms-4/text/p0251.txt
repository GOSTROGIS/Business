4.8.1 Stone Matrix Asphalt Mix

Stone matrix asphalt (SMA) mixtures have been used in the United States
since 1991. These mixtures have performed well in Europe for many years,
and experience in the United States is similar. SMA is a rut-resistant, gap-
graded HMA. The mix relies on stone-on-stone contact to provide its internal
strength. The mix uses a high asphalt binder content of 6 to 7 percent or more.
This high binder content provides improved durability. In most situations, the
asphalt binder is a polymer-modified asphalt. Mineral filler and either mineral
or cellulose fibers are added to form a stiff mortar and prevent draindown of
the asphalt cement. The in-place air-voids are kept low—to less than 6 per-
cent to insure that the mix is impermeable to water. SMA is routinely used in
many states as the surface of choice for high-volume roadways and intersec-
tions. Figure 4.24 shows an SMA mix in Colorado.
