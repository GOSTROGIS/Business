In order to calculate the overlay thickness, the designer must first calcu-
late the design thickness for a new full-depth asphalt pavement using appli-
cable input values for climate, traffic, and subgrade strength. Subtracting the
effective thickness of the existing pavement from the design thickness for
the new pavement yields the required thickness of overlay. A simple equa-

Maximum Deflection Method

The Asphalt Institute deflection procedure for calculating overlay thickness is
based on the representative rebound deflection as measured by the Benkelman
Beam or correlated from other vibratory or impulse-based nondestructive
testing devices. A full description of the method is beyond the scope of this
handbook, and the reader is referred to MS-17 for a detailed description of
the procedure. These two methods of overlay design have been incorporated
in the SW-1 software.
