Inspection of the first trial gradation shows the percent passing the

limit. Increase the proportion of aggregate B (in this case to 0.55) and
compute the gradation of the second trial (Table 3.8c).

Inspection now shows the gradation is critical on the No. 30
(0.600 millimeter) sieve. Reduce the proportion of aggregate B to
0.52 and compute the gradation of the third trial (Table 3.8d). The
third trial is a “best fit” considering the percents passing the No. 30
(0.600 millimeter) and No. 200 (0.075 millimeter) sieves.
