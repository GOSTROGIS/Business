Inspection

During construction, the following should be checked:
* The gradation of aggregate. Samples should be taken at least once

636

every 500 tons (453 tonnes) to ensure that the gradation requirements
are being meet.

The viscosity of the asphalt-rubber binder.

The aggregate spread rate. The aggregate spreader should be cali-
brated. This can be done by placing a section of canvas of known size
and passing the spreader over it while spreading the aggregate. The
aggregate on the canvas is weighed and the spread rate calculated.
Also during production, the yield should be checked by measuring a
distance and noting how much aggregate is used to cover that length
of roadway. Knowing the length and width of the roadway and the
amount of aggregate used, it is possible to check the spread rate.
Embedment of aggregate. Determine the embedment of the aggre-
gate a day or so after construction of the chip seal. Remove several
of the largest stones and determine the percentage of embedment of
the stones into the asphalt rubber binder. The goal is 50-70 percent
embedment. Additional pneumatic tire rollers may be necessary to
ensure proper embedment before the liquid membrane cools.

The shot rate of the asphalt-rubber binder. Most modern distribu-
tors have computer-controlled measuring systems.
