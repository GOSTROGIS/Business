Longitudinal/Transverse Streaking

Longitudinal streaking is observed in pavement sections with alternating
lean and heavy lines of asphalt or aggregate that run parallel to the
centerline of the road, as shown in Figure 11.26. A number of factors can
cause longitudinal streaking, including the following:

® The spray bar on the asphalt distributor is not set at the proper height

so spray fans do not overlap properly.

e The spray bar rises as the load in the distributor lightens.

e The nozzles on the spray bar are not at the same angle, are not the

right size, or are plugged with asphalt.

° The asphalt is too cool.

¢ The pump pressure is incorrect.

Transverse streaking is similar in appearance to longitudinal streaking,
except that the streaking runs transverse to the centerline. Transverse
streaking is caused by the uneven distribution of asphalt spray from the
distributor spray bar.
