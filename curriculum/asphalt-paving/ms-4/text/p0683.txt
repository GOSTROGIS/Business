All the materials are combined in a continuous pugmill unit to produce a
homogeneous mix—of the material from the roadway and new HMA.

This material is laid on the roadway with a standard laydown machine.
Breakdown rolling, typically done with a heavy pneumatic roller, should
follow closely behind the laydown machine. The recycled mix cools
quickly and requires both breakdown and finishing rollers to complete the
compaction process quickly. Finish rolling is accomplished with a double-
drum vibratory roller.

13.4.4 Inspection

The following details require attention:

MS-4

Density. Density requirements should be the same as for new HMA.
Mixture requirements. Samples should be taken from the roadway
and compacted in the lab to ensure that the mixture being placed
meets the design volumetric requirements.

Uniform placement. The material should be placed in a uniform
manner that will depend on the nature of the mixture being recycled
and on the uniformity of the feed systems. The mix should be placed
so that no segregation occurs in the pavement and so that the smooth-
ness requirements are met.

Depth control. This is critical if a quality produce is to be obtained.

Vegetation damage control. Vegetation along the roadway can be
damaged by the heat used during the process. Spraying the vegetation
with water can help minimize this problem.

Traffic control maintenance. Typically, traffic can use the pavement
within one hour after compaction has been completed.
