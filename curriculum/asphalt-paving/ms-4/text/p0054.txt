Chapter 11—Pavement Management

This chapter discusses how the pavement management process works
to ensure an adequate pavement performance over time. The components
of a pavement management system and how that system can be used by
an agency as a tool in the development of a pavement preservation sys-
tem are presented. The steps for conducting a pavement evaluation of an
existing pavement and the procedures to accomplish a pavement distress
survey are covered.

Chapter 12—Maintenance of Asphalt Pavements

This chapter presents a detailed discussion of the materials and proce-
dures that can be used to properly maintain an HMA pavement. It includes
a discussion about what is needed to maintain a pavement and why vari-
ous procedures are used. The chapter covers patching, cracking sealing,
and asphalt surface treatments (fog seals, slurry seals, micro-surface, and
chip seals).

Chapter 13—Rehabilitation Methods for Asphalt Pavements

This chapter discusses the proper methods for rehabilitating an HMA
pavement. It contains a detailed discussion of the steps that should be
taken before placing an overlay including patching, interlayers milling and
inlay procedures, hot-in-place recycling, cold-mix recycling, and full-depth
reclamation.

Chapter 14—Rehabilitation of Portland Cement Concrete Pavements

with HMA

This chapter presents the steps that can be taken to rehabilitate a failed
portland cement concrete pavement. A review of the process of reflection
cracking is provided. The use of saw-cut and seal, crack/break and seat,
and rubbilization are discussed in detail.

Chapter 15—Special Uses of Asphalt and HMA

This chapter is a brief discussion about the ways asphalt is used for other
than highways. For example, the HMA is used for hydraulic structures,
recreational facilities, leaching pads, and racetracks.
