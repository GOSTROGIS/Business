of pull,” which can be thought of as the vertical center of gravity of the

ridge will only reflect a small amount of vertical movement at the tow point.
When the drive tires in the rear pass over this same point, the vertical move-
ment is also reduced. Track pavers also provide this leveling effect. They
have a series of small bogie wheels that are free to move up and down inde-
pendent of one another as they run inside of the flexible continuous track.
