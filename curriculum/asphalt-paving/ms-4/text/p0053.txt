Chapter 5—Soils and Drainage for Asphalt Pavements

This chapter presents a background on the characteristics of soils as they
relate to the design and construction of HMA pavements. The test proce-
dures used to evaluate the strength of soils are reviewed, and the develop-
ment of a proper drainage system for an HMA pavement is covered.

Chapter 6—Asphalt Pavement Structural Design

This chapter presents an overview of the various Asphalt Institute
pavement thickness design methods and how those methods relate to
other thickness design methods used for the design of HMA pavements.
This description includes a discussion about the required inputs, such
as environmental (temperature, rainfall), traffic (type, mix of traffic, and
traffic volume), and subgrade strengths. It also discusses how to handle
very heavy wheel loads in the development of a pavement design for a
specialized use such as container yards or airfields.

Chapter 7—Manufacture of Hot Mix Asphalt

This chapter presents a discussion of the HMA manufacturing process.
It covers the components of an HMA manufacturing facility, how aggre-
gate and RAP are fed in a controlled manner into the HMA plant, how it is
dried, and how the dust generated by the production process is controlled.
It details the operation of both a batch plant and a drum-mix plant, and
it provides a detailed discussion of troubleshooting problems that might
develop during the production of the HMA.

Chapter 8—Hot Mix Asphalt Paving

This chapter presents the process of actually constructing an HMA
pavement including: subgrade and base course preparation for a new
pavement; placement of the HMA layers; and pavement surface preparation
for an HMA overlay over an existing pavement. The operation of the paving
machine is covered in detail, along with steps that can be taken to prevent
problems during the placement of HMA mixtures.

Chapter 9—Hot Mix Asphalt Compaction

This chapter covers the concepts behind the proper compaction of an
HMA pavement. The principles of compaction that are discussed include
the following: the effect of HMA properties; environmental effects; layer
thickness; subgrade and subbase as it affects compaction of the HMA;
and the time available for compaction. A discussion of the different types
of compaction equipment is provided, along with how that equipment
should be used to properly compact an HMA pavement.
