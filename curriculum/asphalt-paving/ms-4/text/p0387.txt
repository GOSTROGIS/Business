7.5.2 Batch-Plant Production
Problem-Solving Guide

The following guide is useful for solving batch-plant mix-deficiency

issues. Common mix deficiencies and causes are discussed.

Gradation Shift/Aggregates

Check for the following:

° Hole in screen

e Hole in screen exit chute

¢ Hole in walls between hot bins

° Cold-feed ratio/blend change (affecting hot-bin gradations)

° Cold-feed bin flow restriction (affecting hot-bin gradations)

* Cold-feed bin calibration shift (affecting hot-bin gradations)

° Gradation change in feed aggregates (affecting hot-bin gradations)

° Improper loader management technique on stockpiled material
(affecting hot-bin gradations)

Gradation Shift/Coarser on -200 Mesh (75 Micron)

Sieve

Check for the following:

° Hot-bin stratification from vibration/idling

° Drastic change in production rate (ow to high takes time for baghouse
fines to catch up to cold-feed change)

° Overdrafting dryer (less fines in mix from damper being more open
relative to previous or typical production rate, together with baghouse
fines being wasted or partial return)

* Baghouse dust-return or mineral-filler additive malfunction (causing
reduced baghouse dust-return or mineral-filler additive)

° Gradation change in feed aggregate(s) (see above)

° Cold-feed ratio/blend changed, affecting hot-bin gradations (see above)

Gradation Shift/Finer on -200 Mesh (75 Micron) Sieve

Check for the following:

* Dust slough (slide) in fines bin

* Hot-bin stratification from vibration/idling

° Drastic change in production rate (high to low takes time for baghouse
fines to catch up to cold-feed change)

* Underdrafting dryer Gnore fines in mix from damper being less open
relative to previous or typical production rate, together with baghouse
fines being wasted or partial return)

¢ Baghouse dust-return or mineral-filler additive malfunction (causing
increased baghouse dust-return or mineral-filler additive)

* Gradation change in feed aggregate(s) (see above)

° Cold-feed ratio/blend changed, affecting hot-bin gradations (see above)
