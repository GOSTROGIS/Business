Timing

The saw-cutting and sealing should be done as soon as possible after
placement (the next morning) of the HMA layer because the saw can be
operated without damaging the HMA layer, but it should be done no later
than 48 hours after the wearing course is placed. The saw-cutting might
possibly damage the joint edges, resulting in poor sealant adhesion and
joint spalling. The concern is that the joint will reflect through before the
saw cut is made. The saw-cutting can be done with either wet or dry cut-
ting. Sawing and sealing should not be done if the pavement is wet or if
the ambient temperature is less than 40°F (4°C).
