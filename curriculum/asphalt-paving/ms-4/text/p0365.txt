Heating elements (on the silo walls) hotter than the mix temperature may
damage the mix by overheating it at the point of contact, and may also cause
it to build up on the silo walls. This buildup, in turn, ultimately acts as an
insulator, which reduces the long-term storage capability of the silo. Heat
should be set at—or at no more than 20°-30°F (11°-17°C) higher than—the
mix temperature.
