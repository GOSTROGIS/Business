When the dry-mix cycle is completed, the asphalt is dropped into the
mixer and the wet-mix cycle starts. The wet-mix cycle lasts 25 to 45 sec-
onds, with the usual length being 30 to 35 seconds. The cycle time is
determined by the time required to produce a homogenous, well-coated
mix. Uniform coating of large-rock mixes typically takes longer than fine
mixtures. The time is usually determined subjectively by visual inspection.
Some regulatory agencies set minimum times for the mixing process.

7.1.2 Drum-Mix Facilities

Figures 7.3 and 7.4 show the key components and flow process in a
drum-mix facility.

The continuous flow of a drum-mix plant requires the availability of
stockpiled aggregate material that will meet the specifications when
proportioned without rescreening. The individual aggregate materials are
proportioned directly from the cold-feed bins to the final job-mix formula.
The materials are then sent to the drum to be dried and heated. In route
to the drum, the blended aggregate passes over a belt scale, which is a
continuous-weighing device. The plant’s control system uses the reading
from the belt scale to calculate the liquid asphalt binder and other additives
