QUALITY CONTROL

oF Hot Mix ASPHALT

Quality control is the set of actions and considerations necessary to
assess and adjust production and construction processes in order to
control the level of quality being produced. Quality control practices
are usually provided by contractors and/or materials suppliers to
monitor the manufacturing process.

Acceptance is the sampling and testing and inspection to determine
the degree of compliance with contract requirements. Acceptance
practices are usually provided by the owner to determine whether
specific terms of the plans or specifications have been met.
