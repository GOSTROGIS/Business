In addition to the principal tests (penetration or viscosity) for defining
grade, several other tests are required to determine specific properties for
these products. For general reference, the tests are listed in Table 2.2, along
with the appropriate AASHTO or ASTM test method and indications of
which specification requires the test. Always refer to the current AASHTO
or ASTM specification.

As a final note, since viscosity is a fundamental material property (unlike
penetration), there is no universal relationship between the two proper-
ties. The relationship between penetration and viscosity varies for different
asphalts produced from different crude sources.
