Materials Selection

The materials required for an SMA mix include aggregates, asphalt
binder, mineral filler, and a stabilizer, which is usually either a cellulose
or a mineral fiber.

SMA mixtures require the use of a high-quality aggregate. AASHTO speci-
fications require that 100 percent of the coarse aggregate have at least one
crushed face and at least 90 percent must have two or more crushed faces.
The strength of an SMA mixture is achieved through stone-on-stone contact.
Therefore, most agencies have placed more stringent requirements on aggre-
gate used in SMA. AASHTO requires a maximum Los Angeles Abrasion loss of
30 percent. But some states—because of a lack of high-quality aggregates—
have built successfully performing projects with a less-stringent requirement.

The gradation for different SMA mixtures is shown in Table 4.9. Note that for
the 19 millimeters, 12.5 millimeters, and 9.5 millimeters mixes, the mixtures
have a low percentage passing the No. 4 sieve (4.75 millimeters) and a high
percentage passing the No. 200 sieve. This is required to provide a mix with
stone-on-stone contact and to provide a high VMA (generally 17 percent). The
high percentage passing the No. 200 sieve (0.075 millimeter) is required to
provide a stiff mix that will be rut resistant and to insure that draindown does
not occur during construction. The high percentage of minus No. 200 material
