Truck Sampling

Truck sampling (Figure 10.5) techniques are used to obtain samples of
the HMA. Quite often, the samples are taken immediately after loading
from the plant where sampling platforms allow the technicians to stand
at the height of the truck bed. HMA in the back of the trucks can become
segregated, making it more difficult to obtain representative samples.
Good adherence to the specified sampling methods is required.
