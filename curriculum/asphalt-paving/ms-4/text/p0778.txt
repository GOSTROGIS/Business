15.5. Asphalt Roofs

15.5.1 History

Roofing was probably the world’s first craft because shelter has always
been a primary need of people. Asphalt, with its extraordinary combina-
tion of waterproofing, preservative, and cementing qualities, was an early
discovery of ancient people and has been used extensively in the con-
struction of their buildings and roads.

Today, asphalt is recognized more than ever as an outstanding roofing
material and is used extensively to cover all kinds of buildings, from homes
to factories. Asphalt roofing materials and construction methods are fully
covered by specifications of the American Society for Testing and Materials
(ASTM) and many government and private agencies. In addition, all types of
asphalt roofing are sanctioned by federal and other public agencies.

Asphalt roofs may be built in single- or multiple-layer systems with
impregnated fabrics or with manufactured shingles.

15.5.2 Types of Asphalt Roofing

Prepared Roofing

Asphalt shingles. This type is composed of three basic materials—
asphalt, felt, and mineral granules. The felt is impregnated with asphalt.
Both sides of the saturated felt are then covered with a harder, tougher
coat of asphalt, in which are embedded mineral granules. This composi-
tion is then cut into individual shingles or strips. Asphalt shingles are
available in almost any color from pastels to deep rich tones. Factory
techniques for the application of granules have improved with the devel-
opment of new permanent colors, and granules may be applied in almost .
any blend or pattern of colors desired.

Asphalt roll roofing—smooth-surfaced. This type consists of a single
layer of roofing felt saturated with asphalt and coated on both sides with
a harder asphalt.

Asphalt roll roofing—mineral-surfaced. This type consists of smooth-
surfaced roll roofing in which mineral granules have been embedded on
either one or both sides.

Prepared roofings are those products manufactured and packaged ready
to apply to the roof deck, usually by nailing only. Asphalt shingles and cer-
tain roll roofings are in this category. They consist of a base of saturated
felt to which has been added an asphalt coating and, usually, a surfacing
of mineral granules.
