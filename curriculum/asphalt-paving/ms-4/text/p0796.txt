Design of the Porous Pavement

The porous asphalt course consists of an OGFC approximately 2 to 4
inches (50 to 100 millimeters) thick. The OGFC should be designed fol-
lowing the procedures discussed in Chapter 4. The OGFC is placed on a
2-inch (50 millimeters) filter course of %-inch (12.5 millimeters), single-
sized crushed aggregate. A primary purpose of this course is to protect
the reservoir course from disturbance when the paving machine places the
OGFC. Without this firm paving platform, the single-size aggregate used
for the reservoir layer might move under the paver.

The reservoir course consists of a single-size stone. The depth is a func-
tion of the storage volume, the structural needs of the pavement and
the frost depth. A typical thickness for this layer is 8-9 inches (200-225
millimeters). The stone has a maximum top size of 1-3 inches (37.5-75
millimeters). This gradation will provide the layer with approximately 40
percent air voids, which will provide for runoff storage. A 9-inch (225
millimeters) layer will provide a little over 3% inches (88 millimeters) of
runoff storage. If the reservoir is placed over marginal soils (infiltration
