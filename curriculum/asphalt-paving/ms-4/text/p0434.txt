To prevent the mixture from sticking and tearing, the screed is heated to
its operational temperature, around 250°F (121°C). The metal areas of the
material handling system that come into contact with the HMA should be
sprayed with an approved release agent before charging the hopper and
the screed. This will keep the asphalt from sticking to the colder metal sur-
faces and help the HMA flow through the paver. The tow-points on both
sides of the paver should be manually centered so they have maximum
travel to provide necessary depth correction.
