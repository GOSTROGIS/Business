When the Asphalt Institute (AI decided to give this flagship publication
its first major overhaul in almost 20 years, we knew from the start that this
would be a team-based project. The expertise required to accurately address
such a broad range of topics simply necessitated this approach. We also
knew that the sheer scale of the task invited the participation of others.

Our initial steps were to review the 1989 version of The Asphalt Handbook
and to ask ourselves what the new handbook needed to include. Dr. Bob
Boyer, P.E., former Asphalt Institute senior field engineer and now a consul-
tant in Florida, led us through a yearlong process to develop a comprehen-
sive and detailed outline for the manual, complete with references for each
chapter. We are indebted to Boyer for his dedication to this fundamentally
important stage of our project.

As we sought to build our writing team, we explored various alterna-
tives with individual authors, partnerships, and companies, all of which
added value to our process. We are indebted to them for their insights,
advice, and remarkable excitement for what we were undertaking.

Selecting AMEC Earth & Environmental Inc., with Douglas Hanson as
our content editor, proved to be the right choice for this project. AMEC’s
group of highly qualified experts with unique experience and knowledge
combined with the Asphalt Institute’s team to precisely deliver a new man-
ual. We especially express our thanks to the following chapter authors for
their expertise and dedication to this project:
