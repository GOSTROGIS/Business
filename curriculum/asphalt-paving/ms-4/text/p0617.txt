12.3.1 Materials

The following materials can be used for crack sealing:

° Liquid asphalt—cutback asphalt; MC-30, 70 and 250; emulsified asphalt;
SS-1, SS-1h, CSS-1, CSS-1h, MFMS-1, and MFMS-1h

° Modified-asphalt crack sealer, ASTM D 6690, or equal

¢ HMA mixtures; slurry seal and hot sand mixes

* Blotter sand

12.3.2 Crack-Sealing Procedures

Clean the cracks using high-pressure air, sandblasting, wire brushing,
hot-air blasting, or high-pressure water.

Cleaning is an essential step, because when the crack is not thoroughly
dried and cleaned, the sealant will not adhere to the sides of the crack.
The most effective procedure is sandblasting, but it is very labor intensive.
Hot-air blasting is done with a hot compressed-air lance or heat lance con-
nected to an air compressor (see Figure 12.11). This method helps dry the
crack, and when the sealing operation follows closely behind the hot-air
drying, the heated crack surfaces help the sealant adhere to the crack.
Care should be taken when using a heat lance. The heat lance produces
superheated air that can burn the binder in the mix along the crack sur-
faces. This will cause early failure of the sealant.
