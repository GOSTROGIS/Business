Demonstrating Benefits

The benefits associated with a pavement preservation program can be
demonstrated in a number of ways. Most agencies focus on the expected
improvement in pavement conditions and safety for the traveling public.
However, it may be more effective to convey the cost-effectiveness of a
pavement preservation program to politicians and upper management
because they often have business backgrounds rather than engineering
backgrounds. For example, an agency may demonstrate the cost of
achieving a targeted condition level under both a worst-first strategy and
a pavement preservation strategy. In most instances, the cost of achieving
the same condition under a worst-first strategy is four to six times more
expensive than it would be under a pavement preservation strategy.

importance of Training

The importance of communicating the pavement preservation philosophy
cannot be overstated. To be fully integrated within the agency, it is
important that personnel understand the underlying philosophy and their
role in supporting the agency’s pavement preservation efforts. A number
of training programs on pavement preservation techniques are available
through the Asphalt Institute, the American Society of Civil Engineers, and
the FHWA’s National Highway Institute.
