7.2.9 Lime Additive Bulk Storage and
Feed Silos

Some specifying agencies require certain aggregates to be treated with
lime for antistripping control.

Bulk lime is usually delivered to the plant site in tankers, transferred
pneumatically with a blower into a storage silo, and then introduced in
ways similar to that of a bulk mineral filler additive. Depending on local
requirements and preferences, lime is either introduced into the mixing
section of the plant or mixed with the aggregate before the drying process.
When the lime is introduced into the mixing area of the plant, the equip-
ment shown in Figures 7.14 and 7.15 can be used. When lime is mixed
with aggregate before the drying process, equipment such as that shown
in Figure 7.8 is often used. Lime can also be premixed with the aggregate
before shipment to the plant site.

The control routine is similar to that previously described for bulk mineral
filler additives.
