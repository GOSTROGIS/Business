Binder Selection Guidelines for RAP Mixtures

Recommended Virgin Asphalt Binder Grade RAP Percentage
No change in binder selection <15
Select a virgin binder one grade softer than normal (e.g., select a 15-25
PG 58-28 if a PG 64-22 would normally be used)
Select a virgin binder using the blending charts > 25

Development of Final Mix Design
The final mix design is developed following one of the standard design

procedures (Superpave, Marshall, or Hveem) and must meet the requirements
of those design procedures.
