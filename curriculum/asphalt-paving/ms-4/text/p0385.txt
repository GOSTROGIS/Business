Gradation Shift/ Coarser on -200 Mesh (75 Micron)
Sieve

Check for the following:

° Drastic change in production rate (low to high takes time for baghouse
fines to catch up to cold-feed change)

° Overdrafting dryer (less fines in mix from damper being more open
relative to previous or typical production rate, together with baghouse
fines being wasted or partial return)

° Baghouse dust return or mineral filler additive malfunction (causing
reduced baghouse dust return or mineral filler additive)

° Gradation change in feed aggregates (see above)

Gradation Shift/ Finer on -200 Mesh (75 Micron) Sieve

Check for the following:

° Drastic change in production rate (high to low takes time for baghouse
fines to catch up to cold-feed change)

° Underdrafting dryer (more fines in mix from damper being less open
relative to previous or typical production rate, together with baghouse
fines being wasted or partial return)

° Baghouse dust return or mineral-filler additive malfunction (causing
increased baghouse dust return or mineral additive)

° Gradation change in feed aggregates (see above)

Low Air Voids
Check for the following:
e Dust-return or dust-additive malfunction (see above)
° Production-rate change (high to low with immediate sampling)
° Lack of draft in dryer—retained fines in dryer/primary baghouse
* Gradation change in feed aggregates (see above)
° AC content high (see below)
° Specific gravity change in aggregates

High Voids
Check for the following:
e Dust-return or dust-additive malfunction (see above)
* Production-rate change Cow to high with immediate sampling)
* Overdrafting dryer—more fines to baghouse versus in dryer/primary
(and baghouse fines are being wasted or used partially)
° Gradation change feed aggregates (see above)
* AC content low (see below)
° Specific gravity change in aggregates

AG Content High
Check for the following:
° Calibration of belt scale (reading high)
¢ Binding belt scale (reading high)
° Wind effect on belt scale (reading high)
* Calibration of AC meter (reading low)
