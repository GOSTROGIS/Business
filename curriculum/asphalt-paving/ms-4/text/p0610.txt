A “patcher truck” is a useful piece of equipment for this type of mainte-
nance work (see Figure 12.6). A patcher truck has bins and tanks for storage
of HMA, liquid asphalt, and other materials. The bins and tanks are heated
so the HMA and liquid asphalt are maintained at proper application tem-
peratures. Tools such as a pneumatic hammer, a vibrating plate compactor,
etc., are located on the truck, thus making it a self-contained patching unit.

Another piece of equipment that can greatly facilitate patching work is
a cold-milling machine. When large and numerous patches are required,
a medium-size milling machine (Figure 12.7) works well. When small and
numerous patches are required, a small milling machine (Figure 12.8)
does an excellent job.
