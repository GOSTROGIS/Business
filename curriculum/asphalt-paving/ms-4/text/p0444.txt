‘Figure 8.32 Milled-In Rumble Strips

The rolled-in method was the original method used for constructing
rumble strips. Steering a straight line with a large steel-wheel roller and
getting uniform indentation as the HMA cools at different rates are the two
major drawbacks to this method. Multiple small milling heads mounted on
rubbert-tire machines have automated the process and have become the
predominate method of installation.
