Other factors that should be considered when evaluating the suitability of
using CIPR are these:

¢ Numerous manholes or drainage outlets

° Steep grades, such as five percent or more

° Asphalt layer thickness less than 2 inches, (50 millimeters) which is

not practical for CIPR

° Traffic control on a roadway with a large number of business-access
driveways
Situations where it is infeasible to operate all of the equipment in
sequence due to frequent intersections and tight curves.

Cold in-place recycling should not be done in cool, inclement weather.
CIPR should be accomplished at temperatures above 50° (10°C), with no
freezing weather in the next 24 hours. It should not be done when fog or
rain are present or imminent.

13.6.2 Mix Design

There is no nationally recognized method for the design of CIPR mixes,
although many agencies have developed procedures for doing so. The
simplest methods consist of using empirical equations or the experience
of the mix-design engineer to establish suggested asphalt content for the
mix based on the amount and consistency of the asphalt binder in the
