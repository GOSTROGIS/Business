Pneumatic-tire rollers have several advantages:

° They provide a more uniform degree of compaction than steel-wheel
rollers.

They improve the seal near the surface, thus decreasing the perme-
ability of the layer.

° They orient the aggregate particles for greatest stability, as high-pressure

truck tires do after using the HMA surface for some time.

The main disadvantage of a pneumatic-tire roller is the tendency for the
tires to pick up fines from the surface of the HMA mix—unless the tires
are kept clean and warm. This can be a serious problem with mixtures
using polymer-modified asphalt binder and especially for SMA. Although
pneumatic-tire rollers have been used successfully to compact SMA, their
use is discouraged with this mixture classification.

Several self-propelled pneumatic-tire rollers have a device to change tire
inflation while the roller is operating. This inflation-on-the-run system auto-
matically maintains any preset tire pressure or can raise or lower the infla-
tion pressure while the roller is operating. Some compacting conditions and.
requirements require different inflation pressures. However, tire inflation pres-
sures should all be very close between tires and checked at regular intervals.

Weight

Self-propelled pneumatic-tire rollers vary in weight from 10.5 to 30 tons
(9.5 to 27.2 tonnes). Ballast can be added to the machines to increase the
weight. Widths of these rollers range from 60 to 90 inches (524 to 2286
millimeters).
