The pavement design procedure for highways and streets allows
engineers to consider a range of six material types and three MAAT levels.
Designers only need to select the appropriate curve from the 18 design curves
that are available based on climate and material type. For the selected curve,
the designer enters the curve with input values for design ESALs and subgrade
M.. The intersection of these input values on the design curve produces the
solution to the problem. SW-1 steps the designer through the process in a user-
friendly interface and prompts the designer for the necessary information.

The SW-1 also allows a conventional design with aggregate base thick-
nesses varying between 4 and 18 inches.

6.1.4 Airports

Air-carrier airport pavements support heavy commercial jets with gross
loads well in excess of 60,000 pounds (27,000 kilograms). A vast second-
ary network of thousands of smaller airports allows the nation’s fleet of
general aviation (GA) aircraft to serve local communities. GA aircraft have
gross weights up to 60,000 pounds (27,000 kilograms) (Figure 6.3).
