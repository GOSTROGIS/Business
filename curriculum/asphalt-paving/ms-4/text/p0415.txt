switch attached to a paddle physically touching the flow of material, which

When a balanced screed (equal distances from the center of the paver
to the outside edges of the screed) is being run, the paver is much easier
to set up and operate. If the screed is wider or paving deeper on one side,
a greater amount of HMA is required on that side. In this unbalanced flow
condition, fine tuning the flow-control gates or adjusting the speed of the
individual material conveyors is critical.

8.4.3 Screeds

Screed Components and Adjustments

Crown

The main screed has the ability to be crowned at the center of the
screed. With this crown, the screed can be set up to give either a positive
or a negative crown to the profile of the HMA pavement being laid. Posi-
tive crown is used when the centerline of the screed is intended to be the
centerline of the road and when fall or drainage is required in both direc-
tions. Negative crown is used if the center of the HMA mat being laid is
used for drainage.
