15 and 30 degrees (0.26 and 0.52 radians). It is important that all nozzles be
set at the proper angle within close tolerances.

The height of the spray bar above the pavement surface must be adjusted
to ensure uniformity of the asphalt spread. It is important, too, that the
correct height be maintained throughout the application. A spray bar at
the incorrect height causes streaking. Also, if the spray bar is set too high,
wind distortion of the spray fans may occur.

For example, the best results with 4-inch (100-millimeter) nozzle spacing
come from an exact triple coverage of the spray fans (see Figure 12.17). But
in order to get triple coverage with 6-inch (150 millimeters) nozzle spacing,
the height of the spray bar would be so great that it could be subject to wind
distortion. In such cases, a double-coverage pattern is used.
