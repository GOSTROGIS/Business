41.3.1 Plant Mix

Asphalt paving mixtures or hot mix asphalt is manufactured in a central
mixing facility (see Figure 1.3). The workhorse of the asphalt industry is the
dense-graded HMA mixture. It consists of a well-graded, high-quality aggre-
gate and asphalt cement. The two components are mechanically mixed while
hot to ensure good coating of the aggregate and consistency of the mix.
In order to ensure the quality of the resultant mix it is necessary to care-
fully proportion the blend of several different aggregates to consistently
produce a uniform final gradation that meets the requirements of local
specifications.

Typically, the asphalt and aggregate are heated to 250°-325°F (121°-
163°C). The hot mixture, kept hot during transit, is hauled to the construc-
tion site, where it is spread on the roadway by an asphalt-paving machine.
The smooth layer placed by the paver is compacted by rollers to proper
density before the asphalt cools (see Figure 1.4).
