Maximum Specific Gravity of the HMA Mixture,
AASHTO T 209 or ASTM D 2041

Maximum theoretical specific gravity (G,_) is the ratio of the mass in air
of a unit volume of the asphalt and aggregate in the mixture at a stated
temperature to the mass in air of equal density of an equal volume of gas-
free distilled water at a stated temperature. In other words, the maximum
theoretical specific gravity (G,,) is the mass of the asphalt and aggregate
mixture divided by the volume of the asphalt coated particles.
