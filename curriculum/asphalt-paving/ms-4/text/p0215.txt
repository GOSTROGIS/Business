To overcome this problem, recent studies have centered on the use of
the CoreLok® vacuum-sealing device (see Figure 4.10). This device has
been shown to be more accurate than conventional methods such as water
displacement, Parafilm, and dimensional analysis. The CoreLok® device
uses an automatic vacuum chamber with a puncture-resistant bag (Figure
4.11), which tightly conforms to the sides of the sample and prevents
water from infiltrating the sample.

The water-displacement procedure (AASHTO T 166 or ASTM D 2726)
can overestimate the density of a hot mix asphalt sample. This overestima-
tion is very small for fine-graded mixes, increases for coarse-graded mixes,
and increases again for gap-graded mixes such as SMA.
