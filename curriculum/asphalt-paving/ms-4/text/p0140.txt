described, are often the subject of questions (for various reasons). A sam-
pling of these questions are addressed in this section.

What Is the Vapor Pressure of Asphalt at Typical
Storage Temperatures?

It is estimated that at a typical inventory temperature of 325°F (163°C),
the vapor pressure of petroleum asphalt is less than 0.01 pounds per square
inch (1.5E-03 kiloPascals).

What Is the Typical Thermal BTU Value for a Pound
of Asphalt?

The BTU value varies by temperature and by the percentage of min-
eral matter in the asphalt. Therefore, a range is usually quoted, but an
approximate value of 158,500 BTU/gallon may be considered an average
value typical of an AC-10 (or PG 58) grade. Most refineries will have a
calorimeter in the lab that may be used to determine this value. Actual test
results should be obtained if a more accurate value is needed.

What Is the Typical Value for the Specific Heat

of Asphalt?
A typical value for the specific heat of asphalt is 0.22 calorie/gram-°C.
