This unit consists of an emulsion tank, aggregate tank, heating components,
high-volume blower, telescoping boom with injection head, and the necessary
controls.

The procedure is essentially a one-person operation. It consists of clean-
ing the area to be patched with compressed air to remove loose material
and debris, applying a tack coat of hot asphalt emulsion, blowing the
combined aggregate and hot emulsion into the patch with forced air, and
treating the top of the patch with a dust coat of aggregate.

The aggregate used in this procedure is essentially a one-size aggregate
similar to a chip-seal aggregate. Compaction of the mix is accomplished by
the force of the air when the mix is.sprayed into the patch. This procedure
is especially adaptable to pothole patching. The success or failure of this
process depends largely on the operator’s skill level.
