In lieu of reiterating these design procedures, a range of asphalt and
aggregate applications is provided in this section as a guide to estimating
the proper quantity of materials for a given aggregate size. Adjustments
should be made for actual field and material conditions. Tables 12.1, 12.2,
and 12.3 provide some guidance on the proper asphalt and cover-aggregate
application rates for single-, double-, and triple-surface treatments.

These quantities of asphalt cover the average range of conditions that
include primed granular bases and old pavement surfaces. The quantities
and types of materials may be varied according to local conditions and
experience. The mass (weight) of aggregate shown in the tables is based
on aggregate with a specific gravity of 2.65. In case the specific gravity
of the aggregate used is lower than 2.55 or higher than 2.75, the amount
shown in tables 12.1, 12.2, and 12.3 should be multiplied by the ratio that
the bulk specific gravity of the aggregate used bears to 2.65. The lower
application rates of asphalt shown in the tables should be used for aggre-
gate having gradations on the fine side of the specified limits. The higher
application rates should be used for aggregate having gradations on the
coarser side of the specified limits.

It is important to adjust the asphalt content for the condition of the road,
increasing it if the road is absorbent and/or badly cracked or decreasing it
if the road is “fat” with flushed asphalt.
