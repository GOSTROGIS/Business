Adjusting Extensions on Variable-Width Screeds

Front- and rear-extendable screeds are widely used. These screeds have
numerous adjustments incorporated into them to produce high-quality
asphalt pavements. Some adjustments are very specific to the design of the
screed, and the manufacturer’s manual should be checked for procedures
for adjustment and maintenance. The objective in operating an extendable
screed with either front- or rear-mounted extensions is to maintain the
screed plate surface in full contact with the HMA pavement being laid at
all times. This will produce an HMA pavement with a consistent texture
from side to side that is also free of blemishes and steps where the main
screed and extension meet. To do this, the extensions must be perfectly
aligned with the main screed. This adjustment is the same for both front-
