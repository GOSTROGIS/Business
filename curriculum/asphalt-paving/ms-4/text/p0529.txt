In some cases, compaction is accepted based on nuclear density results.
Nuclear densities should be required to be correlated to core density tests
if they are used for acceptance.

There are two ways to calculate compaction values, and they can give
very different results. Compaction can be specified by the percentage of
either the maximum theoretical density or the bulk laboratory density. The
method specified should be clarified before bidding. Differences in com-
pactive effort required by the methods and relative values of compaction
specifications are discussed in more detail in Section 10.8.3 under “Refer-
ence Density Specifications.”
