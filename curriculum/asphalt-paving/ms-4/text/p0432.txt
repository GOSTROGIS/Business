8.5 Joint Construction

3.5.1 Transverse or Construction Joints

Good transverse construction joints, like all good HMA joints, are straight,
with a vertical face on the cold side of the joint. The cold face of the joint
should be vertical. There are several ways to establish and maintain the
vertical face on the cold side while traffic is rolling off the edge. The easiest
and most economical method is to build a wedge of sacrificial material that
ramps down to the underlining surface and stays in place until the paving
operation is resumed, at which time the material in the wedge is removed.
There are three methods of properly building the sacrificial wedge:

° In the most widely used method, the paver is stopped and the screed
is lifted over the head of material in the auger area. The excess mate-
rial, which is to be used to build the wedge, is then separated by
shoveling a trench in the hot material just downstream from the joint.
The side of the joint that remains in place should be pushed back
with a lute and a sharp vertical edge built. A bond-breaking material

wedge area and cover the vertical face (see Figure 8.24). After the area
