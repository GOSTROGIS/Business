Proper viscosity is essential for the asphalt to flow easily through the
spray nozzles and adequately coat the aggregate particles. Cutback asphalt,
although already fluid, needs some heating to bring it to the proper vis-
cosity for spraying, which is 20-120 centistokes. Proper safety precautions
should always be taken when heating cutback asphalt. Mixing should
begin immediately, because the viscosity will quickly rise above that rec-
ommended for mixing, which is 150-300 centistokes. The volatiles in cut-
back asphalt keep them fluid long enough for the completion of road
mixing. The aggregate temperature should be at least 50°F (10°C) in the
shade at mixing time.
