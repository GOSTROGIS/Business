5.2.4 Strength Correlations

Most methods used for the design of highway and airfield pavements
are based on elastic-layered theory. The preferred method for determining
the strength of the subgrade soils and unbound subbase/base materials
is the resilient modulus test. But the equipment and the test procedure is
complex and time consuming. Therefore, a number of correlation equa-
tions have been developed to estimate the resilient modulus of unbound
materials. Table 5.5 summarizes the correlations used.
