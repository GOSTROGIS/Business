The Los Angeles Abrasion test has been the primary method to mea-
sure the wear or abrasion resistance of mineral aggregate. Equipment and
procedures for this test are detailed in AASHTO T 96 (ASTM C 131). The
abrasion machine is illustrated in Figure 3.7. The test involves sieving and
proportioning the aggregate to a standard grading, and then placing the
sample into the drum of the abrasion device along with a specified num-
ber of steel balls. The material is then subjected to abrasion and impact
forces inside the drum as it rotates through 500 revolutions. The sample
is then sieved over a No. 12 (1.70 millimeters) sieve. From the mass of the
retained material, the mass of loss material is determined and then calcu-
lated as a percentage of loss of the original mass. The common criterion
in specifications for dense graded mixes is a maximum allowable abrasion
loss of 40 to 50 percent.
