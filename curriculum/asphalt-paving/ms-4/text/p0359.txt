7.2./ Dust-Return Equipment

Most plants are fitted with fabric filter collectors, allowing operating
personnel to return the captured dust to the mix-production process. As
mentioned above, fines collected from primary-type collectors are returned
100 percent to the process without any contemplation of partial return
(see Figure 7.14).

Dust from a baghouse secondary collector, however, can be returned
entirely or in part, depending on the requirements of the job-mix formula.
Fines return is accomplished mechanically with screw conveyors or pneu-
matically with compressed air or blower systems.

The general practice is to design mixes so all the fines collected in the
baghouse are reused in the mix or so all the fines are wasted/removed from
the process. This keeps the equipment options and operating principles
simple.
