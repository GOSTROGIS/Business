7. Foreman’s/inspector’s
Check Sheet

The following list includes important points to keep in mind for the
project foreman, or inspector, who is responsible for placing an HMA
pavement.

To build a smooth pavement:

e Never run the hopper empty between loads (The level of material in
the hoppers should never be allowed to fall below the bottom of the
flow gates.)

Establish and maintain a continuous paving speed. (Establish the speed
by balancing the delivery of material with the compaction process.)
Control the head of material in the auger area to within +1 inch.

Do not allow trucks to bump the paver.

Do not dump excess material on the grade (When dumping directly
into the paver hopper or cleaning truck tailgates.)

Do not fold the hopper wings into an empty conveyor system. (Fold
hoppers often enough while moving forward to keep HMA at place-
ment temperature.)

Practice good starting and stopping techniques of the paver.

Watch for overcorrecting of the depth screws.

Make sure the edger plate is adjusted properly.

°

To make good longitudinal joints:

Strive to minimize segregation at the outside edges of the new HMA.
Steer a straight line on the first lane.

Utilize the proper overlap of the cold HMA pavement being matched.
The depth of the HMA pavement being laid should be approximately
25 percent greater in depth than the HMA pavement being matched
for rolldown.

Watch that material is not allowed to flow out or under the edger plates.
(This excess material can make getting a good joint impossible.)

If luting is required, push material back to the hot side of the joint
only and do not cast material across the mat.

Use proper rolling procedures for compaction. (See Chapter 9.)

To make good transverse joints:

Ensure proper shutdown on original pass. (The HMA pavement left
must be the proper depth and slope. Check it with a straightedge.)
Construct a temporary wedge using some type of bond-breaker
material.

Remove the wedge, and prepare the area where the joint is to be
made. (Clean all dirt, loose material, and tack the area.)

Align the paver with the previously laid mat.
