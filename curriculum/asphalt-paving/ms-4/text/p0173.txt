Dense-graded (or well-graded) aggregate structures plot near and along
the maximum-density line. Mixes with such gradings tend to have low Voids
in Mineral Aggregate (VMA). Plots of gap-graded and open-graded aggre-
gates gradation curve out or away from the maximum-density line (above
or below). Greater VMA is achieved with these aggregate structures. Those
gradations that generally curve below the maximum-density line are consid-
ered coarse gradings, and those that generally curve above the line are con-
sidered fine gradings. See Figure 3.11 for examples of plotted gradations.

duce an aggregate blend that meets grading specifications (or even a spe-
cific desired grading) for a particular asphalt mix is common in asphalt
construction. A number of methods are available for determining relative
amounts of various aggregates needed to obtain a desired gradation.
