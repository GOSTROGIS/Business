Once the target weight is achieved, the controls hold back the asphalt
release until the dry-mix cycle in the pugmill is finished. Then a drain
valve is opened (or a pump is engaged) to allow the asphalt to fall or to
be pumped into the mixer. The drain mechanism remains open or on until
the scale returns to zero.

The entire process of weighing and discharge is typically computer
controlled.

7.3.0 Mixing/Coating with a Batch Plant

Aggregates and asphalt are mixed in a pugmill in a batch plant. The
pugmill is a twin-shaft design, with adjustable hardened steel tips and
hardened steel liners. Tips can be configured for different styles of mixing.

manual provides suggestions for setting the tip configuration. The pattern
