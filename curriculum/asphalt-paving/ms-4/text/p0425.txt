Mobile references. There are two types of mobile references: the

rigid tube or pipe and the floating beam reference (see Figure 8.21).

The purpose of the mobile reference is to average the effects of the

grade changes in an existing reference surface over a greater distance

than that obtained from just the wheelbase of the paver.

o The rigid tubular grade reference uses a rigid pipe that is 20, 30,
or 40 feet (6, 9, or 12 meters) long. The tube rides on the existing
pavement surface. A spring-loaded wire is stretched along the top
of the pipe. The grade sensor on the paver that sends the electronic
signal to the paver’s tow point rides on top of the wire. As the pipe
moves up and down over the existing grade, the stretched wire
on the skid will average the differences in the elevation that occur
under the mobile reference.
