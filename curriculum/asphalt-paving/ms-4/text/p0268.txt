4.9.4 Flexural Fatigue Test

The flexural fatigue test is conducted to evaluate the fatigue charac-
teristics of an HMA mixture. Fatigue cracking of pavement is considered
to be more a structural problem than simply a material problem. Several
external factors influence the fatigue cracking in pavements, such as poor
subgrade drainage, time of placement, and method of compaction and
placement of the asphalt mix.

The specimens for this test are 2.5 inches (62.5 millimeters) by 2 inches
(50 millimeters) by 15 inches (375 millimeters) beams. The test is conducted
in accordance to the procedures in AASHTO T 321. In this method, repeated
haversine loads (0.1 second load and 0.4 second unload) are applied at
the third points of the specimen. The beam fatigue test can be conducted
in controlled stress or controlled strain mode. A photo of the apparatus is
presented in Figure 4.33.
