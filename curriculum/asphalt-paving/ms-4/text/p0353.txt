7.2.5 Aggregate Dryer

An aggregate dryer is needed for both batch and drum-mixer plants.

With some styles of drum-mixers, the dryer is very long because it is also
used as a mixing drum to mix the liquid asphalt binder with the aggre-
gate to produce the final mix. Some drum-mixers are two piece units—the
dryer and the mixing device are separate units.

It is the responsibility of the dryer to produce mix with a consistent
temperature. Wide variations in temperature in the final mix affect place-
ment technique and in-place density. A temperature swing greater than
10°-20°F (6°-11°C) typically affects field performance. Aggregate moisture
that varies widely in the stockpiles can make meeting this directive diffi-
cult for the plant operator. This is one reason why managing the stockpile
area for moisture control is so thoroughly discussed.

Aggregate dryers and drum-mixers come in both counterflow configu-
rations (aggregate and hot gases traveling in opposing directions) and
parallel-flow configurations (aggregate and hot gases traveling in the same
direction). Batch plants have counterflow dryers. The newer drum-mixers
discussed in this chapter also utilize counterflow dryers.
