Force Ductility

Like the Elastic Recovery test, the Force Ductility test (as detailed in
AASHTO T 300), is a simple procedure based on the ductility test equipment.
In the test, a specimen is prepared as in the ductility procedure, except
that the side pieces are flat, rather than V-shaped.

The specimen preparation and test procedure is essentially identical to
the elastic recovery test. The main differences between the tests are the
following:

* A load cell is used to record the force required to pull the asphalt

binder specimen.

° The test is continued to 30 centimeters (see Figure 2.49) at which

point the test ends.

° Test temperature is usually 41°F (4°C).

° The asphalt binder is usually tested in the original (unaged) condition.
