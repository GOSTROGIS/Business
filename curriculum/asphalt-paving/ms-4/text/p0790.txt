Landfill caps. HMA can be used to protect the environment by
providing impermeable caps for abandoned landfills and pads for
storing hazardous or waste materials. These caps or pads typically
consist of hydraulic (impermeable) asphalt, paving fabric, and
aggregate base. Many caps are designed to support haul traffic.
Tacoma, Washington, covered three acres of its landfill for use as a
solid-waste processing station. The cap consisted of 12 inches (300
millimeters) of sand to cover the refuse, a high-density polyethyl-
ene membrane, and then 12 more inches (300 millimeters) of sand
to protect the membrane from construction equipment. An 8-inch
(200 millimeters) layer of dense-graded aggregate was placed over
the sand and surfaced with 3 inches (75 millimeters) of HMA.
