Spraying Temperatures

The lower temperatures are more critical for spraying asphalt cements
and cutback asphalts; therefore, only minimum temperatures are recom-
mended for these asphalt materials. The highest temperature used for
spraying asphalt cement should be that at which no fogging occurs when
the asphalt material leaves the spray nozzle.

For asphalt cements, typical minimum spraying temperatures range
from 266°F to 302°F (130°C to 150°C). For emulsified asphalts, typical
minimum spraying temperatures range from 68°F to 185°F (20°C to 85°C).
For cutback asphalts, typical minimum spraying temperatures range from
68°F to 230°F (20°C to 110°C). As noted previously, the selected applica-
tion temperature may, in some cases, be above the flash point of the mate-
rial. Caution must therefore be exercised to prevent fire or an explosion.
