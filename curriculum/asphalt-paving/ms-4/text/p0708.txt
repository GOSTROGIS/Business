13.7.5 Inspection

Prior to the start of any FDR project, the centerline of the existing road-
way should be referenced by offset markers along the edge of the roadway

outside the limits of construction. Such markers will facilitate the construc-

tion of the new pavement without the need for a survey layout crew.

During the construction process, the following should be checked:

° The treatment depth should be physically checked by removing the
reclaimed material on either side of the reclaimer.

¢ The gradation of the reclaimed material should be checked periodi-
cally. This generally consists of checking the maximum particle size of
the reclaimed layer. The sample should be taken from the full depth
to ensure that the reclaimer is treating the entire depth of the layer
being reclaimed.

° The yield rate of the emulsion or the foamed asphalt and additional
aggregate should be computed and compared with the mix design.
