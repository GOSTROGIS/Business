Steering a straight line is necessary for constructing a sound joint. When
the matching pass is laid, the overlap on top of the cold mat should be
Y-1 inch (12.5-25 millimeters). This overlap will be difficult to achieve if
the first pass is not straight. The screed operator can help maintain this
close tolerance by moving the screed in or out—if the paver is equipped
with a variable-width screed.

Controlling segregation on both outside edges of the screed can be
done with a proper setting of the material sensor controlling the flow of
material. Auger and tunnel extensions can also be used to reduce the seg-
regation of the material being moved outward by the augers. Observation
is the key. Any segregation observed along the outside edges of the screed
requires corrective action (see Figure 8.26).
