In addition to the FDR reclaimer, the FDR process requires the use of a
motor grader, to spread and fine-grade the reclaimed material, and com-

Multiple passes of the reclaimer over the pavement section are neces-
sary to ensure that the target depth is adequately pulverized and to pulver-
ize the entire roadway width. This results in a series of longitudinal joints
and some overlap between the passes. The minimum overlap width is
normally 4 inches (100 millimeter).

The gradation of the reclaimed material should be monitored to ensure
that the gradation being produced matches the mix design. The gradation
of the reclaimed material is influenced by these factors:

® Front- and rear-door opening of the pulverization/mixing chamber. The
more the door is closed, the longer the pulverized material is retained in
the pulverization/chamber, which allows the larger chunks to be broken
down.

° Position of the breaker bar setting in the pulverization chamber. The
closer the breaker bar is to the cutting drum, the finer the gradation
will be.

° The speed of the cutting drum. For thick lifts, a lower cutting drum speed
is used, and for thinner lifts, a faster-cutting drum speed is used.

° The forward speed of the reclaimer. The slower the forward speed, the
finer the gradation of the material will be.
