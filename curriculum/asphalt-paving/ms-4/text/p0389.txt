° Missing pugmill tips; inadequate mixing
¢ Automation error/inconsistency

Inadequate Coating

Check for the following:

° Too short wet-mix cycle time

° Missing pugmill tips

° Tip-to-liner clearance (too great)

* Inadequate drying of aggregate

° Dust slough/dry batch

° Temperature of liquid-asphalt binder too low

Temperature Fluctuations

Check for the following:

° Worn dryer flights (incapable of drying aggregates sufficiently at all
production rates)

* Moisture fluctuations in feed aggregates (burner/dryer cannot rapidly
adjust to rapidly adjusting moisture variations)

° Excessive storage time (mix cooling off from extended storage)

° Mix thermocouple malfunctioning/not reading correctly

¢ Mix thermocouple covered with material and/or not properly positioned
in material stream
