Polyethylene tubing used in agriculture and thin “fin-style” drains like
those used along highways do not have sufficient strength or capacity
to handle the outflow and should not be used for airfield underdrains.
Pipe underdrains, such as rigid corrugated PVC with smooth interior
walls, are recommended because of their strength, high flow capacity,
and configuration for easy maintenance.

Installation of the outlet pipe is critical to the edge-drainage system.
It is recommended that a metal or rigid (PVC) nonperforated pipe be used
for the outlet pipe because of its strength and capacity. A grade of at least
3 percent is recommended so that the pipe will continue to drain if there
is a slight variance of the pipe grade. Because the purpose of subsurface
drainage is to remove water as quickly as possible, it is recommended
that the outlet spacing be a maximum of 250 feet (75 meters). The edge-
drainage design should be coordinated with surface drainage to handle
removal of all sources of water.
