Performance-based specifications have not been used yet for HMA
pavements. Models predicting performance have been developed and are
currently being evaluated and validated. Many state highway agencies
have begun refining the calibration of the models to reflect their local
construction materials, specifications, and techniques.

The prediction models will work in tandem with the pavement design
because the design input parameters will be the same engineering prop-
erties that will be measured on the constructed pavement. If significant
differences are found in properties of the constructed pavement versus
the design, the models could be rerun and effects on the life-cycle costs
could be evaluated.
