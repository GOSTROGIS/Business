2./.2 Asphalt Sampling

To be meaningful, a sample of shipped asphalt must be truly representa-
tive, and it must be handled carefully so that it does not become contami-
nated or altered in any way before being tested. If the sample is not truly
representative, test results can be misleading.

Sampling Asphalt Binder from Vehicle Tanks

The best way to obtain a representative sample of asphalt from a vehicle
tank is to use a sampling valve in the truck tank. If the sampling valve is
used properly, representative samples can be obtained easily and quickly.

The procedure is a simple matter of opening the valve, allowing at least
1 quart (1 liter) of material to be drained off to remove prior material, and
then completely filling the sample container. Certain precautions must
be observed, however, to prevent contamination. Sample containers must
be new, clean, and dry. The filled sample container must be tightly and
positively sealed immediately after the sample is obtained. The sample
container should not be submerged in solvent or wiped with a solvent-
saturated cloth. Any spilled material on the outside of the container should
be wiped with a clean, dry cloth immediately after the container has been
sealed.

Because the asphalt in most cases is hot, safety precautions are manda-
tory at all times during sampling. Gloves should be worn, with sleeves
rolled down and fastened over the gloves while sampling and sealing con-
tainers. Face shields are also a must, and no smoking should be permitted.
