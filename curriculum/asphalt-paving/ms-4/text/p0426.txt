° Sonic trackers. The sonic-tracker mobile reference system mounts
a rigid beam on the side of the paver with four sensors mounted on
it (see Figure 8.22). The sensors direct sonic waves downward and
times the return echo to establish the height of the sensor above the
reference surface. The four height readings are continuously averaged
together to provide a reference line.

° Curb and gutter. When HMA pavement is being placed against a curb
and/or gutter, either the curb or the gutter can be used as a reference
for controlling the grade side of the screed. If the sensor is mounted just
ahead of the augers indexing off the curb or gutter, it will accurately
hold the depth of the HMA in relation to the curb or gutter.
