1. Although not a routine part of this design method, an effort is made to provide a minimum of

3. Hot mix asphalt bases that do not meet these criteria when tested at 140°F (60°C) are satisfactory if

the surface.

This recommendation applies only to regions having a range of climatic conditions similar to those
prevailing throughout most of the United States. A different lower test temperature may be considered in
regions having more extreme climatic conditions.
