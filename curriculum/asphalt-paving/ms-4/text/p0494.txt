Windrow Sampling

Windrow sampling (Figure 10.6) techniques are generally used for
aggregates, but they have also been used to obtain samples of HMA. Wind-
row samples can be used if “belly dump” haul units are used. These units
will create a windrow of HMA that can be sampled before placement into
the paving machine. The HMA can become segregated in the windrow,
so strict adherence to the sampling procedure is necessary to obtain
representative samples.

In-Place Pavement Sampling

Representative portions of HMA can be obtained by sampling behind the
paving machine. These samples are taken immediately after placement by
the paving machine but before any rolling. The use of plates (Figure 10.7)
placed on the grade in front of the paver is recommended except when thin
lifts are being placed.

Core Sampling
Core sampling is the most common form of sampling from completed HMA
pavements (Figure 10.8). Portable electric drills with wet-cut diamond-studded
core barrels provide rapid removal with little or no damage to the specimens.
These samples are normally used to evaluate compaction and thickness.
Once removed from the pavement, the test specimens must be handled with
care because they are very sensitive to damage from heat and point loads.
