8.2.4 Prime Coats and Tack Coats

Prime Coats

A prime coat is an application of a penetrating liquid asphalt (cutback,
specially designed emulsion, or other suitable material) applied with a dis-
tributor (see Figure 8.1) to a compacted aggregate base course. The func-
tion of a prime coat is to provide a protective layer to prevent the surface
of the aggregate base course from raveling under construction traffic. It
also provides a waterproof layer, preventing it from absorbing or losing
excess moisture before paving. A prime coat helps bond the top layer of
the granular base to the first layer of HMA pavement. Although this bond-
ing helps prevent slippage between the two layers, it is very weak and
should not be considered when overall strength of the pavement is ana-
lyzed. The prime coat is applied to an absorbent surface, such as a granular
base or prepared subgrade, with an asphalt distributor truck. A cutback
asphalt (such as MC-30, MC-70, or a specially designed emulsified asphalt)
is typically applied at a rate of 0.20-0.50 gallons per square yard (0.9-2.3
