pavement should be at least 4 inches (100 millimeters) thick. A surface patch
can be constructed without excavating the existing surface. The patch mate-
rial should be feather-edged to match the in-place grade lines. When this pro-
cedure is used, it is a good idea to outline the patch area with a small trench
produced by a pneumatic hammer. This will help tie the patch to the existing
pavement and reduce the possibility of raveling.

Surface patches can also be constructed by milling a portion of the pave-
ment (see Figure 12.9). This eliminates the need to feather-edge the patch
to avoid raveling. It also provides additional depth, which will accom-
modate mixes with larger-size aggregates, thus producing a more stable
patch. In all cases, the patch must match the elevation of the surrounding
pavement.

The patching procedure consists of marking the area to be patched,
making sure it extends 1 foot (0.3 meter) into good sound pavement.
