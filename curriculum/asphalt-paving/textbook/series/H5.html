Markers, grades and stakes

A stake is a compact instruction that depends on its location, legend and reference. Read the whole mark before acting. Protect survey control so the next crew inherits reliable information.

Learning step 1

Abbreviations used on stakes

Common abbreviations include BM or B/M—benchmark; C—cut; CB or C/B—catch basin; SMH—sanitary manhole; EL/Elv—elevation; FG—finished grade; PL—property line; RW—right of way; SAN or S—sanitary; Mon—monument; G—gas; PVC—polyvinyl chloride. Project legends can differ, so confirm the meaning and units. A gas mark identifies a potential utility concern; it does not give complete location, depth or clearance information. An elevation is a height relative to a stated datum, not a depth below the current surface.

Learning step 2

Interpret stakes

Read station, side, offset, target/reference mark, cut/fill, grade and slope together. Station 0+125 means 125 m along the defined alignment in this metric example. An offset stake sits away from the point it describes; preserve both the offset and direction. Property pins and survey monuments are protected control, not disposable construction stakes. A temporary benchmark may use an assumed datum, while other control is tied to a geodetic datum; do not combine them without an authorized relationship.

Cross-heads and reference marks can carry a level used to transfer information. Confirm which physical mark is the reference before measuring. Locate work such as catch basins, manholes and headwalls from the approved layout. Compare the milling limit and required profile with the marks before setting controls. If a stake is disturbed, illegible or conflicts with the plan, protect the evidence and request verification. An apparently small guess can propagate along an entire pass.

Worked example · Read an offset

A fictional stake says STA 0+125, 2.0 m RT OFFSET, FG EL 102.400, C 0.060. The legend defines cut from the marked reference to the required grade.

Think it through.

Locate station 125 m on the alignment and interpret the right-side offset using the project convention. The stated cut is 60 mm from the defined reference, not automatically from the top of the stake. FG is 102.400 m in the stated datum. Confirm the target location and mark before using the value.
