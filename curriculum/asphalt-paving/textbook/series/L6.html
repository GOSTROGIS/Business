Follow shut down procedures

A roller can move unintentionally after the shift if parking and securement are incomplete. Shutdown also prepares the machine for cleaning, staging, maintenance and transport.

Clean equipment
Ensure efficient staging

Learning step 1

Shut-down procedures

Choose the designated stable parking position with service access, clear emergency/public routes and suitable ground for checks. Follow the OMM stopping, cooling and shutdown sequence, apply parking controls and required chocks, and inspect safely for leaks or damage. Use the battery/night switch only as directed; it is not the whole lockout procedure. Record defects and the machine’s status.

Learning step 2

Clean equipment

Clean drums/tires, scrapers and spray equipment through the approved safe method. Control movement, heat, pressure and cleaning-product hazards, and contain waste. Never reach toward a rotating drum or articulation zone to remove pickup. Restore guards and account for tools before release.

Learning step 3

Ensure efficient staging

Coordinate staging with the foreperson so the next shift can access the roller without blocking other equipment, deliveries or public routes. Identify the machine, location, readiness and pending service. Efficient staging includes safe access for a machine that cannot restart.

Learning step 4

Prepare for transport

Prepare for transport using the actual OMM configuration and carrier plan. Assign a competent operator/guide and carrier responsibilities, verify mass/dimensions, ramp/support suitability and designated tie-down points. Use the applicable securement requirements and approved loading sequence. The transport driver’s role does not remove the need for clear machine-control responsibilities.

Learning step 5

Secure machine for safety

Secure controls, keys, detachable items and access panels; protect the public and prevent roll-away through the required parking/chocking arrangement. Consider residual heat and stored energy. Communicate out-of-service status visibly through the site system. Recheck the final state before leaving; a completed numerical checklist score cannot override a failed critical securement item.

Worked example · Ready for the next shift

A roller is clean and fuelled but parked where a delivery truck must reverse around it beside a public entrance.

Think it through.

Have the responsible lead arrange a safer approved staging location. Complete the movement and shutdown under the actual procedure, preserve access and communicate the final location/status. “Ready” includes safe placement, not only clean equipment.
