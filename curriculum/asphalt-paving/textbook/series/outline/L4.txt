      Line (GAC):      L  COMPACTOR OPERATION

      Competency:        L4   Perform Compaction Roller maintenance


       Objectives

      To be competent in this area, the individual must be able to perform regular daily maintenance on the
       compactor.

       Achievement Criteria
       Performance  The learner will perform regular daily maintenance on the compaction roller.

       Conditions    The learner will demonstrate all elements of daily maintenance.
         Criteria      The learner will demonstrate a minimum of 80% efficiency using a checklist.
