         Line (GAC):               I      ASPHALT PAVER OPERATION
         Competency:               I3     Apply paver work safety procedures


         Objectives
         To be competent in this area, the individual must be able to:
         •    Describe how to work safely while working on and around a paver. This will include understanding key
              regulations, machine limitations, operating procedures and tasks and the use of PPE’s.
         •    Comply with safety requirements at all times during this program.


         LEARNING TASKS                                                  CONTENT

         1.    Interpret job plan                                        •   Communicate job plan
                                                                         •   Path
                                                                         •   Pattern
                                                                         •   Asphalt thickness
                                                                         •   Crown
                                                                         •   Obstacles

                                                                                 malfunctions/kickbacks) blind spots,
                                                                                 fumes, hydraulic lines, noise
                                                                             •   Block outs
                                                                             •   Pinch point hazard

                                                                             purpose gloves, safety footwear, hard hat -

                                                                         •   Battery lockout, etc.
