      Line (GAC):      L  COMPACTOR OPERATION

      Competency:        L1   Describe compaction equipment


       Objectives

      To be competent in this area, the individual must be able to:
        •   Describe the functions of the compactor and the drum vs. wheel processes of compaction.
        •   Describe the functions of the compactor and the vibratory vs. static processes of compaction.
        •   Describe the functions of each phase of a compaction train.
