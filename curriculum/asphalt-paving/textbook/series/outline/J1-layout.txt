         Line (GAC):               J      ASPHALT SCREED OPERATION
         Competency:               J1     Describe paving screed and attachments


         Objectives
         To be competent in this area, the individual must be able to describe the functions of the screed and the
         attachments used for the screed process.


         LEARNING TASKS                                               CONTENT

         1.    Describe screed categories                             •     Rigid
                                                                      •     Variable

         2.    Describe common attachments                            •     Extension, hydraulic wings, automatic grade
                                                                            controls (laser)
                                                                      •     String line vs. boom vs. ski boom
                                                                      •     Cut off shoe to narrow down joint, curb form

         3.    Describe principal functions                           •     Basic mix thickness control
                                                                      •     How material gets to screed
                                                                      •     Attachments
                                                                      •     Different circumstances that require
                                                                            adjustments
                                                                      •     Timing
