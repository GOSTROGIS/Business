      Line (GAC):         I   ASPHALT PAVER OPERATION

      Competency:          I9    Transport paver


       Objectives

      To be competent in this area, the individual must be able to understand the correct transport procedures for
        the paving machine.


      LEARNING TASKS                         CONTENT
         1.    Describe compliance with wide load regulations     •   Signage
                                                               •   Pilot car if applicable
                                                               •  Removing extension

         2.    Describe roles and responsibilities                  •  Truck driver is usually responsible for
                                                                      transporting equipment

         3.    Describe how to prepare machine for transport      •  Remove attachments
                                                               •   Secure equipment
                                                               •  Ensure all guards are in place

         4.    Describe loading and unloading procedures         •   Operator is not usually involved in
                                                                 loading/unloading
                                                               •  Use of tie-downs
                                                               •   Safe movement of maching on/off trailer


       Achievement Criteria
       Performance  The learner will demonstrate safe practices while preparing the asphalt paver for transport.
                      Learner will demonstrate safe practices during practical training and on the job.
       Conditions    The learner will prepare the machine for transport using appropriate tools and equipment to
                     manufactures specification.
         Criteria      The learner will be able to secure and prepare a machine for transport within a given time
                       frame.
