         Line (GAC):               L      COMPACTOR OPERATION
         Competency:               L2     Apply compaction work safety procedures


         Objectives
         To be competent in this area, the individual must be able to:
         •    Describe how to work safely while working on and around a compactor. This will include understanding
              key regulations, machine limitations, operating procedures and tasks and the use of PPEs.
         •    Comply with safety requirements at all times during this course.
