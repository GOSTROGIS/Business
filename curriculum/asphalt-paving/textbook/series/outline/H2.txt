      Line (GAC):    H  OPERATE MILLING AND RECLAIMING EQUIPMENT

      Competency:       H2   Identify milling and reclaiming safety procedures


       Objectives

      To be competent in this area, the individual must be able to:
        •   Describe how to work safely while working on and around a milling machine. This will include
           understanding key regulations, machine limitations, operating procedures and tasks and the use of
            PPE’s.
        •  Comply with safety requirements at all times during this course.
