Use this volume

This volume provides complete classroom readings, worked examples, named practice tasks and answer guidance for screed control and handwork. Follow the learning route in each chapter, study the labelled figures and explain your reasoning. The separate answer book contains the worked keys and observation records.

Care for people, place and the next person using the work runs through the cases. Local First Nations, Métis and Inuit learners and partners bring distinct knowledge and priorities. Participation does not require sharing personal stories or cultural teachings, and no diagram claims to represent a Nation’s protocol.

Classroom authoring is complete for the listed competencies. Qualified technical review, actual fleet/site matching, a local teaching pilot and observed practical qualification are separate requirements. The practical records are ready for an instructor to configure and use; they do not certify unobserved work.

Keep the term with its meaning

Aggregate
Mineral particles forming the solid skeleton of the mixture or granular layer.
Binder
The asphalt material that coats and binds the aggregate in the specified mixture.
Compacted thickness
Layer thickness after the required compaction process; distinct from loose depth behind the screed.
Density reference
The specified comparison value used as the denominator of a percent-density calculation.
Gradation
Distribution of aggregate particle sizes, commonly expressed as cumulative percent passing specified sieves.
Head of material
The quantity/distribution of mix immediately in front of the screed.
Job mix formula (JMF)
The accepted mixture proportions and properties under the applicable quality/contract process.
Lockout
Isolation, securing and verification of hazardous energy for work requiring protection.
Lot / sub-lot
Defined portions of work used for sampling, testing and acceptance under the contract.
OMM
Operation and maintenance manual matched to the actual machine, serial range and configuration.
RAP
Reclaimed asphalt pavement: recovered material containing aggregate and aged binder.
SDS
Safety data sheet for the actual product; read with its label and workplace procedure.
Screed
The paver assembly that forms the mat; its response depends on setup, material and movement.
Station / offset
Location along an alignment / distance and direction from a defined reference.
TCP
Traffic control person with the required training and assigned role.
Traceability
A record connecting material, location, time, sample, result and authorized decision.
SVG
Scalable vector graphics: useful for clear diagrams whose labels and geometry must be checked.
DOCX / XLSX
Editable document / spreadsheet workbook; verify actual content and formulas after creation.
PDF / HTML
Page-based reading copy / browser document; inspect completeness, access and linked resources.
MP3 / MP4 / VTT
Audio file / video container / timed web captions; test playback and retain a transcript.

Paving screed and attachments

Worked example · An extension line

A visible line follows the inner edge of an extension while the rest of the mat looks consistent.

Record side, station and conditions. Check the relevant extension setup, plate condition, heating and material distribution through the authorized procedure. A line is a clue; do not assume one control or universal adjustment will fix every model.

Identify tow arm, main plate, extension, end gate and grade sensor on the supplied figures.

Identify tow arm, main plate, extension, end gate and grade sensor on the supplied figures.

Answer guidance.

Tow arm transmits the pull; plate forms the mat; extension changes width as designed; end gate contains/shapes the edge; grade sensor follows its reference. Verify actual labels and model.

Observe: Reading, Communication through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

A fictional main width is 2.4 m with 0.6 m extension on each side

A fictional main width is 2.4 m with 0.6 m extension on each side. Find total width.

Answer guidance.

2.4 + 0.6 + 0.6 = 3.6 m. This arithmetic does not establish compatible attachments or permissible operating width.

Observe: Numeracy, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Create a checked SVG concept map of the factors affecting a floating screed, using AI if available.

Create a checked SVG concept map of the factors affecting a floating screed, using AI if available.

Answer guidance.

Include speed, head of material, tow-point/angle, mixture and screed condition with clearly labelled conceptual connections. Check against the supplied MS-4/CAT explanation, credit sources and omit invented settings.

Screed work safety procedures

Worked example · Width changes beside a walkway

The plan calls for widening the mat, but the extension would enter a protected walking route.

Hold the extension movement, communicate the conflict and have the responsible lead resolve the work/access arrangement. Brief the change with traffic control and the crew before moving. Do not rely on a shouted warning as the only control.

Name three screed hazards that remain after travel stops.

Name three screed hazards that remain after travel stops.

Answer guidance.

Residual heat, stored hydraulic pressure and gravity/unsupported components; electrical/heater energy or automatic functions may also remain. Use the actual energy assessment.

Observe: Reading, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

A 3.0 m width is widened by 0.45 m on each side

A 3.0 m width is widened by 0.45 m on each side. What new width must the crew plan for?

Answer guidance.

3.90 m, plus the actual machine/people movement envelope and required controls. The dimension alone does not define a safe route.

Observe: Numeracy through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Rehearse a stop message when a worker approaches the extension zone.

Rehearse a stop message when a worker approaches the extension zone.

Answer guidance.

Identify the machine/side and stop request clearly, obtain acknowledgement and maintain safe positions. Resume only after the area and plan are confirmed.

Observe: Communication, Collaboration, Adaptability through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Asphalt screed operator roles and responsibilities

Worked example · One controlled change

A mat check is low after a speed change. One person wants more head, another wants a tow-point change and a third wants a different speed.

Pause the conflicting instructions, record the observation and changed condition, and have the responsible lead coordinate a controlled response. Recheck after the relevant response distance. Changing everything at once prevents a useful diagnosis.

A 40 m × 3.5 m area at 0.06 m loose thickness has what geometric loose volume

A 40 m × 3.5 m area at 0.06 m loose thickness has what geometric loose volume?

Answer guidance.

40 × 3.5 × 0.06 = 8.4 m³. It is not a compacted volume or tonnage without the appropriate additional information.

Observe: Numeracy, Reading through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Write a message reporting a low right-side loose-depth reading.

Write a message reporting a low right-side loose-depth reading.

Answer guidance.

State station, right side, measured depth/unit, target and observed condition; ask the responsible operator to confirm the next action. Avoid “make it thicker” without location or evidence.

Observe: Writing, Communication through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Use a shared change log for one setup correction

Use a shared change log for one setup correction. What fields make it useful?

Answer guidance.

Time/station, original condition, measurement, authorized change, operator, response distance/time, new measurement and decision. AI may format the log, but the crew supplies and verifies actual observations.

Observe: Digital, Collaboration, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Screed maintenance

Worked example · A recurring streak

A streak returns along the same line after surface buildup is removed.

Record its position and persistence. Have authorized personnel inspect plate wear/flatness, extension alignment, heating and relevant setup under safe conditions. Do not continue scraping a moving or hot hazard area or guess a replacement tolerance.

A fictional wear check shows 8.0 mm initially and 6.5 mm now

A fictional wear check shows 8.0 mm initially and 6.5 mm now. Find the loss. Does that establish replacement?

Answer guidance.

Loss = 1.5 mm. Replacement depends on the actual manufacturer’s limit, measurement method and condition; the arithmetic alone is insufficient.

Observe: Numeracy, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Identify which maintenance items belong to the actual screed and which to shared tractor systems.

Identify which maintenance items belong to the actual screed and which to shared tractor systems.

Answer guidance.

Use the model diagram and schedule. Screed plates, extensions and thickness screws are screed items; engine radiator/battery may be shared tractor items. Record fitted options and do not invent absent components.

Observe: Reading, Writing through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Draft a safe cleaning handover between two operators.

Draft a safe cleaning handover between two operators.

Answer guidance.

State machine/screed, isolated functions, heat condition, cleaning area/product, people involved, required supports and the person coordinating reinstatement after tools/guards are checked.

Observe: Communication, Collaboration through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Operate asphalt screed

Worked example · Loose depth and final thickness

A verified trial relationship is loose/compacted = 1.25. Target compacted thickness is 40 mm. A loose-depth check reads 50 mm.

Estimated loose target = 40 × 1.25 = 50 mm, so the reading matches that exercise target. Final compacted thickness still requires the specified verification; one loose reading does not establish the whole mat’s acceptance.

For the same fictional factor, estimate loose thickness for a 48 mm compacted target.

For the same fictional factor, estimate loose thickness for a 48 mm compacted target.

Answer guidance.

48 × 1.25 = 60 mm. Confirm through the approved setup and actual checks; the factor is not universal.

Observe: Numeracy, Reading through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Explain why repeated immediate adjustments after each probe reading can be unhelpful.

Explain why repeated immediate adjustments after each probe reading can be unhelpful.

Answer guidance.

The screed response develops over travel distance. Repeated changes before the response is measured can create oscillation and obscure the cause. Use coordinated, authorized changes and location-based feedback.

Observe: Problem solving, Communication through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Record a supervised setup and two subsequent checks in a digital table.

Record a supervised setup and two subsequent checks in a digital table.

Answer guidance.

Include model, reference, target/factor source, support/heating readiness, station/side, readings, authorized adjustment and result. Distinguish actual observations from planned settings.

Observe: Digital, Writing, Collaboration through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Follow shut down procedures

Worked example · Cleaning is delayed

The screed remains too hot for the approved cleaning product and procedure when the scheduled shift ends.

Maintain the safe shutdown state and coordinate the required cooling and cleaning arrangements. Do not spray early to meet the schedule. Record the outstanding task and responsible person so the next shift does not assume cleaning is complete.

Explain three checks before cleaning starts.

Explain three checks before cleaning starts.

Answer guidance.

Actual heat condition/product compatibility; required isolation/support of shared systems; controlled area, PPE and waste containment. Confirm the complete machine procedure.

Observe: Reading, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

An assessment has 20 possible points and earns 15

An assessment has 20 possible points and earns 15. What percentage is that, and what follows?

Answer guidance.

75%, below the outline’s 80% threshold for this checklist task. Give specific feedback and repeat the required practice; all critical safety items must also pass.

Observe: Numeracy through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Write a handover stating that cleaning remains incomplete.

Write a handover stating that cleaning remains incomplete.

Answer guidance.

Identify machine/screed, safe parked/isolated state, reason cleaning is held, residual hazards, required next action and responsible person. Do not mark the task complete.

Observe: Writing, Communication, Collaboration through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Raker roles and responsibilities

Worked example · The same hollow returns

A small low area appears repeatedly behind the same screed location. The raker has filled it twice.

Report the repeated location and timing to the screed/quality lead. Complete only authorized hand correction while the underlying cause is investigated. Repeated filling without diagnosis can change texture and compaction and hide a continuing setup issue.

Choose a lute, shovel, tamper and wheelbarrow for four distinct purposes.

Choose a lute, shovel, tamper and wheelbarrow for four distinct purposes.

Answer guidance.

Lute distributes/finishes as appropriate; shovel transfers/places mix; tamper compacts suitable confined areas through the approved method; wheelbarrow moves manageable quantities on an approved route. Tool choice depends on the task and procedure.

Observe: Reading, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

A rectangular small area is 2.4 m × 1.5 m

A rectangular small area is 2.4 m × 1.5 m. Find area for the work plan.

Answer guidance.

3.6 m². Thickness and suitable density are still needed for volume/mass; do not order from area alone.

Observe: Numeracy through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Prepare a photographed or SVG tool-identification card with AI assistance if available.

Answer guidance.

Show the actual tool, head/edge, handle and attachment/brace; explain its purpose and inspection points. Verify labels against the supplied manufacturer information and credit the image.

Raking safety procedures

Worked example · A handle reaches into the lane

A worker turns with a long lute while watching the mat. Its handle crosses the work-zone boundary.

Stop and reposition through the safe route. Review the work space, tool orientation and traffic boundary with the lead. A safe stance must account for the whole tool, not only the worker’s feet.

Identify three ergonomic improvements for shovelling a small patch.

Identify three ergonomic improvements for shovelling a small patch.

Answer guidance.

Use a suitable tool and manageable load; maintain stable footing and turn with the feet; position material to reduce reach and coordinate task variation/rest. Match the actual task and access needs.

Observe: Problem solving, Reading through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

A practice plan has 40 min of handwork split into four equal periods

A practice plan has 40 min of handwork split into four equal periods. How long is each period? Does that prescribe a heat-rest schedule?

Answer guidance.

10 min each. No; the actual heat/work-rest plan depends on assessment and conditions, not this arithmetic example.

Observe: Numeracy through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Place asphalt hot mix

Worked example · A small rectangular patch

A prepared patch is 1.2 m × 0.8 m with 0.06 m compacted depth.

Area = 0.96 m²; compacted volume = 0.0576 m³. Material quantity requires an appropriate density and justified allowance. Confirm the repair detail and compaction method; the volume calculation does not establish that a single lift or any particular tool is suitable.

For a 2 m × 0.5 m strip at 0.04 m compacted depth, find geometric volume.

For a 2 m × 0.5 m strip at 0.04 m compacted depth, find geometric volume.

Answer guidance.

2 × 0.5 × 0.04 = 0.04 m³ compacted. Use the specified material and approved quantity conversion for ordering.

Observe: Numeracy, Reading through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Explain a safe handwork sequence around a manhole.

Explain a safe handwork sequence around a manhole.

Answer guidance.

Verify structure elevation/detail and protected access; prepare support/face treatment; place/distribute suitable mix; check line/crossfall; coordinate compaction and inspect drainage/finish. Use the actual approved method and keep clear of moving equipment.

Observe: Communication, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Document a supervised practice using a shovel, lute and tamper, then review with a partner.

Document a supervised practice using a shovel, lute and tamper, then review with a partner.

Answer guidance.

Record task/site conditions, tools, safe transfer, selected lute edge, placement/transition/joint work, compaction coordination and observed result. Identify actual feedback and a next attempt without claiming unobserved workplace competence.

Integrated practice · all nine Skills for Success

Prepare a small-area finishing plan

This is a complete fictional classroom case. It does not describe an actual community, Nation, employer or project. Learners may use the supplied case without disclosing personal or cultural knowledge.

A fictional rectangular repair is 2.5 m × 1.2 m with a 0.05 m compacted depth. The structure elevation and joint treatment are supplied by an approved drawing, but no mixture density or universal compaction allowance is given. A draft proposes heating any cooled mix with a torch and using the same loose-thickness factor on every job.

Reading · Check the facts

Identify the controlling facts, missing information and one unsupported statement in the brief.

Evidence and feedback: Use the exact case facts; distinguish observation, assumption and missing authority.

Writing · Make the handover useful

Draft a concise handover with location, quantities, status and the next responsible action.

Evidence and feedback: The note preserves verified facts, units and unresolved holds without inventing a release.

Numeracy · Calculate and check

Show the case calculation with units and an independent reasonableness check.

Evidence and feedback: Use the worked numerical key below; show the relationship and its practical limits.

Digital · Direct AI and verify the files

Use an available AI assistant to draft a findings table and editable record from this brief. Request DOCX or a structured document, XLSX with formulas where useful, and a PDF/HTML reading copy. Audit every claim and reopen the actual outputs.

Evidence and feedback: Record the product/capabilities, prompt, source facts, saved files and checks. A chat response alone is not a created or verified file. Check formulas and preserve originals.

Problem solving · Investigate the discrepancy

Explain the discrepancy, what evidence is needed and who should authorize the next step.

Evidence and feedback: Separate the observed issue from a guessed cause; preserve evidence and identify the correct decision role.

Communication · Send and check the message

Brief a partner, ask for a check-back and repair one misunderstanding if it occurs.

Evidence and feedback: Observe an actual exchange and confirmation of the essential facts; do not infer communication from a shared file.

Collaboration · Review together

Agree reviewer/author roles, complete a review and record each person’s contribution.

Evidence and feedback: Both learners take an identifiable turn; feedback is respectful and tied to the case evidence.

Adaptability · Respond to the change

Apply the change card, identify what changes and what stays, and revise the record.
