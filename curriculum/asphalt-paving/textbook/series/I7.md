# Operate asphalt paver

Paver operation is learned through observed, supervised control of the actual machine. In-school practical work in the outline is on flat surfaces; workplace performance adds the approved grades, slopes and conditions under competent supervision.

## Standard moves

Complete pre-start checks and daily maintenance, identify controls and warnings, and enter using the designated access and three-point contact. Follow the OMM start and travel sequence. Practise forward–stop–reverse–stop on the cleared flat training area, checking surroundings before each movement. Apply the specified parking and transmission controls. Transmission neutral or a control lock is not a substitute for maintenance lockout.

## Maneuvering the paver

Manoeuvre smoothly within the machine’s operating limits and the instructor’s plan. Maintain awareness of space, position, clearance and ground support. In-school learners stay on the approved flat area. Hills, slopes and varied conditions require a separate workplace plan, machine limits and supervised assessment; do not infer a safe slope from a generic diagram. Stop when visibility or support is uncertain.

## Position paver

Position the paver for different starting scenarios, confirming line, width, joint, truck approach and screed readiness. Coordinate hopper opening/closing, material-flow settings and truck guidance through the actual controls and agreed signals. Observe the whole movement envelope. The instructor sets time and quantity appropriate to conditions; speed is not rewarded at the expense of control.

## Simulate paving laying down a mat using 5mm crush and sand mixture or similar compact quality material or asphalt

For a simulated mat, use only an instructor- and manufacturer-approved training material and method; a sand/crushed-material exercise does not reproduce every hot-mix behaviour. For asphalt, follow the approved mixture, temperature, placement and rolling plan. Maintain the line and steady process, communicate with screed and ground crew, monitor performance, and clean/shut down using the safe procedure. Record classroom and workplace observations separately.

## Worked example: Flat practice is a specific achievement

A learner completes a controlled flat-pad pass with simulated material. The next job involves a steep access road and hot mix.

Record the demonstrated flat-pad skills. Plan the additional machine/site-specific preparation and supervised workplace assessment for grades, slopes and hot-mix conditions. Do not label the first exercise as proof of unrestricted operation.
