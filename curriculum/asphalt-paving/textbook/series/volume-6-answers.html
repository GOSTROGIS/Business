Use this volume

This volume provides complete classroom readings, worked examples, named practice tasks and answer guidance for paver operation. Follow the learning route in each chapter, study the labelled figures and explain your reasoning. The separate answer book contains the worked keys and observation records.

Care for people, place and the next person using the work runs through the cases. Local First Nations, Métis and Inuit learners and partners bring distinct knowledge and priorities. Participation does not require sharing personal stories or cultural teachings, and no diagram claims to represent a Nation’s protocol.

Classroom authoring is complete for the listed competencies. Qualified technical review, actual fleet/site matching, a local teaching pilot and observed practical qualification are separate requirements. The practical records are ready for an instructor to configure and use; they do not certify unobserved work.

Keep the term with its meaning

Aggregate
Mineral particles forming the solid skeleton of the mixture or granular layer.
Binder
The asphalt material that coats and binds the aggregate in the specified mixture.
Compacted thickness
Layer thickness after the required compaction process; distinct from loose depth behind the screed.
Density reference
The specified comparison value used as the denominator of a percent-density calculation.
Gradation
Distribution of aggregate particle sizes, commonly expressed as cumulative percent passing specified sieves.
Head of material
The quantity/distribution of mix immediately in front of the screed.
Job mix formula (JMF)
The accepted mixture proportions and properties under the applicable quality/contract process.
Lockout
Isolation, securing and verification of hazardous energy for work requiring protection.
Lot / sub-lot
Defined portions of work used for sampling, testing and acceptance under the contract.
OMM
Operation and maintenance manual matched to the actual machine, serial range and configuration.
RAP
Reclaimed asphalt pavement: recovered material containing aggregate and aged binder.
SDS
Safety data sheet for the actual product; read with its label and workplace procedure.
Screed
The paver assembly that forms the mat; its response depends on setup, material and movement.
Station / offset
Location along an alignment / distance and direction from a defined reference.
TCP
Traffic control person with the required training and assigned role.
Traceability
A record connecting material, location, time, sample, result and authorized decision.
SVG
Scalable vector graphics: useful for clear diagrams whose labels and geometry must be checked.
DOCX / XLSX
Editable document / spreadsheet workbook; verify actual content and formulas after creation.
PDF / HTML
Page-based reading copy / browser document; inspect completeness, access and linked resources.
MP3 / MP4 / VTT
Audio file / video container / timed web captions; test playback and retain a transcript.

Asphalt placement process

Worked example · Convert a trial thickness relationship

A fictional trial produced 50 mm compacted thickness from 62 mm loose thickness. The same approved trial relationship is used to estimate a 45 mm compacted target.

Loose/compacted factor = 62/50 = 1.24. Estimated loose thickness = 45 × 1.24 = 55.8 mm. This is an exercise estimate tied to the stated trial, not a universal setting; confirm with actual placement and compaction checks.

Explain and apply the idea

Put surface acceptance, treatment readiness, paver setup, placement and rolling in a useful sequence.

Answer guidance.

Accept and prepare the surface; apply/verify the specified treatment; inspect and set the paver/screed; place consistently; compact through the approved rolling plan. Communication and quality checks continue throughout.

Observe: Reading, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

A rectangular 50 m × 3 m mat has 0.05 m compacted thickness

A rectangular 50 m × 3 m mat has 0.05 m compacted thickness. Find compacted volume.

Answer guidance.

50 × 3 × 0.05 = 7.5 m³. Tonnage requires an appropriate density; loose thickness and delivered mass are different quantities.

Observe: Numeracy through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Write a call to the roller lead when delivery is interrupted.

Write a call to the roller lead when delivery is interrupted.

Answer guidance.

State paver location, expected interruption, placed area needing attention, known temperature/quality information and proposed coordination. Confirm the rolling plan with the lead rather than inventing new pass counts.

Observe: Communication, Collaboration, Adaptability through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Convert nominal 8 ft and 10 ft widths to metres

Convert nominal 8 ft and 10 ft widths to metres. Do these establish the actual screed settings?

Answer guidance.

8 × 0.3048 = 2.4384 m; 10 × 0.3048 = 3.048 m. These conversions do not establish a particular machine’s working width or setting.

Asphalt paver operator roles and responsibilities

Worked example · Hopper level is not the whole story

The hopper contains mix, but the right side of the screed has a visibly reduced material head and texture changes.

Report the side and location, coordinate with the screed operator and inspect the feed-system status from safe positions. A full hopper does not prove uniform delivery across the screed. Use authorized adjustments and record the response; do not reach into the feed system.

Explain the difference between hopper contents and head of material.

Explain the difference between hopper contents and head of material.

Answer guidance.

The hopper receives/stores incoming mix; conveyors and augers deliver/distribute it to the screed. Head refers to the material in front of the screed. One does not guarantee the other is consistent.

Observe: Reading, Communication through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

A 20 t load lasts 8 min at a steady fictional rate

A 20 t load lasts 8 min at a steady fictional rate. Find t/min; why is it not a universal paver speed?

Answer guidance.

20/8 = 2.5 t/min. Travel speed also depends on width, thickness, density, supply and the approved process; mass rate alone is insufficient.

Observe: Numeracy, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Draft a truck-change handover that includes an access constraint.

Draft a truck-change handover that includes an access constraint.

Answer guidance.

Identify machine/lane, designated guide, approach/stop point, protected access, overhead concern, communication channel and lost-signal response. Confirm read-back before movement.

Observe: Writing, Collaboration, Adaptability through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Paving machine maintenance

Worked example · A repeated low level

Hydraulic oil is low again after yesterday’s top-up. A fresh wet area is visible beneath the machine.

Record the repeated loss and safe visual observation, secure/hold the machine as required and notify the responsible person. Do not treat repeated top-up as a repair or touch the suspected leak. Confirm authorized correction before release.

A fictional service interval is 10 operating hours

Answer guidance.

9.5 h elapsed; 0.5 h remains under this exercise interval. Calendar, condition and other schedule requirements may make service due sooner.

Markers, grades and stakes

Worked example · Offset versus edge

A stake marked 1.5 m RT OFFSET describes the approved right paving edge at station 0+060. A learner treats the stake itself as the edge.

Use the project legend and stated offset direction to locate the actual edge; the stake is a reference away from it. Verify the relation with the grade lead before setting the paver line. Do not shift the whole mat to the stake.

At one verified point, base EL is 101.250 m and finished EL is 101.300 m

At one verified point, base EL is 101.250 m and finished EL is 101.300 m. Find the vertical difference.

Answer guidance.

0.050 m = 50 mm. It is the specified geometric difference at that point, not automatically the loose screed setting.

Observe: Numeracy, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Write a read-back containing station, side, offset and unresolved datum.

Write a read-back containing station, side, offset and unresolved datum.

Answer guidance.

Example: “Station 0+060, right-side offset stake, marked 1.5 m. I need confirmation of the reference direction and datum before using its elevation.” Include the actual mark and ask the responsible person to confirm.

Observe: Writing, Communication through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

It can draft definitions, which must be checked against the project legend. It cannot establish actual utility depth, property boundary, datum relationship or safe machine clearance from abbreviations alone.

Follow shut down procedures

Worked example · An incomplete handover

The paver is parked correctly, but the operator noticed a leak and left without recording it.

The shutdown/handover is incomplete. Record the leak observation, location and machine status, notify the responsible person and arrange the required hold/inspection. The next operator needs the information before starting.

Explain shutdown versus lockout.

Explain shutdown versus lockout.

Answer guidance.

Shutdown ends operation in the prescribed state. Lockout isolates, secures and verifies hazardous energy for work requiring protection. A shutdown may be part of lockout but is not the whole process.

Keep delivery and mat records consistent

This is a complete fictional classroom case. It does not describe an actual community, Nation, employer or project. Learners may use the supplied case without disclosing personal or cultural knowledge.

A fictional approved area is 50 m × 3.2 m with a 45 mm compacted target. A verified trial relationship is loose/compacted = 1.24 for this exercise. The delivery register contains ticket 81 for 12 t and ticket 82 for 11 t, but a draft totals 35 t by counting ticket 81 twice. No density for estimating required mass is supplied.

Evidence and feedback: Use the exact case facts; distinguish observation, assumption and missing authority.

Writing · Make the handover useful

Evidence and feedback: The note preserves verified facts, units and unresolved holds without inventing a release.
