## Assess the integrated brief fairly

Use **Not observed**, **Not yet demonstrated**, **Demonstrated with support** and **Demonstrated independently** for the task under the observed conditions. Record the support and next practice. These local descriptions are not official Skills for Success levels or a trade qualification.

|---|---|

| Writing | Produces a clear brief with named responsibilities and unresolved items. |
| Numeracy | Retains the checked P-01 quantity/revision and distinguishes angle conventions; does not invent a lifting capacity. |
| Digital | Saves, exports or organizes the brief and reopens the correct version. |

**Numeracy inclusion for the integrated brief:** attach the checked Chapter 5 quantity/revision sheet and the marked angle-convention diagram from TB7-09. This keeps a visible individual numeracy product with the brief. It does not add a lift calculation.

For delivery, divide Chapter 7 into supported sessions with time for reading, discussion and individual attempts. Use pictures or isolated teaching samples for recognition exercises. Schedule qualified instruction and authorized practical observation separately. Before teaching, verify current legislation, actual venue emergency arrangements, the fleet/manual match and any product-specific SDS. Technical approval and classroom pilot remain open in the delivery record.
