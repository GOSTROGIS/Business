Unit 6 · Answer and teaching guidance

Use 90 minutes: claim reading 15, material/lightweight-fill discussion 15, cost calculation 10, supplier enquiry 10, option table 20, changed condition 10 and brief/check 10. Supply N1–N8 and the retained public-source excerpts. Learners need no product samples, site data or external accounts. Distinguish an evidence-supported mechanism from a project performance prediction.

Task Expected evidence
LA6-01 Paving-stone permeability depends on the complete system; porous asphalt with a reservoir still needs site/drainage design and maintenance; recycling alone cannot establish total impact. Each revision names a condition and a question rather than simply adding “may” to a vague claim.
LA6-02 $1,145,000 − $633,000 = $512,000. Limits include historical 2016/2017 context, California/U.S. geography/currency context, project-specific quantities/design, missing installation/overhead/profit and no current B.C. supplier quotes. Technical suitability depends on site/design requirements, not the cheapest listed total. The example concerns lightweight fill; that role cannot be relabelled as approved pavement sub-base without design and material evidence.
LA6-03 Questions identify actual product/system, intended use, relevant tested performance, maintenance, compatibility and approval. Pattern questions include access/visibility and permission for any protected imagery. No universal epoxy, colour or pattern performance is accepted without evidence.
LA6-04 Porous asphalt relates to water management, subject to site/loading/drainage/maintenance study. Coloured asphalt may relate to wayfinding; it does not answer infiltration or structural needs by colour alone. EPS geofoam relates to embankment/load conditions that have not been established for this courtyard. Accept different investigation shortlists when evidence and limits are explicit. Appropriate review may include qualified pavement/civil/geotechnical/design professionals and the facility's maintenance decision-maker.
LA6-05 Lack of a specialized-maintenance commitment is relevant to clogging/maintenance risk in the porous-asphalt reference. A reasonable revision makes maintenance feasibility a condition for investigation or postpones that option. It does not establish an alternative's suitability.
LA6-06 The brief recommends an investigation, with a known mechanism, a case-specific unknown, source date and next decision. Reader can distinguish a recommendation to study from permission to construct. Record actual file actions separately.

Model brief: “For the fictional 600 m² courtyard, investigate whether a porous-asphalt/stone-reservoir system could meet the surface-water and loading requirements. FHWA's2015 brief describes the mechanism and identifies clogging and specialized maintenance as considerations. We have no infiltration/geotechnical results or maintenance commitment, so we cannot recommend construction. First obtain site/design review and a feasible maintenance plan. Evaluate wayfinding separately: colour may help a communication purpose but does not establish drainage performance. Keep environmental comparisons within a defined life-cycle boundary and use project-specific information before ranking options.”

Occupational learning-task evidence

Learning task Activities Locally authored criterion
A6.1 · Green roads: paving stones, porous asphalt, recycling LA6-01, LA6-04 Explain three different mechanisms and conditions; avoid universal “green” claims
A6.2 · Epoxy/coloured/modified materials LA6-03, LA6-04 Distinguish product families and request performance/application evidence
A6.3 · New sub-bases/pumice LA6-02 Identify pumice in a bounded historical lightweight-fill comparison and state suitability/data limits
A6.4 · Floating roads/foam LA6-02, LA6-04 Explain lightweight-fill purpose and EPS term without implying flotation or generic-foam substitution
A6.5 · Added-value enhancements/street print LA6-03, LA6-06 Separate visual purpose, technical evidence and permission

A qualified reviewer must check the material descriptions, local suitability questions, dated-reference use and terminology before technical delivery. These sources are evidence for mechanisms and questions, not current B.C. design approval. A retry can use an unfamiliar marketing claim with a different missing condition. Ask learners to identify what evidence would change their conclusion.
