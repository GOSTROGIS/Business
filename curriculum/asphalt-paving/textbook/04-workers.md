## Connect the job title to the work

| Outline job term | Broad function to explain | Information another role needs |
|---|---|---|
| Raker | Supports hand placement and finishing | Where the agreed handwork is needed |
| Screed | Forms the placed mat as part of the paver system; the screed role attends to this work | The applicable placement requirements and changes |
| Asphalt Paver | Places the supplied mixture through the machine system | Delivery status and the approved work arrangement |
| Compaction Roller | Compacts the placed material under the assigned procedure | Placement status and the applicable compaction requirements |
| Milling machine | Removes or profiles existing pavement | The authorized removal or profile requirements |

**Worked language check.** “The roller does the road” is hard to check. “The compaction roller compacts the placed material” names the equipment and its function. In LA4-01, make each explanation this specific, then add the information handover. These are broad functions, not machine operating instructions.

## Understand the role and the handover

A paving team coordinates people whose tasks depend on one another. The driver delivers material; the paver operator manages the machine's placement function; the screed role attends to the screed's contribution to the placed mat; rakers support hand placement and finishing; roller operators perform the assigned compaction work; milling operators work with equipment that removes or profiles existing material. Job titles and boundaries vary. A job title alone does not establish a person's training, authorization or responsibility on a particular site.

**Fictional team brief W1.** At the Willow Reach training centre, a classroom team is preparing an explanation of a paving job. Jo coordinates the display. Ren checks the delivery/placement sequence. Ash prepares the screed-and-raker explanation. Kim checks the compaction and milling distinctions. The team has public introductory descriptions, but no site plan, machine manuals or permission to enter a worksite. The display must explain broad roles and show where one role needs information from another.

### LA4-01 · Make a role and dependency map

Map haul delivery, paver, screed, raker, roller and milling roles. For each, write one broad function and one information handover. Identify two assumptions W1 does not authorize. Each learner explains a different role before the group combines the map. **Skills:** Reading, Communication, Collaboration.

## Treat health and safety as a shared system with defined duties

Health and safety includes planning, suitable equipment and systems, instruction, supervision, reporting and review. It is not satisfied by telling a worker to “be careful.” A new person needs to know the supervisor, relevant hazards, emergency arrangements and how the actual task is done safely. Employers have responsibilities that cannot be replaced by a worker's confidence or a classroom certificate.

B.C.'s orientation provisions require workplace-specific training before a young or new worker begins work. A new worker can be an experienced person who is new to that workplace or affected by changed hazards. Additional orientation and training must be provided when observation shows a need or when the young or new worker requests it. Records are required. The complete workplace program and applicable legislation must guide actual work.

**Fictional case W2.** A learner arrives for a first workplace visit after completing classroom vocabulary. A host says, “You know the equipment names, so join that crew.” No supervisor contact, task demonstration or emergency information has been provided. Another host offers to stop the assignment and arrange the workplace orientation. The learner wants to participate but does not know the site's communication arrangements.

### LA4-02 · Choose the responsible next step

Explain why the second host's approach addresses a real gap. Draft a brief request for orientation and task clarification. Identify information the employer must provide and a question the learner can ask. Do not improvise signals or a route through live equipment. **Skills:** Problem Solving, Writing, Communication.

## Check licences and authorization precisely

A driver's licence, admission to a training course, equipment competence and site authorization answer different questions. ICBC identifies Class 5 as the common passenger-vehicle licence. A provider may publish its own admission requirement. Neither statement proves that the holder may operate every paving machine or drive every vehicle combination. Determine the actual vehicle, activity and location, then verify the applicable licence class, restrictions, endorsements, training and employer authorization through the responsible sources.

**Document-check case W3, using a real provider statement and fictional decision.** IUOE Local 115's public Asphalt Paving Laydown Technician page, retrieved 29 October 2026, lists a valid Class 5 or 7 driver's licence among its admission prerequisites. A fictional learner reads this and says, “Then Class 7 authorizes me to drive any haul truck at work.” The page is a course-admission source; it is not a legal ruling on that vehicle or a workplace assignment.

### LA4-03 · Separate four kinds of permission

Create a table for course admission, driver's licence, equipment competence and site assignment. State the question each answers and who or what record can confirm it. Correct the learner's claim without replacing it with another unsupported universal rule. **Skills:** Reading, Problem Solving, Writing.

## Practise professionalism through observable actions

Professionalism includes preparing for the agreed work, communicating changes, checking information, respecting people and taking care with records and materials. Pride in a project can be shown through a correction made before a mistake is passed on. It does not require pretending to know something or remaining silent when instructions are unclear.

**Worked handover.** “The map is finished” leaves the receiver guessing. A useful handover is: “The haul, paver and roller functions match our classroom brief. The licensing question is unresolved. The source note is saved as version 2. Please check the role labels; do not describe the draft as a site procedure.” The receiver restates the completed and pending parts before continuing.

### LA4-04 · Repair a rushed handover

Jo says, “Everything is approved—just print it,” although W3 is still unresolved. Give a respectful correction that protects the deadline and the accuracy of the work. Agree a revised print note and record each person's contribution. **Skills:** Communication, Collaboration, Adaptability.

## Plan development without promising job security

A worker may develop from entry tasks toward equipment roles, specialist functions, supervision or other pathways through training, experience and appropriate assessment. Progress is not automatic or identical for everyone. Ask what evidence, supervision and opportunities the actual employer or provider requires. A forecast of industry demand cannot guarantee a particular person's employment. Season, project timing, skills, location, employer needs and economic conditions can affect work.

Paving work may involve outdoor conditions, dust and fumes, noise, night or weekend schedules and seasonal changes. A job enquiry should ask about the actual schedule, hazards, controls, travel, orientation and employment terms. Do not assume a fictional schedule establishes lawful hours, overtime or pay rules.

**Fictional schedule W4.** A classroom planning record lists a Monday period from 18:00 to 02:00, including a 30-minute unpaid break for arithmetic practice. Tuesday is cancelled because of a changed project plan. Wednesday has six paid hours. These are invented conditions, not a recommended work arrangement or statement of employment law.

### LA4-05 · Check time and revise the plan

Find Monday's elapsed and paid time, then the total paid hours recorded for Monday and Wednesday. Write two questions about a real employer's terms that this classroom record cannot answer. Revise a weekly learning plan so a cancelled day does not become falsely recorded practice. **Skills:** Numeracy, Adaptability, Writing.

## Understand organized labour and employment documents

A union can represent workers in collective bargaining with an employer. A collective agreement records negotiated terms for the bargaining relationship; its application depends on the employer, bargaining unit, dates and the actual agreement. B.C.'s Labour Relations Board maintains a searchable directory of filed agreements. An old agreement found online is not automatically the current agreement for a new job. A training provider's fee page is also different from an employment agreement.

### LA4-06 · Prepare a useful enquiry and growth plan

Create an enquiry with four questions: applicable employment/collective-agreement information; expected working conditions; training and authorization for a desired role; and how progression is assessed. Add one sentence explaining collective bargaining and one explaining why industry demand or course attendance does not guarantee employment. Add a two-step learning goal with an observable result and a check date. Use fictional names; do not send the enquiry. On a device, save and reopen it with a clear version name. A partner suggests one improvement and you decide whether to use it. **Skills:** Digital, Writing, Creativity and Innovation, Communication.
