A1 · Foundations added to Chapter 1

Activity Expected evidence and reasoning
TB1-01 Source: approved aggregate/binder supply and identity; production: plant and specified mixture record; delivery: haul vehicle/ticket; placement: paver/tractor/screed and current work location; compaction/checking: roller and applicable inspection/test evidence. Accept equivalent accurate terms. Look for each learner explaining the handover, not simply copying five boxes.
TB1-02 Correctly locate the two paver sections and eight requested hand-tool features. Compare the real product rather than assuming every lute/rake has the same head. A usable report: “During the teaching inspection, the rake head moved at the handle connection. I set it aside from use and reported it to the instructor for assessment under the tool procedure.” A repair specification is not available.
TB1-03 Gravel/concrete: surface or material; logging/recreational access: use; corduroy: construction form; seasonal ice road: surface plus seasonal context. Roads can support services, trade, work and recreation. Appropriate project questions concern access changes, land/water effects and who must be consulted. No learner is required to disclose cultural knowledge.
TB1-04 A coherent route uses the five required terms accurately, explains a purpose and supporting layers, and identifies one legitimate limit such as actual thickness, density or operating settings. Observe the saved/reopened file for Digital. Record the listener's restatement and the author's revision.

A3 · Machine and process studies

Activity Expected evidence and reasoning
TB3-01 Feeder bed plate/chain/foot shaft/head shaft in the feeder diagram; auger segments/shaft in the auger diagram. Feeding moves mixture rearward; augers distribute it laterally. The bed plate is a wear surface beneath the material path; the chain/shaft assembly has moving parts. Actual service work requires the relevant isolation and maintenance procedure.
TB3-02 Match forward motion, screed weight, head of material, angle of attack and material stability. Weight is one factor in a responding system; it does not guarantee a fixed layer thickness. Identify actual model/manual, task conditions, approved procedure and qualified instruction before changing controls. No numeric setting can be inferred.
TB3-03 P has two recorded passes. Right strip has none, so complete width coverage cannot be claimed. The record does not establish density or an accepted compaction result. A clearer record distinguishes point and width observations and links the required test evidence. No operating pattern should be prescribed.

A5 · Map desk

Activity Expected evidence and reasoning
TB5-01 Site point: location record, not entrance or boundary. Entrance: teaching access point, not authorization for a real vehicle or route. Utility record: approximate and unverified, not exact position, depth or excavation clearance. Hidden layer changes display only. Observe layer toggling, record selection, export and reopening; check revision and selected record in the exported JSON.
TB5-02 B: 50 × 6 = 300 m²; 40 mm = 0.040 m; volume 12 m³; model mass 24.00 t. C: 60 × 6 = 360 m²; volume 14.4 m³; model mass 28.80 t. Increases: 60 m², 2.4 m³, 4.80 t. Delivered record total remains 23.60 t. Difference below model requirement: 0.40 t for B, 5.20 t for C. The factor is fictional and the difference does not establish placed thickness, density or payment.
TB5-03 Distinguish all four features, name the selected revision and identify genuine information gaps. Accept different clear prose. Reject claims that a parcel gives permission, a map line gives a safe dig location or a car route permits a heavy truck. Confirm an individual authored brief and revision.

Worked access brief (revision C): “Cedar Learning Access uses P-01 revision C for this classroom calculation. The site point locates the fictional facility; the entrance point identifies its teaching access location. The dashed boundary and approximate utility record have different meanings. Neither gives permission to excavate or establishes a verified utility alignment. The model route is 60 m by 6 m with a 0.040 m layer: 14.4 m³ and 28.80 t using the supplied invented factor. Actual work instructions would need the authorized design, verified access and utility arrangements, applicable traffic plan and current site controls. Please confirm the responsible reviewers before any field instruction is issued.”

B1 · Safety chapter

Activity Expected evidence and reasoning
TB7-01 8.2 lists weather clothing, general-purpose gloves, appropriate/safety footwear and safety headgear as worker provision responsibilities; agreements may allocate supply to the employer. Employer supplies other required PPE without charge. Purchasing an item does not prove selection, fit, compatibility, serviceability or training.
TB7-02 All four cases have unresolved protection. Avoid the exposure while the responsible person resolves selection/fit or withdraws defective/contaminated items under the procedure. Respirator interference needs the respiratory program's response, not an improvised adjustment. Chemical glove suitability requires product/task evidence; cracked headgear and contaminated equipment require appropriate removal/assessment/care.
TB7-03 (a) Possible inhalation/combustion exposure; assess equipment, location, ventilation and contaminants; do not treat a confined space as ordinary open work. (b) Ingestion route; hygiene and separation of contaminated work from eating. (c) Force/repetition/posture plus trip/route hazards; assess tool/load/layout/work organization. (d) Heat/workload/acclimatization concerns; applicable plan, controls and supervision. Identify affected people and a responsible role, with reassessment after a change. No exposure limit or control effectiveness can be inferred from the brief.
TB7-04 Wind and access changes affect exposure and affected people. Withdraw the old conclusion; have the relevant people reassess task controls and the entrance arrangement. An improvement makes unresolved decisions and responsibilities easier to find. Record each learner's contribution and the result of the peer test.
TB7-05 O-01 authorizes only the stated supervised teaching task; actual machine operation is excluded. Real emergency/first-aid/assembly details and demonstration limits require confirmation. Questions should be specific and assigned to the instructor/venue responsible person. Invented emergency details are unacceptable.
TB7-06 Product identifiers do not match; prevent use and obtain the correct label/SDS and workplace instruction through the responsible person. A similar name is insufficient. Observe actual file retrieval/save/reopen. Pictogram meanings must match CCOHS; no pictogram alone selects PPE or provides a complete emergency response. Environmental symbol is not mandatory under WHMIS.
TB7-07 The learner records verified venue arrangements or the responsible person and open confirmation. Assess the spoken message for location, incident, people and known hazards, without a real call. Classroom practice is not first-aid certification; actual designated coverage follows the employer assessment and applicable requirements.
TB7-08 Missing load mass, lift points, legible equipment identity/WLL, suitable configuration, inspection, qualified rigger/supervision and communication arrangement. No lift should proceed on this information. 15.2 concerns qualified people; 15.31 requires visual inspection before use each shift and immediate removal of defective gear; 15.34 addresses sling angle from vertical and required WLL reduction.
TB7-09 Identify the angle between sling leg and vertical, not horizontal. Signal names must match the official picture; verify each chosen signal directly. Unclear/conflicting signals require the response in the actual safe-work procedure and qualified communication arrangements. The exercise demonstrates recognition, not operational signaller competence.
TB7-10 Use the actual warning text in the illustrated CB2.5 GC/CB2.7 GC/CC2.7 GC manual, with publication and serial-range check. Identify the steering frame lock and articulation area. A stopped engine or one lock does not prove electrical, hydraulic, thermal, gravity and other energy is controlled. Actual isolation/blocking/verification is procedure-specific.
TB7-11 Map: obtain actual utility responses/locates/procedure. Overhead: confirm line/voltage and applicable limits/procedure with the responsible authority. Raised part: avoid exposure and have trained people establish specified support/isolation. Familiar task: reassess current conditions and instruction needs. Digital evidence is the saved plan-record note.
TB7-12 Consider facility users, deliveries, mobility/access needs and nearby workers. Drawing lacks actual dimensions, traffic-control plan, barrier specification and site assessment. Questions address safe accessible route, visibility, entry control, emergency access and change management. A notice can be clearer without pretending to design the work zone.
TB7-13 All nine requested brief elements appear, are internally consistent and identify responsibility/evidence for open decisions. A strong brief does not mark unverified work “safe.” Peer review tests findability; final record includes individual reasoning, revisions and observed digital actions.

Assess the integrated brief fairly

Use Not observed, Not yet demonstrated, Demonstrated with support and Demonstrated independently for the task under the observed conditions. Record the support and next practice. These local descriptions are not official Skills for Success levels or a trade qualification.

Numeracy inclusion for the integrated brief: attach the checked Chapter 5 quantity/revision sheet and the marked angle-convention diagram from TB7-09. This keeps a visible individual numeracy product with the brief. It does not add a lift calculation.

For delivery, divide Chapter 7 into supported sessions with time for reading, discussion and individual attempts. Use pictures or isolated teaching samples for recognition exercises. Schedule qualified instruction and authorized practical observation separately. Before teaching, verify current legislation, actual venue emergency arrangements, the fleet/manual match and any product-specific SDS. Technical approval and classroom pilot remain open in the delivery record.
