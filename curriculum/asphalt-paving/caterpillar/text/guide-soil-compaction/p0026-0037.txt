         [ PROPERTIES OF SOIL ]

        Engineers typically use a number of terms when
         defining the characteristics and properties of various
          soils. Understanding these terms is essential to
        understanding soil compaction principles and
        techniques.

       Load-Bearing Strength is the critical property of
        a road structure. Simply stated, it is the capacity
         of a structure to support the load that it carries.
       Assessment of load-bearing strength is normally
        achieved by proof rolling with a loaded dump truck
       and observing rutting or by plate load tests. In
        road construction, other properties like modulus,
         stiffness and density are typically used to provide a
        working target for load-bearing strength.
