[ SONIC GRADE SENSORS ]

Grade sensors are either sonic or mechanical.

Sonic sensors do not touch the grade reference.
Instead, sonic sensors emit sound pulses. The
sound pulses travel from the sensor to the grade
target. The grade target can be an existing roadway
surface, a milled surface, an adjacent bituminous
layer, a gutter, or a string. The sound pulses
rebound from the grade reference back to the
sensor.

When the grade sensor emits a sound pulse, a
timer starts. When the echo returns to the sensor,
the timer stops and this information is continuously
sent to the control panel or to the system
controller. The speed of sound is a known factor.
The controller uses the time it takes for the sound
pulses to rebound back to the sensor to calculate
the distance between the sensor and the grade        Sonic sensors use sound pulses to measure the profile of
target.                                                   the grade over which the bituminous layer is being placed.

Some sonic sensors have a single transducer that
emits one set of sound pulses. Cat Sonic Sensors
have five transducers.
