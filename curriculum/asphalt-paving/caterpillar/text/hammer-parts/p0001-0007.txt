 WEAR COMPONENTS
                                                                                                                                                 Power Cell                 Hydraulic component that converts hydraulic flow from the machine into reciprocating motion of the piston.
                                   HAMMER TOOLS                                                                                     Tool                     Ground engaging part of hammer. It transfers energy from the piston into the material being broken.

                                 HAMMER BRACKETS                                                                                      Tie Rod Nuts/Washers      Holds the back head, cylinder, and front head power cell sections together.
                                                                                                                                                                        Charging Valve           Used to charge the back head with nitrogen and to check nitrogen pressure.
                                   HAMMER LINES
                                                                                                                                                                                           Shuttle Valve               Controls the up and down motion of the piston via hydraulic fluid distribution.
                                          QUICK DISCONNECTS                                                                        Upper Bushing          Wear parts that guide the tool and keep it inline with the piston. The upper bushing constrains the upward motion of the tool.

                                       BUSHINGS, RETAINING PINS                                                                    Grease Fitting            Used to supply grease between the bushings and tool.
                                                                                                                                                                                     Tool Pin                    Retains the hammer tool within the front head. It is retained, in turn, by a roll pin.
                                              SEAL KITS
                                                                                                                                                       Lower Bushing          Wear parts that guide the tool and keep it in-line with the piston.
 OTHER WEAR COMPONENTS                                                                                                                                        Nitrogen Reservoir         Pressurized compartment within the back head, above the piston.
                                              BUFFERS                                                                         Back Head               Top portion of power cell. It contains the nitrogen charge used to store energy and impart down force to the piston.
                                                                                                                                                                                             Air Check Valve            Vents air pressure from the impact chamber during hammer operation, preventing damage to the lower cylinder seals.
                                                         TIE RODS
                                                                                                                                                                                      Cylinder                  Middle portion of the power cell. It contains the piston which cycles up and down, in time with the shuttle valve.
 COMPATIBILITY                                                                                                                                                               Piston                    Cycles up and down within the cylinder. It strikes the top of the tool, transferring an "energy wave" through the tool.

                                                                                                                                                                                            Front Head               Lower portion of the power cell. It retains and guides the hammer tool.
