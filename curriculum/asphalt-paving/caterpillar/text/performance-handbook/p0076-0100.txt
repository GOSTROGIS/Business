  The following example shows how trenching pro­duc­

  Digging time      =   9. 6 min

  Pipe Installation time = 19. 6 min

Calculate max. pipe installations/day:
  8 hrs (3. 06 pipe/hr) = 24. 48 pipe/day
Actual pipe/day:
