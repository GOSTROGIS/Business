## Getting to every part on the actual machine

Record these fields for each training asset:

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Manufacturer and model | To confirm for the actual training asset |
| Complete product identification / serial number and prefix | To confirm |
| Attachment model and serial | To confirm |
| Installed options, build and arrangement | To confirm |
| Applicable OMM publication and revision | To confirm |
| Complete parts catalogue and serial applicability | To confirm |
| Diagram group, callout, part number and quantity | Read from that confirmed catalogue |
| Supersession or dealer confirmation when needed | Record the dated result |

Use [Cat Parts Diagrams](https://parts.cat.com/en/catcorp/parts-diagram), [Cat manuals](https://www.cat.com/en_US/support/maintenance/service-manuals.html) and the provider's dealer/publication access. Training-machine model/serial details have not yet been supplied in this task. Do not guess catalogue applicability from a photograph, substitute a selected-parts guide for every assembly, or treat a diagram callout count as an ordering quantity.

The historical Section 4 course resources remain separately tracked in [the requirements record](../manuals/access-status.md). These OEM additions do not assert possession of the six named course manuals or the two historical training CDs. No purchase or external request was made.
