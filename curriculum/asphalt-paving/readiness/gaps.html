Currentness corrections for curriculum authors

Keep the historical outline intact. Apply the following checks when building instruction; do not copy an isolated historical phrase into a universal operating rule.

Outline location Issue found Required treatment in lessons
B1, source pp. 28–29 Rigging was named but missing from the collected teaching context Use complete Part 15, Part 14, official signals and the acquired inspection/manufacturer information. Practical lifts require qualified planning.
B1 / Section 4 PPE responsibility can be oversimplified Current s. 8.2 distinguishes worker-provided items, including safety headgear, from other employer-provided PPE, and permits agreements to provide worker items. Preserve those distinctions; apply selection, assessment and training requirements.
B1 First-aid framework has changed since 2016 Use current s. 3.16 and the Part 3 guidelines, including the written assessment, consultation and reassessment requirements effective November 1, 2024.
B2 and equipment lines Part 16 was replaced in 2021 Teach from the current mobile-equipment provisions and actual OEM instructions.
B3/B4 and H2 “Dust mask” is not an adequate selection method Assess the hazard and controls under current Parts 5/6/8. Respirator selection, fit and program requirements must follow the exposure and actual task.
B7 Traffic-control requirements have changed Use current Part 18, including qualified planning, risk assessment and orientation requirements, with the 2020 manual and road authority requirements.
B9, source p. 37 The under/over 5,000 kg shorthand is incomplete Check current BC cargo/vehicle/carrier applicability and NSC Standard 10; separately assess TDG duties. Do not teach a universal threshold from this sentence.
D1, source p. 43 Blanket environmental assertions need qualification Distinguish paving, production, recycling and contaminated materials; use evidence, actual products, emissions/permit conditions and site controls.
G1–G6 and Section 4 The required reference is a 2006 edition Teach historical document literacy explicitly. Use the edition and amendments incorporated into the actual contract for project decisions.
H3, source p. 62 Crew signals and crane/hoist hand signals differ Crew communication arrangements do not override Part 15.20's regulated crane/hoist hand signals.
H4 / I5 Example grease-shot quantities are not universal Use the specific OEM lubrication chart, lubricant and attachment requirements.
H5, source pp. 65–66 “EL” is expanded as “elevator” in one outline passage Use elevation, supported by the corresponding I6 passage and actual drawings.
I1, source p. 71 A generic compaction allowance appears Establish allowance from the mix, project and trial/field evidence; do not teach 20% as universal.
L2, source p. 96 A three-metre “best practice” phrase can be overgeneralized Determine exclusion zones from the hazard assessment, machine, task and applicable requirements; do not substitute a single universal distance.
E4/L5 Nuclear gauge terminology needs Canadian regulatory context Pair model instructions with CNSC licensing, radiation protection and TDG requirements.
Acquired 2025 specifications Update 3 Header and main heading give different dates Preserve both dates in the catalogue and have the contract administrator confirm the issued amendment set.

Access and scope limits

Official BC Hydro downloads returned HTTP 403 during this acquisition. The historical seven-steps handout was obtained from Metchosin Fire Department and is clearly identified as that copy; current Part 19 was acquired directly from WorkSafeBC. A Fluke manual could not be acquired from the attempted public endpoints; an actual ThermoWorks thermometer manual and diagram were acquired instead. The selected Leica level's manufacturer brochure/data sheet were acquired; its full user manuals are offered through MyWorld and remain an equipment-specific acquisition item. These are documented source limits, not completed manuals.

The catalogue records publication type, model/edition, source URL and original checksum. The manifest records the complete PDF page ranges, source article selectors, preserved images and derived-file checksums. PDF split parts retain source page appearance; text extraction is a search aid. The Tiger Torch sheet is image-only and the historical BC Hydro PDF has a defective text encoding; inspect their original page images for wording.

Use the readiness record to assign an owner, evidence and reviewer for each unresolved item. Blank fields mean pending. This collection supplies the context for curriculum development; practical readiness remains an evidence-based provider decision.
