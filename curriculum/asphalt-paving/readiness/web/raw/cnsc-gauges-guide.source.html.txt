 <p><a href="https://api.cnsc-ccsn.gc.ca/dms/digital-medias/Portable-Gauge-Quick-Reference-Guide-eng.pdf/object">Portable Gauge Quick Reference Guide (PDF) </a></p> <div class="col-md-12 alert alert-info mrgn-rght-0"> <h2>Important information to keep you on hand:</h2>  	<ul> <li>Radiation Safety Officer’s (RSO’s) name and 24-hour phone number</li> <li>CNSC 24-hour duty officer phone number: <strong>613-995-0479 or toll free 1-844-879-0805</strong></li> </ul> </div> <h3>Required documents</h3> <ul> <li>A valid TDG training certificate for Class 7</li> <li>A properly completed shipping document</li>  	<li>Emergency procedures</li>  	<li>Complete copy of current CNSC licence</li> </ul> <h3>Required device labelling</h3> <ul> <li>Name or job title of person to contact</li> <li>24-hour phone number</li>  	<li>Source details</li>  	<li>Radiation warning symbol</li> </ul> <div> <a class="col-md-12"><img src="https://api.cnsc-ccsn.gc.ca/dms/digital-medias/Portable-gauge-Quick-Reference-Guide.jpg/object" alt="" class="img-responsive" /> </a> </div> <div class="clear"></div> <h3>Package marking and labelling</h3> <ul type="disc"> <li>Markings:</li>  <ul>  	<li>Shipping name</li>  	<li>UN Number</li>  	<li>Consignor ID</li>  	<li>Specification mark "Type A"</li>  	<li>Name of package manufacturer</li>  	<li>Country of manufacturer (VRI code)</li>  </ul>  	<li>Class 7 category label on opposite sides  of the package – each label must include  the radioactive contents, activity and  transport index (TI)</li> </ul> <div> <a class="col-md-12"><img src="https://api.cnsc-ccsn.gc.ca/dms/digital-medias/Portable-gauge-Quick-Reference-Guide1.jpg/object" alt="" class="img-responsive" /> </a> </div> <div class="clear"></div> <h3>Device security</h3> <ul type="disc"> <li>The portable gauge must be either under the constant surveillance of a worker, or secured in a transport vehicle or at the storage location</li> <li>Verify the structural integrity of the Type A package</li> </ul>  	<h3>Notify the CNSC duty officer <strong>immediately</strong> of any reportable incident, including any of the following:</h3> <ul type="disc"> <li>lost, stolen or missing gauges</li> <li>damaged gauge impairing normal use</li> <li>transport accidents involving a gauge</li> <li>gauge with a stuck/open shutter</li> </ul>  	<p><strong>A full written report must also be submitted to the CNSC within 21 days.</strong></p>  	<h2>Radiation Protection</h2>  	<h3>Remember the ALARA principle:  As low as reasonably achievable</h3>  	<h3>Minimize your exposure by decreasing time, increasing distance and making use of shielding:</h3> <div> <a><img src="https://api.cnsc-ccsn.gc.ca/dms/digital-medias/Portable-gauge-Quick-Reference-Guide2.jpg/object" alt="" class="img-responsive" /> </a> </div> <div class="clear"></div> <table class="col-md-12"> <tr> <th>Time:</th> <th width="275">Distances:</th> <th>Shielding:</th> </tr> <tr> <td>Minimize time by planning your actions.</td> <td>Minimize distance by staying away from the gauge as much as possible.</td> <td>Incorporate shielding wehnever possible.</td> </tr> </table> <div class="clear"></div>  	<h3>Always ensure the gauge shutter is fully closed before transporting:</h3> <div> <a class="col-md-12"><img src="https://api.cnsc-ccsn.gc.ca/dms/digital-medias/Portable-gauge-Quick-Reference-Guide3.jpg/object" alt="" class="img-responsive" /> </a> </div>  	<div class="clear"></div>  	<h3>If the shutter is open - do not transport</h3>  	<h3>Best practice:</h3>  	<p>To avoid unnecessary exposure, use a radiation survey meter to confirm that the shutter is fully closed.</p>  	<p><strong>Ascertaining radiation doses</strong></p>  	<ul>  	<li>Wear your whole-body dosimeter (between the neck and waist) if you assigned one</li>  	<li>Log every shot (practice and real) to calculate dose</li> <ul> <li>1 shot = approximately 1.2 microsievert (&#181;Sv) of dose</li> </ul>  	</ul> <table class="text-right col-md-12"> <tr> <th>Incident response checklist</th> </tr> <tr> <td>Set a safe perimeter of 2 m and keep people away from the gauge</td> </tr> <tr> <td>Inform the appropriate person(s) immediately</td> </tr> <tr> <td>Initiate your emergency procedures</td> </tr>
