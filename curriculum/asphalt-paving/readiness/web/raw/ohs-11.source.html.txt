    $(function() {
        $("#md-global-search .SearchBoxButton").click(function () {

            if (searchValue != null && searchValue != '') {
                window.location.href = '/en/search#q=' + encodeURIComponent(searchValue) + '&sort=Relevancy&f:language-facet=[English]';

                    window.location.href = '/en/search#sort=relevancy&f:language-facet=[English]';

        });
    });

                <h4>Fall Protection</h4>
<ul class = "navHorizPipe navRegTools"><li><a href="/law-policy/occupational-health-safety/searchable-ohs-regulation/ohs-guidelines/guidelines-part-11#28B88C1741E3433FBF568D6FCDC8FF8B"/>Related Guidelines</a></li><li><a class='add_remove add_to_handbook' name='789DB6B3014043B38AE8FB37AD49106E' id='789DB6B3014043B38AE8FB37AD49106E' href='#'><span class='handbook_title'>+ Add to My Handbook</a></span></li></ul>


<a name="SectionNumber:11.1" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<h6>11.1 Definitions</h6>
<p>In this Part</p>

<p class="subL1"><em>&quot;anchor&quot;</em> means a component or subsystem of a fall protection system used to connect the other parts of a fall protection system to an anchorage, and includes an anchorage connector;</p>

<p class="subL1"><em>&quot;anchorage&quot;</em> means a secure connection point for a fall protection system;</p>
<a name="fall_arrest" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<p class="subL1"><em>&quot;fall arrest system&quot;</em> means a system that will stop a worker's fall before the worker hits the surface below;</p>
<a name="Endfall_arrest" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<p class="subL1"><em>&quot;fall protection system&quot;</em> means</p>
<p class="subL2">(a) a fall restraint system,</p>
<p class="subL2">(b) a fall arrest system, or</p>
<p class="subL2">(c) work procedures that are acceptable to the Board and minimize the risk of injury to a worker from a fall;</p>

<p class="subL1"><em>&quot;fall restraint system&quot;</em> means a system to prevent a worker from falling from a work position, or from travelling to an unguarded edge from which the worker could fall;</p>

<p class="subL1"><em>&quot;full body harness&quot;</em> means a body support device consisting of connected  straps designed to distribute the force resulting from a fall over at least the thigh, shoulders and pelvis, with provision for attaching a lanyard, lifeline or other components; </p>

<p class="subL1"><em>&quot;horizontal lifeline system&quot;</em> means a system composed of a synthetic or wire rope, installed horizontally between 2 anchors, to which a worker attaches a personal fall protection system;</p>

<p class="subL1"><em>&quot;lanyard&quot;</em> means a flexible line of webbing, or synthetic or wire rope, that is used to secure a safety belt or full body harness to a lifeline or anchor;</p>

<p class="subL1"><em>&quot;lifeline&quot;</em> means a synthetic or wire rope, rigged from one or more anchors, to which a worker's lanyard or other part of a personal fall protection system is attached;</p>
 
<p class="subL1"><em>&quot;personal fall protection system&quot;</em> means a worker&#146;s fall restraint system or fall arrest system composed of</p>
<p class="subL2">(a) a safety belt or full body harness, and</p>
<p class="subL2">(b) a lanyard, lifeline and any other connecting equipment individual to the worker</p>
<p class="subL2">that is used to secure the worker to an anchor, an anchorage or a horizontal lifeline system;</p>

<p class="subL1"><em>&quot;safety belt&quot;</em> means a body support device consisting of a strap with a means for securing it about the waist and attaching it to other components;</p>
<p class="amended">[Amended by B.C. Reg. 420/2004, effective January 1, 2005.]<br> 
[Amended by B.C. Reg. 404/2012, effective April 1, 2013.]</p>
 <a name="EndSectionNumber:11.1" style="padding: 0px;"></a>
 
<a name="SectionNumber:11.2" style="padding: 0px;"></a>
 
 <h6>11.2 Obligation to use fall protection</h6>

<p class="subL1">(1) Unless elsewhere provided for in this Regulation, an employer must ensure that a fall protection system is used when work is being done at a place</p>
<p class="subL2">(a) from which a fall of 3 m (10 ft) or more may occur, or</p>
<p class="subL2">(b) where a fall from a height of less than 3 m involves a risk of injury  greater than the risk of injury from the impact on a flat surface.</p>

<p class="subL1">(2) The employer must ensure that guardrails meeting the requirements of Part 4 (General Conditions) or other similar means of fall restraint are used when practicable. </p>

<p class="subL1">(3) If subsection (2) is not practicable, the employer must ensure that another fall restraint system is used.</p> 
<p class="subL1">(4) If subsection (3) is not practicable, the employer must ensure that one of the following is used:</p>
<p class="subL2">(a) a fall arrest system;</p>
<p class="subL2">(b) a rope access system that meets the requirements of Part 34.</p>

<p class="subL1">(5) If subsection (4) is not practicable, or will result in a hazard greater than if  a fall arrest system or a rope access system was not used, the employer must ensure that work procedures are followed that are acceptable to the Board and minimize the risk of injury to a worker from a fall. </p> 

<p class="subL1">(6) Before a worker is allowed into an area where a risk of falling exists, the employer must ensure that the worker is instructed in the fall protection system for the area and the procedures to be followed.</p> 

<p class="subL1">(7) A worker must use the fall protection system provided by the employer. </p> 
 
<p class="amended">[Amended by B.C. Reg. 420/2004, effective January 1, 2005.]<br>
[Amended by B.C. Reg. 199/2014, effective February  1, 2015.]</p>
<a name="EndSectionNumber:11.2" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<a name="SectionNumber:11.3" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<h6>11.3 Fall protection plan</h6>

<p class="subL1">(1) The employer must have a written fall protection plan for a workplace if</p>
<p class="subL2">(a) work is being done at a location where workers are not protected by permanent guardrails, and from which a fall of 7.5 m (25 ft) or more may occur, or</p> 
<p class="subL2">(b) section 11.2(5) applies.</p> 
<p class="subL2">(c) Repealed. [B.C. Reg. 420/2004, effective January 1, 2005.] </p> 
<p class="subL1">(2) The fall protection plan must be available at the workplace before work with a risk of falling begins.</p> 
<p class="subL1">(3) Repealed. [B.C. Reg. 420/2004, effective January 1, 2005.] </p> 
<p class="amended">[Amended by B.C. Reg. 420/2004, effective January 1, 2005.]</p> 
<a name="EndSectionNumber:11.3" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<a name="SectionNumber:11.4" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<h6>11.4 Selection of harness or belt</h6>

<p class="subL1">(1) A worker must wear a full body harness or other harness acceptable to the Board when using a personal fall protection system for fall arrest.</p>

<p class="subL1">(2) A worker must wear a safety belt, a full body harness or other harness acceptable to the Board when using a personal fall protection system for fall restraint.</p>

<p class="amended">[Enacted by B.C. Reg. 420/2004, effective January 1, 2005.]</p>
<a name="EndSectionNumber:11.4" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<a name="SectionNumber:11.5" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<h6>11.5 Equipment standards</h6>

<p class="subL1">Equipment used for a fall protection system must </p>
<p class="subL2">(a) consist of compatible and suitable components,</p>
<p class="subL2">(b) be sufficient to support the fall restraint or arrest forces, and</p>
<p class="subL2">(c) meet, and be used in accordance with, an applicable CSA or ANSI standard in effect when the equipment was manufactured, subject to any modification or upgrading considered necessary by the Board.</p>

<p class="amended">[Enacted by B.C. Reg. 420/2004, effective January 1, 2005.]</p>
<a name="EndSectionNumber:11.5" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<a name="SectionNumber:11.6" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<h6>11.6 Anchors</h6>

<p class="subL1">(1) In a temporary fall restraint system, an anchor for a personal fall protection system must have an ultimate load capacity in any direction in which a load may be applied of at least</p>
<p class="subL2">(a) 3.5 kN (800 lbs), or</p>
<p class="subL2">(b) four times the weight of the worker to be connected to the system.</p>

<p class="subL1">(2) Each personal fall protection system that is connected to an anchor must be secured to an independent attachment point.</p>

<p class="subL1">(3) In a temporary fall arrest system, an anchor for a personal fall protection system must have an ultimate load capacity in any direction required to resist a fall of at least</p>
<p class="subL2">(a) 22 kN (5 000 lbs), or</p>
<p class="subL2">(b) two times the maximum arrest force.</p>

<p class="subL1">(4) A permanent anchor for a personal fall protection system must have an ultimate load capacity in any direction required to resist a fall of at least 22 kN (5 000 lbs).</p>

<p class="amended">[Enacted by B.C. Reg. 420/2004, effective January 1, 2005.]<br>
[Amended by B.C. Reg. 19/2006, effective May 17, 2006.]<br>
[Amended by B.C. Reg. 404/2012, effective April 1, 2013.]</p>
<a name="EndSectionNumber:11.6" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<a name="SectionNumber:11.7" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<h6>11.7 Temporary horizontal lifelines</h6>

<p class="subL1">A temporary horizontal lifeline system may be used if the system is</p>
<p class="subL2">(a) manufactured for commercial distribution and installed and used in accordance with the written instructions from the manufacturer or authorized agent, and the instructions are readily available in the workplace,</p>
<p class="subL2">(b) installed and used in accordance with written instructions certified by a professional engineer, and the instructions are readily available in the workplace, or</p>
<p class="subL2">(c) designed, installed and used in a manner acceptable to the Board.</p>

<p class="amended">[Enacted by B.C. Reg. 420/2004, effective January 1, 2005.]<br>
[Amended by B.C. Reg. 19/2006, effective May 17, 2006.]</p>
<a name="EndSectionNumber:11.7" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<a name="SectionNumber:11.8" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<h6>11.8 Certification by engineer</h6>

<p class="subL1">The following types of equipment and systems, and their installation, must be certified by a professional engineer:</p>
<p class="subL2">(a) permanent anchors,</p>
<p class="subL2">(b) anchors with multiple attachment points,</p>
<p class="subL2">(c) permanent horizontal lifeline systems, and</p>
<p class="subL2">(d) support structures for safety nets.</p>

<p class="amended">[Enacted by B.C. Reg. 420/2004, effective January 1, 2005.]</p>
<a name="EndSectionNumber:11.8" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<a name="SectionNumber:11.9" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<h6>11.9 Inspection and maintenance</h6>

<p class="subL1">Equipment used in a fall protection system must be</p>
<p class="subL2">(a) inspected by a qualified person before use on each workshift,</p>
<p class="subL2">(b) kept free from substances and conditions that could contribute to its deterioration, and</p>
<p class="subL2">(c) maintained in good working order.</p>

<p class="amended">[Enacted by B.C. Reg. 420/2004, effective January 1, 2005.]</p>
<a name="EndSectionNumber:11.9" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<a name="SectionNumber:11.10" style="padding: 0px;"></a>

<h6>11.10 Removal from service</h6>

<p class="subL1">(0.1) If, at any time, a permanent anchor does not meet the requirements of section 11.5(c), the anchor must not be used until it has been inspected and recertified, by a professional engineer, as meeting the requirements of section 11.5(c).</p>

<p class="subL1">(1) After a fall protection system has arrested the fall of a worker, it must</p>
<p class="subL2">(a) be removed from service, and</p>
<p class="subL2">(b) not be returned to service until it has been inspected and recertified as safe for use by the manufacturer or its authorized agent, or by a professional engineer.</p>

<p class="subL1">(2) Subject to subsection (3), subsection&nbsp;(1)(b) does not apply to a personal fall protection system designed and intended for reuse by a performer in the entertainment industry for conducting a planned fall sequence.</p>

<p class="subL1">(3) The following conditions must be met before a personal fall protection system described in subsection&nbsp;(2) will be exempt from subsection&nbsp;(1)(b):</p>
<p class="subL2">(a) the system must be designed and used in accordance with a standard acceptable to the Board;</p>
<p class="subL2">(b) each use of the system must be carried out in accordance with the plan for the conduct of the fall;</p>
<p class="subL2">(c) the peak arrest forces generated in the system during each use must be at or below both the planned limits and the maximum forces allowed for the system;</p>
<p class="subL2">(d) after each use of the system no part of the system, including the anchorage, may be reused until a qualified person has inspected it and determined it is in serviceable condition and safe for reuse.</p>

<p> [Enacted by B.C. Reg. 420/2004, effective January 1, 2005.]<br>
[Amended by  B.C. Reg. 320/2007, effective February 1, 2008.]<br>
[Amended by B.C. Reg. 404/2012, effective April 1, 2013.]</p>
