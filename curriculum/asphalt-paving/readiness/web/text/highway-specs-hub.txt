Standard Specifications for Highway Construction
Last updated on April 1, 2026
Standard specifications for highway construction serves as a standard reference in construction contracts.
The specifications outline minimum requirements for highway construction in British Columbia. Where modifications are necessary to suit local conditions they will be specified in special provisions for each contract.
2025 Standard Specifications
The 2025 Standard Specifications for Highway Construction is completed and is adopted February 1, 2025.
