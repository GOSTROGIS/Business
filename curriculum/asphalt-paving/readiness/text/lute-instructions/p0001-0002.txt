             Light Lute/Lute
               Assembly Instructions

                             Tools Needed for Assembly:
                                               - 7/16” wrench (two)

                               Parts Shipped with Unit:
                                               - Head
                                               - Aluminum handle (not shown)
                                               - Braces:
                                                  -   Gusset braces (set of 2)
                                                  -   Bolt bag for gusset braces
                                 AND/OR
                                                  -   Wrap-around braces (set of 2)
                                                  -   Bolt bag for wrap-around braces


                            NOTE:  If you have chosen the Goliath bracing option, proceed with steps 1
                                through 3 of Gusset Brace instructions and steps 3 through 5 of Wrap-Around
                               Brace instructions.

                            Gusset Brace Instructions:
                                      1. Slide slotted handle over center hole on the aluminum head.
                                      2. Position one gusset on bottom of handle, as shown, and the other
                                   gusset on top of the handle.
                                      3. Insert the two longest bolts through the top gusset, the handle, and the
                                  bottom gusset. Insert the two shortest bolts through the two remaining
                                       holes. Install nuts, but do not tighten.
                                      4. Tighten all nuts on rake, being careful not to over tighten. Over
                                        tightening hardware may result in crushing the handle, thereby
                                 compromising its strength.


                            Wrap-around Brace Instructions:
                                      1. Slide slotted end of handle over center hole on aluminum head.
                                      2. Align holes and insert middle size bolt. Install nut, but do not tighten.
                                      3. Align holes of flat end of braces with holes on rake head, as shown, and insert
                                     the two shortest bolts. Install nuts, but do not tighten.
                                      4. Align holes of wrap-around end of braces with the handle, as shown, and insert
                                     the longest bolt. Install nut, but do not tighten.
                                      5. Once all nuts have been installed, tighten until snug, being careful not to over
                                          tighten. Over tightening may result in crushing the handle, thereby
                                 compromising its strength.
