ABOUT US





      Dear Seymour Midwest Partners,


          If you’re looking for a commodity tool company, we're sorry you’ve come to the wrong place. However, if you’re
      looking for a Partner who has never stopped engineering and manufacturing long handled and task specific
       tools, well, you’ve come to the right place.

     Our commitment to excellence has not wavered since 1872 when the Company we’re proud to lead today
     was founded. In an era of endless value engineering, product rationalization & increasingly anonymous
      companies, we’re delighted to lead a team of passionate employees that are dedicated to being
      toolmakers.


         At Seymour Midwest we start every day with two very simple goals, both of which start with you our
        Customers and Partners:
              • Delight the Customer
              • Outperform the Competition


    We trust you’ll find that our Partner Resource Guide meets both of our goals and ultimately makes it easier
      for you to buy, your customers to sell, and the end-user to select the right product.

      There is a lot of energy across the Seymour Midwest business as we look toward the future. We’re excited
      about our prospective outlook and look forward to our continued partnership.

       In the meantime, we look forward to seeing you at upcoming tradeshows and partner events. Don’t hesitate to
      reach out if there is anything we can do to help you grow your business.

      Your partnership and loyalty to Seymour Midwest and our family of brands is appreciated and valued!





                      SEYMOUR MIDWEST LEADERSHIP TEAM

LIMITED WARRANTY





      At Seymour Midwest we’re proud to continue our more than 150 YEAR HERITAGE by designing, manufacturing
     and selling tools offering outstanding performance year-after-year. We stand behind our products by
      extending to the original purchaser a warranty that our products will be free from manufacturing
      defects in materials and workmanship during normal use, subject to the qualifications listed below.

     The typical life of a tool is determined by the conditions and level of use. Frequent, daily use will wear out a
      tool faster than infrequent, occasional use. Defects in materials or workmanship typically arise early in
     the life of a tool. Our Limited Warranty does not cover normal wear, lost parts, damage inflicted
       intentionally by the user, or failures due to accident, misuse, abuse, negligence, alteration or modification.

      At Seymour Midwest’s discretion, Seymour Midwest will replace or issue credit for any tool or accessory that
     our examination determines to be within this warranty. Tools or accessories which are abused or worn out
     from normal use are excluded from this warranty.
