                        SPECIAL PROVISIONS APPENDIX
         FOR AMENDMENTS TO THE 2025 STANDARD SPECIFICATIONS
                    FOR HIGHWAY CONSTRUCTION
                         Update #3: April 1, 2026


The following amendments apply to and take precedence over the 2025 Standard Specifications for Highway
Construction, and over earlier amendments (if any).
This document is a consolidation of all SS 2025 amendments issued to date.
Amendments are presented in order of Section number, rather than release date. New amendments within this
Update are flagged as NEW. Others have been previously released on the indicated “Issued” date heading each
provision.
Unless the context implies otherwise:  Italicized text is instructions; unitalicized text is the amended language;
Redline/Strikeout formatting or grey-shading in table cells (when used) is only presented to aid users in identifying
changes that have been made within a block of text. Not all changes are marked.


                         SECTION 202 – GRANULAR SURFACING, BASE AND SUBBASES
NEW Issued Date: April 1, 2026
       SS 202.22 Target Density (page 7 of 14) is amended by deleting paragraph (a) and replacing it with the
        following:

              SS 202.22(a) Laboratory Density – Where the material gradation is within the applicable range,
              the Target Density shall be 100% of the Standard Proctor Maximum Dry Density obtained under
              the current ASTM D698, with oversize correction in accordance with ASTM D4718; or


                                SECTION 317 – P.E. PLASTIC DRAINAGE PIPE
Issued Date: September 8, 2025
       SS 317.06 Requirements (page 2 of 3) is amended by adding the following new provision:
              SS 317.06.05 Perforations – When perforated P.E. Plastic Drainage Pipe is specified, perforations
                   shall be 8 mm to 10 mm in diameter, circular, cleanly cut, and be configured in accordance with
               Table 318-C and Figure 318-1 of SS 318 PVC Plastic Drainage Pipe.
               For gasketed bell and spigot pipe, the pipe shall be unperforated for a length equal to the depth
                of the socket and/or shoulder.



                                SECTION 318 – PVC PLASTIC DRAINAGE PIPE
Issued Date: September 8, 2025
       SS 318.06.04 Perforations is amended by deleting “37.5 mm” from the first paragraph and replacing it
       with “375 mm”.

                                SECTION 412 – CONCRETE REINFORCEMENT
Issued Date: February 3, 2025
       SS 412.11 General (page 1 of 6) is amended by adding the following sentence to the end of the third
       paragraph:
             Wire ties for stainless steel reinforcing shall be double-wrapped and have a minimum diameter
                of 1.0 mm.


       SS 412.34.01 Lapped Splices (page 5 of 6) is amended by deleting paragraph (a) and replacing it with the
        following:
                 (a) Transverse bars for bridge decks shall not be spliced unless greater than standard mill lengths
                – 12 m for 10M bars, 18 m for larger bars, and 11.89 m for stainless steel bars – would
                  otherwise be required.

                                 SECTION 757 – REVEGETATION SEEDING
Issued Date: September 8, 2025
       SS 757.12.03 Seed Analysis Report (page 1 of 8) is amended by deleting “seven (7) days” from the first
       sentence and replacing it with “twenty-one (21) days”.
