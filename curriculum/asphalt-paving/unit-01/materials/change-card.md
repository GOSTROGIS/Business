## The visit has changed

The learner will have about **one minute** to review your information sheet. The printed copy will be **black and white**.

They want the three answers easy to find and do not want to rely on colour:

1. Where does the paving material come from?
2. Who owns the road in the fictional project?
3. Does a shorter delivery route prove which material is greener?

Revise your sheet to **no more than 90 words**. Keep the three answers and the labels or qualifications needed to avoid a misleading claim. **Do not reduce the font size to fit more words.**

Before revising, identify what changed, one useful feature to keep and one feature to change. Record a strategy that helps you continue, such as checking priorities, asking a clear question or trying a simpler layout.

Swap sheets with a partner. Ask them to locate each answer and identify anything unclear. Use their feedback to make one justified improvement. Check your word count and keep both drafts.

**Instructor reminder:** This change creates evidence of Adaptability. The first draft must use the original task conditions before learners receive this card.
