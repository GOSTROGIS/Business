Answer guidance

WH-01 · Reading · Communication

Name the message
Flame concerns specified fire-related hazards; flame over circle identifies oxidizers. Hot surface on the CAT safety
page is a machine warning. Exact chemical hazards come from the actual label and SDS.



WH-02 · Reading · Problem solving

Match the right document
Neither is confirmed. Obtain the current SDS for the exact product and supplier, compare product identifiers and
label information, and resolve any distributor/supplier naming relationship with the responsible person. Do not
choose by similar title.



WH-03 · Reading · Digital

Find the section
Sections 4, 6, 7, 8 and 16 respectively. Source: CCOHS WHMIS—Safety Data Sheet (SDS), revised 28 May 2026.
Observe the digital save/reopen action.

Answer guidance

WH-04 · Numeracy · Writing

Keep a count and report a gap
2 + 1 = 3 cards. 3 ÷ 12 × 100 = 25%. Example: “Three of twelve classroom cards lack a confirmed SDS match: two
names differ and one sheet is missing. Obtain and verify the correct sheets before using these as product-specific
examples.”



WH-05 · Problem solving · Communication

No symbol is not no hazard
Some hazard classes/categories require no pictogram. Read the complete label and SDS Section 2 and follow
workplace training. The biohazardous infectious materials pictogram also uses a circular border. A partner should
restate the need to check the full information.



WH-06 · Adaptability · Collaboration · Creativity and innovation

Update a shared briefing
Retain both design options, the changed revision record, a reader trial and explained improvement. Each learner
contributes and checks the new product/date against the supplied revised document. The exercise does not imply
every SDS revision changes the hazard classification.
