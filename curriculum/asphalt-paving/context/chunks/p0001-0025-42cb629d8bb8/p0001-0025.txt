         looking for a Partner who has never stopped engineering and manufacturing long handled and task specific

         companies, we’re delighted to lead a team of passionate employees that are dedicated to being
         toolmakers.

            Customers and Partners:

         for you to buy, your customers to sell, and the end-user to select the right product.

         about our prospective outlook and look forward to our continued partnership.

         In the meantime, we look forward to seeing you at upcoming tradeshows and partner events. Don’t hesitate to
         reach out if there is anything we can do to help you grow your business.
