Note: Thorough cleaning of fuel storage tanks is
strongly recommended before converting to Ultra
Low Sulfur Diesel (ULSD) (15 ppm or less sulfur)
and/or biodiesel/biodiesel blends. Conversion to
ULSD and/or biodiesel/biodiesel blends can loosen
fuel system and fuel storage tank deposits. Bulk tank
filtration unit and dispensing point filters, and onboard
engine filters change intervals may need to be
shortened for an extended period of time to allow for
this cleaning effect.
Note: Consult your local Cat dealer for additional
information on Cat designed and produced filtration
products.
