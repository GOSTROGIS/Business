Use this volume
This volume provides complete classroom readings, worked examples, named practice tasks and answer
guidance for screed control and handwork. Follow the learning route in each chapter, study the labelled
figures and explain your reasoning. The separate answer book contains the worked keys and observation
records.

Care for people, place and the next person using the work runs through the cases. Local First Nations, Métis
and Inuit learners and partners bring distinct knowledge and priorities. Participation does not require
sharing personal stories or cultural teachings, and no diagram claims to represent a Nation’s protocol.


    Classroom authoring is complete for the listed competencies. Qualified technical review, actual
    fleet/site matching, a local teaching pilot and observed practical qualification are separate
    requirements. The practical records are ready for an instructor to configure and use; they do not certify
    unobserved work.




Read the picture, then test the idea
    1. Identify the object, process or quantity shown.
    2. Read labels, units and the figure’s stated limits.
    3. Connect the image to the chapter explanation.
    4. Try the exercise and check against evidence.
    5. Explain the result to another person and revise if needed.



Use AI as a checked working tool
Claude and ChatGPT can help explain provided documents and create supported files. Claude Code,
Cowork and Codex can help with permitted multi-file tasks and repeatable workflows when their tools are
available. Give a clear purpose, named sources, output formats, limits and checks. Ask for a findings table
with page/cell evidence; verify it against the source. Reopen DOCX, XLSX, HTML or PDF outputs. For study
audio/video, verify the script, captions, diagrams and actual playback. A generated result is not a technical
approval.

Paving screed and attachments

Worked example · An extension line
A visible line follows the inner edge of an extension while the rest of the mat looks consistent.

Record side, station and conditions. Check the relevant extension setup, plate condition, heating and
material distribution through the authorized procedure. A line is a clue; do not assume one control or
universal adjustment will fix every model.


  Identify tow arm, main plate, extension, end gate and grade sensor on the supplied
  figures.

  Identify tow arm, main plate, extension, end gate and grade sensor on the supplied figures.

  Answer guidance.

  Tow arm transmits the pull; plate forms the mat; extension changes width as designed; end gate
  contains/shapes the edge; grade sensor follows its reference. Verify actual labels and model.

  Observe: Reading, Communication through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation
  or saved work.




  A fictional main width is 2.4 m with 0.6 m extension on each side

  A fictional main width is 2.4 m with 0.6 m extension on each side. Find total width.

  Answer guidance.

  2.4 + 0.6 + 0.6 = 3.6 m. This arithmetic does not establish compatible attachments or permissible
  operating width.

  Observe: Numeracy, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation,
  explanation or saved work.

Screed work safety procedures

Worked example · Width changes beside a walkway
The plan calls for widening the mat, but the extension would enter a protected walking route.

Hold the extension movement, communicate the conflict and have the responsible lead resolve the
work/access arrangement. Brief the change with traffic control and the crew before moving. Do not rely on
a shouted warning as the only control.


  Name three screed hazards that remain after travel stops.

  Name three screed hazards that remain after travel stops.

  Answer guidance.

  Residual heat, stored hydraulic pressure and gravity/unsupported components; electrical/heater
  energy or automatic functions may also remain. Use the actual energy assessment.

  Observe: Reading, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation
  or saved work.




  A 3.0 m width is widened by 0.45 m on each side

  A 3.0 m width is widened by 0.45 m on each side. What new width must the crew plan for?

  Answer guidance.

  3.90 m, plus the actual machine/people movement envelope and required controls. The dimension
  alone does not define a safe route.

  Observe: Numeracy through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Asphalt screed operator roles and
responsibilities

Worked example · One controlled change
A mat check is low after a speed change. One person wants more head, another wants a tow-point change
and a third wants a different speed.

Pause the conflicting instructions, record the observation and changed condition, and have the responsible
lead coordinate a controlled response. Recheck after the relevant response distance. Changing everything
at once prevents a useful diagnosis.


  A 40 m × 3.5 m area at 0.06 m loose thickness has what geometric loose volume

  A 40 m × 3.5 m area at 0.06 m loose thickness has what geometric loose volume?

  Answer guidance.

  40 × 3.5 × 0.06 = 8.4 m³. It is not a compacted volume or tonnage without the appropriate additional
  information.

  Observe: Numeracy, Reading through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or
  saved work.




  Write a message reporting a low right-side loose-depth reading.

  Write a message reporting a low right-side loose-depth reading.

  Answer guidance.

  State station, right side, measured depth/unit, target and observed condition; ask the responsible
  operator to confirm the next action. Avoid “make it thicker” without location or evidence.

  Observe: Writing, Communication through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation
  or saved work.

Screed maintenance

Worked example · A recurring streak
A streak returns along the same line after surface buildup is removed.

Record its position and persistence. Have authorized personnel inspect plate wear/flatness, extension
alignment, heating and relevant setup under safe conditions. Do not continue scraping a moving or hot
hazard area or guess a replacement tolerance.


  A fictional wear check shows 8.0 mm initially and 6.5 mm now

  A fictional wear check shows 8.0 mm initially and 6.5 mm now. Find the loss. Does that establish
  replacement?

  Answer guidance.

  Loss = 1.5 mm. Replacement depends on the actual manufacturer’s limit, measurement method and
  condition; the arithmetic alone is insufficient.

  Observe: Numeracy, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation,
  explanation or saved work.




  Identify which maintenance items belong to the actual screed and which to shared tractor
  systems.

  Identify which maintenance items belong to the actual screed and which to shared tractor systems.

  Answer guidance.

  Use the model diagram and schedule. Screed plates, extensions and thickness screws are screed
  items; engine radiator/battery may be shared tractor items. Record fitted options and do not invent
  absent components.

  Observe: Reading, Writing through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved
  work.

Operate asphalt screed

Worked example · Loose depth and final thickness
A verified trial relationship is loose/compacted = 1.25. Target compacted thickness is 40 mm. A loose-
depth check reads 50 mm.

Estimated loose target = 40 × 1.25 = 50 mm, so the reading matches that exercise target. Final compacted
thickness still requires the specified verification; one loose reading does not establish the whole mat’s
acceptance.


  For the same fictional factor, estimate loose thickness for a 48 mm compacted target.

  For the same fictional factor, estimate loose thickness for a 48 mm compacted target.

  Answer guidance.

  48 × 1.25 = 60 mm. Confirm through the approved setup and actual checks; the factor is not universal.

  Observe: Numeracy, Reading through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or
  saved work.




  Explain why repeated immediate adjustments after each probe reading can be unhelpful.

  Explain why repeated immediate adjustments after each probe reading can be unhelpful.

  Answer guidance.

  The screed response develops over travel distance. Repeated changes before the response is
  measured can create oscillation and obscure the cause. Use coordinated, authorized changes and
  location-based feedback.

  Observe: Problem solving, Communication through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation,
  explanation or saved work.

Worked example · Cleaning is delayed
The screed remains too hot for the approved cleaning product and procedure when the scheduled shift
ends.

Maintain the safe shutdown state and coordinate the required cooling and cleaning arrangements. Do not
spray early to meet the schedule. Record the outstanding task and responsible person so the next shift
does not assume cleaning is complete.


  Explain three checks before cleaning starts.

  Explain three checks before cleaning starts.

  Answer guidance.

  Actual heat condition/product compatibility; required isolation/support of shared systems; controlled
  area, PPE and waste containment. Confirm the complete machine procedure.

  Observe: Reading, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation
  or saved work.

  Answer guidance.

  75%, below the outline’s 80% threshold for this checklist task. Give specific feedback and repeat the
  required practice; all critical safety items must also pass.

Raker roles and responsibilities

Worked example · The same hollow returns
A small low area appears repeatedly behind the same screed location. The raker has filled it twice.

Report the repeated location and timing to the screed/quality lead. Complete only authorized hand
correction while the underlying cause is investigated. Repeated filling without diagnosis can change texture
and compaction and hide a continuing setup issue.


  Choose a lute, shovel, tamper and wheelbarrow for four distinct purposes.

  Choose a lute, shovel, tamper and wheelbarrow for four distinct purposes.

  Answer guidance.

  Lute distributes/finishes as appropriate; shovel transfers/places mix; tamper compacts suitable
  confined areas through the approved method; wheelbarrow moves manageable quantities on an
  approved route. Tool choice depends on the task and procedure.

  Observe: Reading, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation
  or saved work.




  A rectangular small area is 2.4 m × 1.5 m

  A rectangular small area is 2.4 m × 1.5 m. Find area for the work plan.

  Answer guidance.

  3.6 m². Thickness and suitable density are still needed for volume/mass; do not order from area alone.

  Observe: Numeracy through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.

Raking safety procedures

Worked example · A handle reaches into the lane
A worker turns with a long lute while watching the mat. Its handle crosses the work-zone boundary.

Stop and reposition through the safe route. Review the work space, tool orientation and traffic boundary
with the lead. A safe stance must account for the whole tool, not only the worker’s feet.


  Identify three ergonomic improvements for shovelling a small patch.

  Identify three ergonomic improvements for shovelling a small patch.

  Answer guidance.

  Use a suitable tool and manageable load; maintain stable footing and turn with the feet; position
  material to reduce reach and coordinate task variation/rest. Match the actual task and access needs.

  Observe: Problem solving, Reading through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation
  or saved work.




  A practice plan has 40 min of handwork split into four equal periods

  A practice plan has 40 min of handwork split into four equal periods. How long is each period? Does
  that prescribe a heat-rest schedule?

  Answer guidance.

  10 min each. No; the actual heat/work-rest plan depends on assessment and conditions, not this
  arithmetic example.

  Observe: Numeracy through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved work.
