Pneumatic Compactors – Pneumatic compactors
are used on small-to-medium size soil compaction
jobs, primarily on bladed, granular base materials.
Often, they are used as a finishing compactor after
a vibratory drum compactor completes compaction
of the lift. Pneumatic compactors are best suited
for sealing the surface, special applications such
as compaction of thin lifts, or special requirements
dictated by the job.

The compactive forces (pressure and manipulation)
generated by the rubber tires work from the top
of the lift down to produce density. The amount
of compactive force can be varied by altering the
tire pressure (the normal method) or by changing
the weight of the ballast (done less frequently).
The kneading action caused by the staggered tire
pattern helps seal and smooth the surface.

Pneumatic compactors can be used on soil and
asphalt, an advantage that allows a road-building
contractor to use one compactor for multiple stages
of construction.
