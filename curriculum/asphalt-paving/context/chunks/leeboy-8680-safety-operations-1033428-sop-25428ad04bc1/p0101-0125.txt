Plus +1® Controllers
The Plus +1® system contains several controllers and
OX-modules operating in the background that moni-
tors paver control systems and alerts the operator in
the event of a fault. This is the brains behind paver
and engine systems. When it senses a fault, the Plus
+1® sends a message to the DM430 display to alert
the operator. The Plus +1 controller and OX-modules
are located throughout the engine compartment and
on the screed. (A complete list of fault codes with
corrective action messages are shown in Section 5,
Troubleshooting.)
The Plus One system will not allow the engine to start if:
• Steering control box not detected.
• Joystick(s) not in neutral at start-up.

Operation

Keep these items in mind for operating with rubber
tracks and maximizing track life:
• Avoid detracking episodes by:
    •   Maneuvering at lowest speed possible;
    •   Avoiding sharp turns on hot asphalt (move to a
        cold area for turns);
    •   Making gradual turns in as large an arc as
        possible, repeating zigzag motions gradually
        instead of pivoting;
    •   Using forward instead of reverse when possible;
    •   Preventing large foreign objects from being
        entangled in the undercarriage/tracks.
• Properly trained operators who practice good
  operating techniques will make a big difference.
• Avoid spot turns, spinning, constant operation on
  slopes and contact with the side of tracks on curbs,
  hard walls and other objects. Limit use around large,
  sharp and rocky surfaces such as manholes and
  drainage covers.
• Clean, inspect and check alignment on the rubber
  tracks at least once per day. Replace worn parts
  immediately if needed.
• Avoid track contact with abrasive/corrosive materials.
  If oil or similar products get on the tracks, clean it off
  immediately. Over time, oil degrades rubber quality.
