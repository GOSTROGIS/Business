 Check the torque hub oil level at the rear of the
 tracks weekly to ensure optimum operation. To
 check the oil level:
                                                                                                  5
 1.   Move paver so that one of the hub plugs is in the
      12 o’clock position and the other is in the 3 or 9
      o’clock position as shown in Figure 5-3.
