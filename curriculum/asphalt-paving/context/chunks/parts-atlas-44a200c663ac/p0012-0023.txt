Build the record for your actual machine
Record manufacturer, model, complete product identification/serial number, attachment model, publication number,
revision, applicable serial range, diagram group and callout. A similar-looking machine is a starting point for a search;
it is not enough to choose a part number.

Use Cat Parts Diagrams and the Cat service-manual route. Confirm any required account or dealer access through
the provider. Keep the applicable operation manual beside the parts catalogue.
