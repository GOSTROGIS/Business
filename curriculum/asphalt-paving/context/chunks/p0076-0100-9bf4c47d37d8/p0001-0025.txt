Your engine is equipped with one belt that operates
the alternator and the water pump. For maximum
engine performance and maximum utilization of your
engine, inspect the belt for wear and for cracking.
Check the tension of the belts. Adjust the tension of
the belt to minimize belt slippage. Belt slippage will
decrease the belt life. Belt slippage will also cause
poor performance.
