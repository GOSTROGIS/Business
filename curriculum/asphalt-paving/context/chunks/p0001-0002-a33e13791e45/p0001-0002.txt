                                Gusset Brace Instructions:

                                          1. Slide slotted handle over the center hole on the blade.
                                          2. Position one gusset on the bottom of the handle, and the other
                                             gusset on top of the handle.
                                          3. Insert the two longest bolts through the top gusset, the handle and
                                             the bottom gusset. Insert the two shorter bolts through the two
                                             remaining holes on the frame.
                                          4. Tighten all nuts, being careful not to over-tighten. Over-tightening
                                             hardware may result in crushing the handle, thereby compromising
                                             its strength.
