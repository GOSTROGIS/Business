MP (Metal Professional) counter alignment

                                                                               measuring wheels

                                                            Counter not reading right?
                              Locate the flapper.                  Check your flapper alignment!


                                                                              MP counter close-up

                                                                                     B


                                                                     C                   A



                                                                               30º
                                                           (If you’re replacing the flapper, undo the
                                                           A screw and put on the new flapper.
                                                           Replace screw “A”.)

                                                           Adjust the angle of the flapper if neces-
                                                           sary. Loosen the B screw and adjust the
                                                           angle of the flapper to 30º . If you look
                                                           directly at the counter from the side,
                                                           align the flapper so that you can just see
                                                           the cicle (C) on the counter box. It is not
                                                           red on your counter, it is black.

                                                           Test it by rotating the wheel slowly. The
                                                           counter should tick off a foot when the
                                                           pin is about 1/4” from the bottom tip of
                                                           the flapper.
