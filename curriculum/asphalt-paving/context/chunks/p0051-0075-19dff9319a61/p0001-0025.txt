WORK LIGHTING PACKAGE
Includes forward-facing and rear-facing lights that mount
to the tractor. Includes hardware and harnesses.




ROADING LIGHT PACKAGE
Includes:
  • 2 – Front headlamps with turn signals
  • 2 – Rear flood lamps
  • 2 – Rear turn signals with brake lamps
  • Hardware and harnesses




LED SCREED LIGHTING PACKAGE
Includes four lights mounted to the rear of the tractor
and four mounted on the screed. Includes hardware
and harnesses.




HAZARD BEACON PACKAGE
An LED amber beacon mounted on a 91 cm (3 ft) mast
with coil cord. Available on pavers without the hardtop
canopy option. Includes hardware and harnesses.
