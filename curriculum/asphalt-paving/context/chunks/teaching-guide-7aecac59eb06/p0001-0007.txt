Teach the source check
Model one callout: show the leader line, read the exact term, explain its purpose and name the publication/page.
Then ask the learner to do the next example. Accept pointing, speech or accessible text for identification, and collect
writing or digital work when those skills are being assessed.

Record: accurate name; correct callout; correct source/model scope; explanation of uncertainty; successful check-
back. Give feedback and another attempt. These classroom records do not certify equipment operation or complete
a technical trade competency.

Answer and observation guidance

CAT-ID-01

Follow the material
Tractor and screed. The feeder chain carries material through the feeder path; the augers distribute it across the
width. Use the original leader lines, not the nearest-looking feature. The screed plate is the lower working surface
that shapes the mat.



CAT-ID-02

Read every conveyor callout
1 guide rollers; 2 trough rollers; 3 return idlers; 4 tail pulley; 5 tail pulley bearings; 6 bunk rollers; 7 head pulley.
Example: “The component is the tail pulley bearings in the cold-planer conveyor diagram. CAT-08, Cold Planer Parts
Reference Guide, PDF page 25, callout 5.”



CAT-ID-03

Count callouts without inventing quantities
7 + 6 = 13 legend entries across the diagrams. 2 × 7 = 14 printed labels on two posters. A callout may identify a type
of part, several instances or an assembly. Required quantity and part number come from the applicable parts table
and machine identity.

Answer and observation guidance

CAT-ID-04

Check a roller-control match
6 emergency stop knob; 12 parking brake; 14 water spray system; 16 vibratory mode / emulsion switch. Engine start
switch is 15, not 12. Retain optional equipment and model conditions. This is a diagram check, not an instruction to
operate the controls.



CAT-ID-05

Follow the loader letters
A loader arm to front frame; K lift cylinder to loader arm; Y lift cylinder to front frame; S1 steering cylinder to rear
frame; S2 steering cylinder to front frame. A complete check-back preserves front/rear and the cylinder name.



CAT-ID-06

Save a source you can find again
CAT-08, QEXQ3131-01 (05/2026), original PDF page 25, sources/cold-planer-parts-official/p0001-0025.pdf, viewer
page 25. Accept the actual generated path from the link if the collection is reorganized. Observe
save/reopen/retrieval; a written promise to save is not evidence of the Digital action.

Answer and observation guidance

CAT-ID-07

Respond to a changed machine record
Move from EMB to KSA and the stated lift configuration. Obtain the complete serial/product identification number
and verify the current applicable parts catalogue and arrangement. Prefix and lift type affect the table lookup. Keep
the original and corrected record; do not issue a part order from this exercise.



CAT-ID-08

Make a component check-back
Accept callouts 1–4 with exact source names and correct diagram location. Observe both contributions and an
actual check-back. If the listener identifies the wrong ski or edge, return to the leader line and repair the message
together.



CAT-ID-09

Design a useful learning card
Keep both ideas, the reader’s task result and the explained revision. A useful card has a readable callout,
source/page credit, accurate scope and no obscured labels. Do not redraw a WHMIS symbol or replace a
manufacturer warning with invented artwork.

Answer and observation guidance

CAT-ID-10

Complete a machine identification record
A complete record distinguishes what is stated in the manual from what is known about the actual machine.
Unknown identity fields remain explicit. The reviewer verifies the same source and records the result. No learner
needs to share personal information or enter an operating area to complete this classroom task.
