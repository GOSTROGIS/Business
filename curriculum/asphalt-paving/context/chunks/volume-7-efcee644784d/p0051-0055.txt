Prepare a small-area finishing plan

    This is a complete fictional classroom case. It does not describe an actual community, Nation,
    employer or project. Learners may use the supplied case without disclosing personal or cultural
    knowledge.



A fictional rectangular repair is 2.5 m × 1.2 m with a 0.05 m compacted depth. The structure elevation and
joint treatment are supplied by an approved drawing, but no mixture density or universal compaction
allowance is given. A draft proposes heating any cooled mix with a torch and using the same loose-
thickness factor on every job.




  Reading · Check the facts
  Identify the controlling facts, missing information and one unsupported statement in the brief.




  Writing · Make the handover useful
  Draft a concise handover with location, quantities, status and the next responsible action.




  Numeracy · Calculate and check
  Show the case calculation with units and an independent reasonableness check.
