Wheel loader Forestry-specific configurations equip the

USE OF LOG VOLUME TABLES
   The tabulated volumes on these pages were calcu­
lated with no taper in log diameter from base to top.
Therefore each value listed in the table represents the
volume of a true cylinder. In practice this may occur
only in short sections of large diameter trees. To obtain
the volume of solid wood logs, exclud­ing bark:
1.	 Establish the base diameter of the log inside the
    bark and above the butt flare (extreme end taper).
2.	 Repeat the procedure for the top (small end) of log.
3.	 Enter log volume table at each of the two estab­lished
    diameters. Move horizontally to the vertical column
    closest to the length of the log being measured.
4.	 Establish the volume figures for each end of the log,
    add the two together and divide by two to obtain aver­
    age log volume.
