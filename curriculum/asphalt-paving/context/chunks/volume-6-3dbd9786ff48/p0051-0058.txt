Study chapter · Outline I9



Transport paver
Moving a paver between sites requires a verified transport configuration and a coordinated loading plan.
The fitted screed and attachments can change width, mass and securement requirements.


  Compliance with wide load regulations

  Roles and responsibilities

  Prepare machine for transport

  Loading and unloading procedures


LEARNING STEP 1




Compliance with wide load regulations
Measure actual transport dimensions and confirm the load, route and applicable BC commercial transport
requirements. Signs, lighting, permits, pilot cars and travel restrictions depend on the actual movement;
they are not triggered by a single classroom rule. Removing extensions may change width but requires the
approved handling and securement method. The carrier confirms the current requirements before
departure.

LEARNING STEP 2




Roles and responsibilities
Assign a competent machine operator, carrier/driver and ground guide as required. The driver/carrier
manages transport compliance and securement within their responsibility; the designated operator follows
the machine loading procedure. Do not assume the paver operator is always included or excluded.
Everyone must know the stop signal, communication method and final release responsibility.

LEARNING STEP 3




Prepare machine for transport
Follow the OMM transport configuration for screed, extensions, augers, cut-offs, guards and loose
equipment. Use approved lifting/rigging for removed components and designated tie-down points. The
LeeBoy 8680 example gives specific preparation and loading instructions in printed pages 4-64–4-65;
those instructions apply to the stated model/serial scope, not every paver. Read the complete procedure
before a practical demonstration.

Loading and unloading procedures
Check carrier/ramp capacity, alignment, support, traction and the clear movement envelope. Use the
specified loading direction and controlled speed, with a guide positioned safely. Stop if alignment or
communication is lost. After loading, position/support the machine, apply required parking controls and
secure using rated components and the applicable National Safety Code Standard 10/BC requirements.
Control the machine before releasing restraints for unloading. A time target never justifies rushing a critical
step.
