BEFORE YOU DIG...
                          6               Steps
                                          to Safe
                                          Digging


1. Clearly identify and white line the excavation
   area.
2. Click bc1c.ca or Call
   BC 1 Call at 1.800.474.6886
   at least three working days
   before you dig.*
3. Contact all utility, pipeline,
   property or infrastructure
   owners who are not
   BC 1 Call members.
4. Visually inspect the work site for signs of
   underground infrastructure and other safety
   concerns.
5. Obtain all agreements/permits and keep
   them on site.
6. Verify location and expose underground
   utilities by hand digging or other approved
   non-mechanical excavation means.

Further information and excavation Best Practices
are available at commongroundbc.ca
*Some utility owners may require more than three working days
and under the regulations you must follow their requirements.
