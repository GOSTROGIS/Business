Recognize the symbol.
Read the whole message.
A pictogram alerts you to a type of hazard. The label and safety data sheet (SDS) explain the particular product and
its precautions. The same work area can also have machine warnings, traffic signs and emergency signs; each has a
different purpose.

In an asphalt crew, check the actual products used for the task: fuels, cleaners, release agents and other supplied
materials. Do not assign a WHMIS classification from the word “asphalt”, a container colour or a familiar brand.


    Some WHMIS hazard classes and categories have no required pictogram. No pictogram does not mean no
    hazard. Check the product identifier, complete label and SDS Section 2.


This classroom material develops WHMIS understanding. The employer also provides training for the actual
hazardous products, tasks, controls and emergency procedures at the workplace.

Current teaching basis: CCOHS fact sheets revised 28 May 2026, reflecting the December 2022 HPR amendments. The two WorkSafeBC books in
the source pack are labelled as 2019 historical explanations.

                                 Corrosion or serious damage. May identify corrosive-to-metals, skin-corrosion or serious-
                                 eye-damage hazards. Read which hazards apply to the product.

                                 Exclamation mark

                                 Health hazard
                                 Serious health effects. Used for specified hazards such as cancer, respiratory sensitization,

Supplier label

Look for the product identifier, initial supplier identifier, applicable pictograms, signal word, hazard statements and
precautionary statements. Required supplemental information may also apply. Supplier label information is provided
in English and French. “Danger” and “Warning” are signal words; their use depends on classification.

Workplace label

A workplace label generally contains the product identifier matching the SDS, safe-handling information and a
statement that the SDS is available. Requirements and limited exceptions for transferred containers depend on the
circumstances and jurisdiction. Use the employer’s procedure; do not treat “only a small amount” as a reason to
leave a shared container unidentified.

Machine warnings have their own context
The pages below show genuine CAT roller safety messages. Hot-surface and crush warnings describe equipment
hazards. A chemical pictogram on a product label describes that product’s classification. Keep the message with its
location, machine and complete instruction.

  Example                 Where to look                           What to preserve

  Hot surface             CAT-10, PDF page 9, safety message 4    Location near exhaust outlet, illustration and full burn warning

  Crush hazard            CAT-10, PDF page 10, messages 5 and 6   Articulation location, machine movement and frame-lock context

  ROPS warning            CAT-10, PDF page 10, message 7          Complete do-not-weld-or-drill warning and model context




    Use the exact machine’s warning labels and manual during supervised training. The reproduced pages are from
    M0130633-02 for its listed CB2.5 GC, CB2.7 GC and CC2.7 GC serial ranges. These images are classroom
    references, not replacement machine decals.
