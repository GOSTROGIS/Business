Use this volume
This volume provides complete classroom readings, worked examples, named practice tasks and answer
guidance for road geometry and acceptance. Follow the learning route in each chapter, study the labelled
figures and explain your reasoning. The separate answer book contains the worked keys and observation
records.

Care for people, place and the next person using the work runs through the cases. Local First Nations, Métis
and Inuit learners and partners bring distinct knowledge and priorities. Participation does not require
sharing personal stories or cultural teachings, and no diagram claims to represent a Nation’s protocol.


    Classroom authoring is complete for the listed competencies. Qualified technical review, actual
    fleet/site matching, a local teaching pilot and observed practical qualification are separate
    requirements. The practical records are ready for an instructor to configure and use; they do not certify
    unobserved work.

Drainage, grade and base

Worked example · Follow the water
A 120 m access road has a sound-looking base. A blocked catch basin sits at its low point, and water is
pumping through one wheel path. The paving crew is ready.

Hold the affected work and identify the drainage and support problems. Protect public access and the inlet,
document the location, and request the responsible person’s correction and acceptance. Do not treat extra
asphalt as a drainage repair. Record the repaired outlet and accepted foundation before release.


  Draw the four typical layers from prepared soil to asphalt

  Draw the four typical layers from prepared soil to asphalt. Label one check at each interface.

  Answer guidance.

  From bottom: subgrade—support/moisture; sub-base—approved material and accepted lift; base—
  grade/crossfall/cleanliness; asphalt—specified mixture and compacted thickness. Actual designs may
  omit or add layers; use the project section.

  Observe: Reading, Writing, Creativity and innovation through the learner’s actual contribution,
  calculation, explanation or saved work.




  An approved 80 m × 4 m sub-base lift is 0.15 m compacted thickness

  An approved 80 m × 4 m sub-base lift is 0.15 m compacted thickness. Find its compacted volume.
  Can you order that many loose cubic metres without further information?

  Answer guidance.

  80 × 4 × 0.15 = 48 m³ compacted. Loose supply volume requires a justified conversion or project
  allowance; 48 m³ loose is not automatically enough.

  Observe: Numeracy, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation,
  explanation or saved work.

Verify slopes, crowns and grades

Worked example · A level-book entry
Benchmark EL 100.000 m; backsight 1.250 m; foresight to prepared base 1.670 m. Target base EL 99.650
m.

HI = 101.250 m. Existing base = 101.250 − 1.670 = 99.580 m. Target minus existing = +0.070 m: fill 70
mm at this point, subject to the specified material and compaction method. Check against the correct
drawing revision and benchmark before directing work.


  Use the same benchmark with backsight 1.400 m and foresight 1.600 m

  Use the same benchmark with backsight 1.400 m and foresight 1.600 m. Target EL is 99.650 m. State
  cut or fill.

  Answer guidance.

  HI 101.400 m; existing EL 99.800 m; target − existing = −0.150 m, so cut 150 mm at the measured
  point.

  Observe: Numeracy, Reading through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or
  saved work.




  A profile uses horizontal 1:500 and vertical 1:50

  A profile uses horizontal 1:500 and vertical 1:50. Explain why measuring its apparent angle is
  unreliable.

  Answer guidance.

  The vertical scale is exaggerated ten times relative to horizontal. Calculate actual rise over horizontal
  run using dimensions and elevations. A screenshot may also be resized.

  Observe: Digital, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or
  saved work.

Final grade preparation

Worked example · Width before tonnage
A rectangular paving area is 60 m long. The approved width is 4.2 m, but the marked limits measure 4.0 m.

Approved area = 252 m²; marked area = 240 m². The shortfall is 12 m². Verify the drawing and correct the
layout before ordering or placing to the wrong boundary. Extra mix later does not automatically restore the
intended edge support and joint quality.


  The target casting EL is 102.340 m and its verified current EL is 102.295 m

  The target casting EL is 102.340 m and its verified current EL is 102.295 m. Find the required
  elevation change.

  Answer guidance.

  Raise 0.045 m, or 45 mm, using the approved adjustment system and recheck. This arithmetic does
  not specify which ring or bedding detail is acceptable.

  Observe: Numeracy, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation,
  explanation or saved work.




  Build a five-item release record for the base.

  Build a five-item release record for the base.

  Answer guidance.

  Include drawing/revision and location; grade/crossfall checks; compaction/support acceptance; clean
  surface and drainage; utilities/edges/access ready. Identify checker, date and unresolved holds.

  Observe: Writing, Digital through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or saved
  work.

Gradation

Worked example · One good result is not full acceptance
A fictional 600 t lot qualifies for a +$0.75/t gradation adjustment under the complete applicable table
conditions. A separate density result is under review.

The isolated gradation adjustment is 600 × $0.75 = +$450. Record it as provisional for this parameter. Do
not declare the lot accepted or payable until all applicable requirements and the authorized disposition are
resolved.


  An audit finds sample Q17 on a report but no matching location log

  An audit finds sample Q17 on a report but no matching location log. Write a finding and action.

  Answer guidance.

  Finding: Q17 cannot be traced to a location/lot from the available log. Preserve records, notify the
  quality lead, investigate original evidence and obtain an authorized disposition; do not invent a
  location.

  Observe: Writing, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation
  or saved work.




  For a fictional 420 t lot, calculate a −$1.50/t isolated adjustment.

  For a fictional 420 t lot, calculate a −$1.50/t isolated adjustment.

  Answer guidance.

  420 × −$1.50 = −$630. It is a deduction for the identified parameter only, subject to the actual
  contract and acceptance process.

  Observe: Numeracy, Reading through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or

Oil content

Worked example · Read the basis before the band
Approved binder content is 5.20 kg/100 kg dry aggregate. The three fictional sub-lot results are 5.45, 5.50
and 5.49 on that same basis. Lot mass is 500 t.

Mean = (5.45 + 5.50 + 5.49)/3 = 5.48. Absolute deviation = 0.28. Under the dated Table 502-F exercise,
0.16–0.30 gives $0/t, so this isolated adjustment is $0. This says nothing about other acceptance
parameters.


  Approved content is 5.20 and the mean is 4.86, both kg/100 kg dry aggregate

  Approved content is 5.20 and the mean is 4.86, both kg/100 kg dry aggregate. Find deviation and the
  historical isolated adjustment for 300 t.

  Answer guidance.

  Absolute deviation = 0.34. Table 502-F band 0.31–0.35 gives −$1/t; 300 t gives −$300. Check lift,
  reporting and all other contract conditions before disposition.

  Observe: Numeracy, Reading through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or
  saved work.




  A report says 5.2% but has no basis

  A report says 5.2% but has no basis. Can it be compared directly with 5.2 kg/100 kg dry aggregate?

  Answer guidance.

  No. Obtain the test report’s basis and method. A percentage of total mixture is not the same as a
  percentage of dry aggregate.

  Observe: Problem solving, Communication through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation,
  explanation or saved work.

Application

Worked example · Mass over area
A fictional lot receives 288 t over 2,400 m². Specified application is 120 kg/m².

288 t = 288,000 kg. Actual = 288,000/2,400 = 120 kg/m². Ratio = 120/120 × 100 = 100%. In the dated
96.0–103.9% band, isolated adjustment = 288 × $0.50 = +$144, subject to all applicable conditions.


  A 200 m × 3.5 m rectangle receives 77 t

  A 200 m × 3.5 m rectangle receives 77 t. Find actual application rate.

  Answer guidance.

  Area = 700 m². Mass = 77,000 kg. Rate = 110 kg/m². Compare with the specified rate only after
  checking the lot geometry and ticket allocation.

  Observe: Numeracy, Reading through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or
  saved work.




  Specified rate is 115 kg/m²

  Specified rate is 115 kg/m². What percentage of specified is 110 kg/m²?

  Answer guidance.

  110/115 × 100 = 95.652…%, approximately 95.65% before applying the contract’s reporting rule. Do
  not round prematurely to 96% to choose a different band.

  Observe: Numeracy, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation,
  explanation or saved work.

Segregation

Worked example · Describe before diagnosing
A coarse-looking strip appears near the right edge after three truck changes. The surrounding surface has
more fine matrix.

Record the repeated location and timing, photograph from a safe position with a scale and location label,
and notify the paving/quality lead. Compare with agreed criteria and inspect the process under authorized
conditions. Do not declare severe segregation or choose a repair from appearance alone.


  Write three useful observations and one unsupported conclusion for the case.

  Write three useful observations and one unsupported conclusion for the case.

  Answer guidance.

  Useful: right-edge location; repeated after three truck changes; reduced visible fine matrix relative to
  surroundings. Unsupported: “the driver caused it” without investigation.

  Observe: Writing, Problem solving through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation
  or saved work.




  In the dated Table 502-I-2, a 1 lane-km top lift has 4 slight areas, zero moderate and zero
  severe

  In the dated Table 502-I-2, a 1 lane-km top lift has 4 slight areas, zero moderate and zero severe.
  What is the isolated adjustment?

  Answer guidance.

  The row requiring 4–5 slight, 0 moderate and 0 severe gives +$500 per lane-km. All three columns
  and the clause conditions must apply. This does not remove remediation obligations.

  Observe: Numeracy, Reading through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or
  saved work.

Density

Worked example · Two legitimate references, two different percentages
In-place density is 2.340 Mg/m³. A fictional plant briquette result is 2.400 Mg/m³; maximum theoretical
density is 2.500 Mg/m³.

Marshall-style comparison: 2.340/2.400 × 100 = 97.50%. Maximum-theoretical comparison: 2.340/2.500
× 100 = 93.60%. Both calculations are arithmetic examples; use only the reference required for the actual
mixture and contract.


  For 2.325 Mg/m³ in place and 2.500 Mg/m³ maximum theoretical, calculate percent.

  For 2.325 Mg/m³ in place and 2.500 Mg/m³ maximum theoretical, calculate percent.

  Answer guidance.

  2.325/2.500 × 100 = 93.00%. Identify the reference as maximum theoretical density and retain the
  actual method and lot details.

  Observe: Numeracy, Reading through the learner’s actual contribution, calculation, explanation or
  saved work.




  A 400 t fictional Superpave lot average is 93.4%

  A 400 t fictional Superpave lot average is 93.4%. Find the isolated dated table adjustment, then name
  two checks still needed.
