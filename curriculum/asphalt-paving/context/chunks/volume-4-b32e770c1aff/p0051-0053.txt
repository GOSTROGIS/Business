Integrated practice · all nine Skills for Success



Check the grade and the quantity

    This is a complete fictional classroom case. It does not describe an actual community, Nation,
    employer or project. Learners may use the supplied case without disclosing personal or cultural
    knowledge.



A fictional rectangle is 80 m × 3.5 m. Assigned mix mass is 33.6 t and specified application rate is 120
kg/m². Benchmark EL is 100.000 m, backsight 1.300 m, foresight 1.720 m and target base EL 99.650 m. A
draft claims “the application rate proves the whole mat is accepted.” No density or smoothness results are
supplied.




  Reading · Check the facts
  Identify the controlling facts, missing information and one unsupported statement in the brief.




  Writing · Make the handover useful
  Draft a concise handover with location, quantities, status and the next responsible action.




  Numeracy · Calculate and check
  Show the case calculation with units and an independent reasonableness check.

   Change card · Use after the first attempt
   A corrected drawing changes width to 3.6 m before placement; the mass allocation has not been

Individual evidence record
Keep the brief, calculation, saved work, review findings and revision. Record each person’s actual
contribution and support. Use Not observed, Not yet demonstrated, Demonstrated with support, or
Demonstrated independently for the stated action; these are local feedback descriptions.

For AI/media work, a transcript, script or storyboard is distinct from a generated audio/video file. Name
what was actually produced and inspected. Do not upload private records or community-held material
without the relevant authority.
