Practise with supplied information

These are fictional classroom records. They are not labels or SDSs for a real product. Use the reference pages and linked CCOHS documents. No product handling is required.

Keep each learner’s source check, calculation, written note and revision. Use pointing or reading aloud for access, then observe the specific skill action being assessed.

WHMIS practice

WH-01 · Reading · Communication

Name the message

Find the flame, flame over circle, corrosion and gas cylinder. Explain one difference between the flame and flame over circle. Then locate the hot-surface warning in the CAT page. Which belongs to a machine warning system rather than a product classification?

WH-02 · Reading · Problem solving

Match the right document

Fictional desk records: container label = “Crew Clean A”, supplier = “North Training Supply”; SDS 1 = “Crew Clean B”, same supplier; SDS 2 = “Crew Clean A”, supplier = “West Training Supply”. Neither sheet has been checked against the container. Which sheet is confirmed? What information would you seek before the task?

WH-03 · Reading · Digital

Find the section

Open the CCOHS SDS sheet. Record which SDS sections address first aid, accidental release, handling/storage, exposure controls/PPE and revision information. Save your answers with the source URL and revision date; close and reopen the file.

WHMIS practice

WH-04 · Numeracy · Writing

Keep a count and report a gap

Fictional classroom audit: 12 labelled sample cards are supplied. Nine cards have a matching practice SDS; two have the wrong product name; one has no SDS. How many lack a confirmed match? What percentage is that? Write a brief note describing the gap without inventing a product hazard.

WH-05 · Problem solving · Communication

No symbol is not no hazard

A fictional learner says, “There is no diamond, so we can use it.” Use the pictograms sheet to check that statement. Explain your correction to a partner and ask for a check-back.

WH-06 · Adaptability · Collaboration · Creativity and innovation

Update a shared briefing

Fictional change: the supervisor replaces a practice SDS with a newly verified revision. In pairs, design two ways to make the revision visible on a briefing card. Choose one, test whether another reader can find the date and exact product, revise it and record both contributions. Keep official pictograms unchanged.
