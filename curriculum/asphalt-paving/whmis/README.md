# WHMIS and machine hazard symbols

**ACCESS IS4S · 30 October 2026**

[Visual reference](index.html) · [Reference PDF](whmis-reference.pdf) · [Six learner tasks](practice.html) · [Learner PDF](practice.pdf) · [Answer guidance](teaching-guide.html) · [Teaching PDF](teaching-guide.pdf) · [Complete classroom ZIP](whmis-classroom-pack.zip)

The repository contains the **ten official CCOHS pictograms as PNG, EPS and SVG**: the nine WHMIS pictogram families plus the environment pictogram, which is not mandatory under Canadian WHMIS. The original CCOHS download ZIP is preserved unchanged. SVGs were converted from the official EPS files without redesigning the symbols, border geometry or colours. The biohazard pictogram retains its circular border.

Seven source PDFs supply **218 pages**: five CCOHS sheets revised **28 May 2026**, and two WorkSafeBC explanatory books from **2019**. The current CCOHS sheets reflect the December 2022 federal HPR amendments. The older B.C. books are explicitly marked historical; current workplace requirements remain applicable.

Two complete CAT roller safety pages show equipment warnings for hot surfaces, crush hazards and ROPS. Their machine, publication and source pages are identified beside each reproduction. They are classroom references, not replacement machine decals.

WH-01–06 provide complete fictional practice records and separate answers. Across the sequence, learners read and explain symbols, match a product and SDS, save and reopen a source note, calculate a document-match gap, check back with a partner and revise a briefing aid. These are classroom learning materials; workplace-specific WHMIS education/training and equipment qualification are separate requirements.
