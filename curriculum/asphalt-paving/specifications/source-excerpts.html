Clause 501.15.03 · Grading Curve

PDF pages 286–287, printed 3–4 of 13. The opening sentence spans the page boundary:

Once the grading curve is established within the above limits, the maximum permissible variation of the mean of any four consecutive tests from the grading curve shall be within the tolerance specified in Table 501-F.

The paragraph immediately following Table 501-F begins:

These tolerances do not waive the requirement that the running average of four consecutive tests must be maintained at all times inside the limits specified in DBSS 501.15.01.

The rest of that paragraph discusses refinements to maintain the curve and a variation cushion within the limits. The exercise does not instruct a learner to make a real mix-design adjustment.

Clause 502.04.04 · Delivery records

PDF pages 299–300, Section 502, printed 3–4 of 22. This excerpt begins with the clause's second paragraph and continues with the five-item list on the next page:

For the purposes of confirming delivery of asphalt mix to the road and the calculation of material application rates, the Contractor shall consolidate the Road Checker’s Summary and the weigh tickets for each load received at the paving operation at the end of each shift. The Road Checker’s Summary shall include, but not be limited to, the following information:

a) Truck Number

b) Weigh Ticket Number and Net Weight of load

c) Time and location by station of delivery

d) Material Application Rate Dimensions and Calculations. Calculation frequency minimally shall be for every 10 loads.

e) Notes pertaining to the paving of any appurtenances (letdowns, intersections, tapers, etc.)

The classroom version/checker/correction-reason fields are additional teaching choices. They are not represented as text from this list.

Clause 502.14.01 · Material application rate

PDF page 309, Section 502, printed 13 of 22:

Asphalt mix shall be applied to the roadway at the rate or rates specified in the Design, in conformance with the Contract. Material Application Rates will be determined by the tonnage delivered to the paver as recorded by weigh tickets generated by automated scales, divided by the area covered by the Lot after allowance has been made for entrance letdowns and/or intersections. The Contractor shall provide the material application rate calculations to the Quality Manager at end of each shift.

The workbook uses the mass/area relationship for an invented ten-load check block. It does not designate that block as a Lot or apply the payment and rejection provisions following this clause. The target of 200 kg/m² is supplied by the fictional design brief.

The work shall consist of shaping and compacting the existing granular base course to the designated grade and cross-section.

This work consists of preparing a foundation for placement of new surfacing by cold milling the existing pavement surface to the depth and width as indicated in the Contract and the Design.
