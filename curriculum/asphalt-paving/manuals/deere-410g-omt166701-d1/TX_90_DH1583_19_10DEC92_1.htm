Do Not Service Injection Nozzles

IMPORTANT: Do not service or remove injection nozzles. The service life of the injection nozzles may be shortened by:

  • Overheating

  • Improper operation

  • Poor quality fuel

  • Excessive idling

If injection nozzles are not working correctly or are dirty, the engine will not run normally. (See your authorized dealer for service.)
