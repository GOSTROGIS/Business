Operating on a Slope

CAUTION: To avoid injury from machine rollover, DO NOT turn while operating on slopes.

Driving straight up or down slope with loader and backhoe bucket near ground and dipperstick retracted:

With front bucket loaded:

  • Level or slightly retract bucket when driving up slope.
  • Fully retract bucket when driving down slope.

With front bucket unloaded:

  • Level bucket when driving up slope keeping bucket raised high enough to prevent digging into ground.
  • Point bucket down slope and drive slow when traveling down slope.
