Lubricate Front Wheel Bearings-Non-Powered

IMPORTANT: Lubricate daily when machine is used in mud.

1. Remove plug and install lubrication fitting.

2. Grease with approximately five low pressure strokes of grease. (See Fuels and Lubricants chapter for recommended type of grease.)

3. Remove lubrication fitting and install plug.
