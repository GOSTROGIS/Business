Draining Fuel Filter Sediment

Drain water and sediment as necessary.

1. Loosen knob (B) and drain fuel for several seconds from drain tube (A).

2. Tighten knob.

3. Bleed fuel system. (See Change Fuel Filter in Maintenance-Every 500 Hours chapter.)
