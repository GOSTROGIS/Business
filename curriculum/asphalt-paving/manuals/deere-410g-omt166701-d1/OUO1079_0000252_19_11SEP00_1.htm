Preparing to Operate Backhoe

IMPORTANT: Prevent possible machine damage. Do not operate machine when monitor is inoperable, malfunctioning, or does not successfully complete self test.

1. Back machine close to desired digging location and park on flat ground.

2. Move FNR/range lever to NEUTRAL. Engage park brake.

3. Position loader bucket on ground and lower boom until front wheels rise off ground. (See Operating Front Loader in this chapter.)

4. Pivot seat to backhoe operation position. (See Adjusting Seat in Operator's Station chapter.)

5. Lower stabilizers until rear tires are just off the ground to level the machine. (See Operating Stabilizers in this chapter.)

6. Remove swing locking pin and install pin in storage position. (See Swing Locking Pin in Operator's Station chapter.)

7. Disengage backhoe boom lock. (See Boom Lock Control in Operator's Station chapter.)

8. Remove extendible dipperstick locking pin, if equipped, and install pin in storage position. (See Extendible Dipperstick Locking Pin-If Equipped in Operator's Station chapter.)

9. Check backhoe levers for proper clearance. (See Backhoe Valve Linkage Adjustment in this chapter.)
