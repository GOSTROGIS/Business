Windshield Wipers and Washer Reservoir-If Equipped

IMPORTANT: To avoid damage to wiper blades, DO NOT operate wipers if blades are frozen to windshield.

1. Push and hold front windshield washer switch (A) to operate front windshield washer.

2. Push front windshield wiper switch (B) to operate front windshield wiper. Front windshield wiper switch has three positions:

  • Push lower half to turn front windshield wiper OFF.
  • Push to middle position to turn front windshield wiper on SLOW.
  • Push upper half to turn front windshield wiper on FAST.

3. Push right half of rear windshield wiper switch (C) to turn rear windshield wiper ON. Push left half of rear windshield wiper switch to turn rear windshield wiper OFF.

4. Open engine hood and add windshield washer fluid at windshield washer reservoir cap (D) as needed.


A-Front Windshield Washer Switch
B-Front Windshield Wiper Switch
C-Rear Windshield Wiper Switch
D-Windshield Washer Reservoir Cap
