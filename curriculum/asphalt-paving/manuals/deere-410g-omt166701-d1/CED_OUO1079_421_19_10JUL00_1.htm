Drain Fuel Tank Water and Sediment

CAUTION: Handle fuel carefully. If the engine is hot or running, DO NOT drain fuel tank. DO NOT smoke while you drain fuel tank or work on fuel system.

NOTE: Dispose of waste properly.

1. Loosen sediment drain plug (A) on front of fuel tank and allow fuel to flow for several seconds.

2. Tighten plug securely to stop fuel flow.
