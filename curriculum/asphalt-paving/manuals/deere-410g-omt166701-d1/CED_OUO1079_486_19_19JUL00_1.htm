Check and Adjust Engine Speed Control Linkage

1. Move speed control lever to SLOW IDLE.

2. Measure injection pump lever override. Override must be to specification.

Specification

Forward Position (Slow Idle) Injection Pump Override -Distance 3-6 mm (1/8-1/4 in.)

3. Adjust yoke on speed control rod to provide override.

4. Move speed control lever to FAST IDLE and check override. Override must be to specification.

Specification

Rear Position (Fast Idle) Injection Pump Override -Distance 1.5 mm (1/16 in.) Minimum

5. Check high idle override again.

If engine speed control pedal position requires adjustment, see your authorized dealer.
