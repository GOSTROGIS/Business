Work at Proper Angles

Work perpendicular or at an angle to stockpile to prevent highest part of pile from collapsing onto machine. Use backhoe bucket to remove material that may fall onto machine.

Work perpendicular or at an angle to excavation to prevent cave-ins. Do not operate near the edge of an excavation or trench.

Use backhoe bucket to fill areas where cave-ins occur.
