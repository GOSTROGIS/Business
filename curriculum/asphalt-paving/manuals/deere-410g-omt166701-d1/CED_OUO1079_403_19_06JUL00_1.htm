Air Suspension Seat Height Adjustment Switch-If Equipped

Push upper half of air suspension seat height adjustment switch (A) to increase air suspension seat height and firmness of ride.

Push lower half of air suspension seat height adjustment switch to decrease air suspension seat height and firmness of ride.

See Adjusting Seat in this chapter for information about other seat adjustment controls.
