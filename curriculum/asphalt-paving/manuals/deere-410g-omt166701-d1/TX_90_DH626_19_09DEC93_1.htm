Do Not Adjust Injection Pump

IMPORTANT: Never steam clean or pour cold water on an injection pump while the engine is running, or while injection pump is still warm. To do so may cause seizure of pump.

Clean trash regularly from injection pump.

Adjusting the injection pump in any way not approved by the manufacturer will end the warranty. (See your copy of the John Deere warranty on this machine.)

Do not attempt to service an injection pump that is not operating correctly. (See your authorized injection pump service center.)
