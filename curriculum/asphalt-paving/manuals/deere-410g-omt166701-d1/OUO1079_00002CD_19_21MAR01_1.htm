Change Engine Oil and Replace Filter

IMPORTANT: If fuel sulfur content exceeds 0.5 percent, change engine oil at 1/2 the normal interval.

If engine has not run 250 hours before the season changes, change oil.

CAUTION: Prevent possible injury from unexpected machine movement. Never rely on FNR/range lever to keep machine from moving. Always engage park brake to hold machine.

1. Run engine to warm oil. Park machine on a level surface. Engage park brake. (See Prepare Machine for Maintenance in Periodic Maintenance chapter.)

NOTE: Dispose of waste oil properly.

2. Remove engine oil drain plug (A). Allow oil to drain into a container.

3. Using a suitable filter wrench, turn engine oil filter (B) counterclockwise and remove from base. Clean mounting surfaces of base as necessary.

4. Apply a thin film of oil to sealing ring on new engine oil filter, and install by turning new filter clockwise by hand until gasket touches mounting surface. Torque an additional 1/2-3/4 turn with filter wrench.

5. Install engine oil drain plug.

6. Remove engine oil fill cap (C) and fill engine with oil to specification. (See Fuels and Lubricants chapter for proper type of oil to use.)

IMPORTANT: Before starting engine after a filter change, crank engine for ten seconds without starting to fill new engine oil filter and pre-lubricate the turbocharger.

7. Disconnect fuel pump electrical connector (D) to keep engine from starting. Turn key switch to START and hold for ten seconds.

8. Connect fuel pump electrical connector, start engine, and run at a slow idle. If engine oil pressure light on monitor does not go out and alarm does not shut off, stop engine immediately and find the cause.

9. Stop engine. Check for leaks around drain plug and filter, and tighten as necessary. Check oil level. (See Check Engine Oil Level in Maintenance-Every 10 Hours or Daily chapter.)
