Check Instruments After Starting

IMPORTANT: Prevent possible machine damage. Do not operate machine when monitor is inoperable, malfunctioning, or does not successfully complete self test.

If STOP indicator does not go out within ten seconds after starting engine, STOP THE ENGINE and correct the problem before restarting.

NOTE: Cold oil may cause hydraulic oil filter restriction indicator to light temporarily.

With park brake engaged and engine running, check gauges and instrument panel.
