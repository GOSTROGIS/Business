Check Engine Speeds

1. Warm engine to normal operating temperature.

2. Use tachometer (A) to check engine speeds:

Specification

Slow Idle -Speed 850 rpm
Fast Idle (Using Speed Control Pedal or Lever) -Speed 2375 ± 25 rpm

Adjust idle speeds if necessary. (See Check and Adjust Engine Speed Control Linkage in this chapter.)
