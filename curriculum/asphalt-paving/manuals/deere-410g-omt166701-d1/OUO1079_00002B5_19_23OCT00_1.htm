Park Brake Indicator

Park brake indicator will light when park brake is engaged.

Park brake indicator and STOP indicator will light and alarm will sound if FNR/range lever is shifted into FORWARD or REVERSE while park brake is ON.

Park brake indicator and STOP indicator will light and alarm will sound if FNR/range lever is shifted into FORWARD or REVERSE while park brake is OFF and seat is rotated toward the backhoe position.

See Park Brake Switch in this chapter for information on park brake operation.
