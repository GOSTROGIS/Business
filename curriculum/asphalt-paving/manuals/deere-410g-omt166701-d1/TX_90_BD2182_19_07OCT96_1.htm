Remove and Install Halogen Bulb

1. Remove screws (A), cover (B), lamp (C), and lamp housing (E).

2. Disconnect wiring lead and release retainer clip to remove bulb (D).

CAUTION: Do not touch the halogen bulb with bare hands. Oil and moisture may cause premature bulb failure. Clean bulb glass if touched, using an oil-free cloth with alcohol.

3. Install new bulb, lamp and assemble housing.


A-Screw (2 used)
B-Lamp Cover
C-Lamp
D-Bulb
E-Lamp Housing
