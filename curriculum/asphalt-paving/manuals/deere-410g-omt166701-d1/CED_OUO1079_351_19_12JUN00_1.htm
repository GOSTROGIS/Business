Mechanical Front Wheel Drive (MFWD) Switch and Indicator-If Equipped

Push mechanical front wheel drive (MFWD) switch (A) UP to engage the MFWD axle.

Push mechanical front wheel drive (MFWD) switch DOWN to disengage the MFWD axle.

The mechanical front wheel drive (MFWD) indicator (B) will light when the MFWD axle is engaged.


A-Mechanical Front Wheel Drive (MFWD) Switch
B-Mechanical Front Wheel Drive (MFWD) Indicator
