Avoid Mud Build-Up

A clean machine is easier to service. Clean the following areas as required, especially when mud begins to build up.

  • Stabilizer hoses going through main frame
  • Between rear tires and fenders
  • Backhoe cylinders
  • Backhoe pivot points and hoses
  • Loader cylinders, pivot points and hoses
  • Front axle kingpins
