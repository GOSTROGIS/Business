Stalled Engine

IMPORTANT: Engine damage may occur due to overheating after stalling when working under load. Follow restart procedure to keep coolant circulating through engine.

RESTART WARM ENGINE: Immediately remove load and restart engine. Run engine for 30 seconds at 1/3 idle before adding load and resuming work. (See Stopping the Engine-Normal Shutdown in this chapter if machine is to be stopped after restarting stalled warm engine.)
