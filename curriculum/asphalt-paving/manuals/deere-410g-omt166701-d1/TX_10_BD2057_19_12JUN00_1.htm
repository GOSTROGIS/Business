Hydraulic Oil Filter Restriction Indicator

IMPORTANT: If hydraulic oil filter restriction indicator lights and hydraulic oil is warm, change filter as soon as possible to prevent damage. (See Replace Hydraulic Oil Filter in Maintenance-Every 500 Hours chapter.)

NOTE: Cold oil can cause indicator to light temporarily. Allow time for oil to warm up and indicator to go out before servicing filter.

Indicator will light when hydraulic oil filter element is restricted.
