Dozing and Plowing

DOZING: With bucket parallel to ground, remove as little dirt as possible from top surface. Let dirt spill from partially dumped bucket to fill in low spots.

PLOWING: Place boom in float position and bucket in dig position to prevent gouging surface (e.g.-cleaning concrete and snow removal). Also use float position to avoid mixing surface and stockpile material.
