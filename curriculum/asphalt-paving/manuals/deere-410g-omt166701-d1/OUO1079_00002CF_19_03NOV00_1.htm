Changing Reverse Warning Alarm Volume

IMPORTANT: It may be necessary to adjust the volume to meet local regulations.

Setting High Volume

1. Remove nuts from positive (+) high-volume terminal (C) and positive (+) low-volume terminal (D).

2. Remove positive (+) wire (E) from positive (+) low-volume terminal.

3. Install shorting bar to connect positive (+) terminals.

4. Install positive (+) wire on positive (+) high-volume terminal.

5. Install nuts on positive (+) high-volume terminal and positive (+) low-volume terminal.

Setting Low Volume

1. Remove nuts from positive (+) high-volume terminal (C) and positive (+) low-volume terminal (D).

2. Remove positive (+) wire (E) from positive (+) high-volume terminal.

3. Remove shorting bar from positive (+) terminals.

4. Install positive (+) wire on positive (+) low-volume terminal.

5. Install nuts on positive (+) high-volume terminal and positive (+) low-volume terminal.
