Transmission and Torque Converter Oil Temperature Gauge

Transmission and torque converter oil temperature gauge needle will enter red zone (A), STOP indicator will light, and alarm will sound when transmission oil temperature is above 121°C (250°F).

Reduce load immediately, shift into NEUTRAL, and run engine at slow idle. Inspect for plugged oil cooler.
