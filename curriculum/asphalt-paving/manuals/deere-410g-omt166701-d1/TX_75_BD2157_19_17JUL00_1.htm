Check MFWD Planetary Housing Oil-If Equipped

1. Rotate housing until OIL LEVEL line (B) is horizontal and fill plug (A) is above line as shown.

NOTE: MFWD is filled with API GL-5 gear oil at the factory. If oil level is low, use HY-GARD oil to fill to proper level.

2. Remove plug and check that oil is filled to bottom of plug hole.

3. Add oil to plug hole if necessary. (See Fuels and Lubricants chapter for proper type of oil.)

4. Install plug.

5. Repeat procedure for opposite side wheel.


A-MFWD Planetary Housing Oil Fill Plug
B-Oil Level Line
