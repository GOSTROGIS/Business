# Section 4 resource requirements and remaining access gaps

## What the uploaded outline actually lists

The uploaded August 2016 *Asphalt Paving/Laydown Technician Program Outline* identifies **Section 4 — Training Provider Standards**. Its **Reference Materials** page is **printed page 105, PDF viewer page 107**. Under Required Reference Materials it lists an Instructor Guide and PowerPoint slides alongside these six course-manual categories: **Common Core, Raker, Compaction Roller, Screed, Paver and Milling Machine**.

It also lists the **2006 Design Build Specifications**, **The Asphalt Handbook MS-4, 7th Edition**, two Caterpillar interactive training CDs—**Fundamentals of Asphalt Paving** and **Fundamentals of Asphalt Compaction**—and WorkSafeBC online resources. The outline states that the CDs can be purchased. This is the outline's historical resource list, not confirmation of present stock, pricing or delivery format.

Section 4 does not name John Deere. It does not explicitly identify the six named course manuals as manufacturer operator manuals. This pack adds genuine OEM references for the requested curriculum work while keeping the course-resource requirement visible.

## How the files were selected

The six OEM PDFs were retrieved from manufacturer-hosted pages or manufacturer-branded content delivery URLs. Their first pages identify actual operation, safety or maintenance publications. Dealer brochures, product specification sheets, search-result snippets and short previews are not counted as complete manuals. The manifest records exact URLs, bytes, SHA-256, page count, model/serial scope and dated edition evidence.

Files retain original manufacturer notices. Public download availability does not make a manual open-licensed, and the authored ACCESS lesson does not change the manufacturer's rights. The short extract files contain cover identity and original navigation guidance; operational chapters have not been rewritten as generic instructions. The pack remains a dated reference collection whose applicability must be checked against actual equipment and current documentation.
