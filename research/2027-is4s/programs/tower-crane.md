# Tower Crane — check a simulated planning information pack

[All programs](README.md) · [Teaching framework](../framework.md) · [Worked numeracy starters](../numeracy-starters.md)

**Research stage:** Initial learning-design draft. Activities are proposed for ACCESS/instructor review. Cohort and public-program details come from [the supplied reference](../sources/supplied-program-reference-2026-10-22.txt), section “Tower Crane”, and have not been independently confirmed.

**Supplied context:** July 5–August 27, 2027; Trades; partner recorded as “IOUE Maple Ridge.” Verify the partner name, course level and the supplied document's July 2027 certification-transition claim.
**Integrated challenge:** Review a provider-authored fictional planning pack, identify missing or conflicting information and deliver a clear hold-for-clarification handover. The task does not approve or execute a lift.
**Inputs to request:** Trainer-approved scenario, explicitly educational chart extracts, equipment terminology, communication protocol and known-answer checking sheet.

| Skill | Proposed activity → observable evidence |
|---|---|
| Reading | Locate the specified row, column, units and notes in an educational chart → accurately annotated extract, including conditions the learner must clarify. |
| Writing | Record missing information and who needs to clarify it → concise issue log without inventing operational values. |
| Numeracy | Practise unit conversion and checking supplied quantities in a classroom example → workings with units, independent check and clear identification of uncertainty. |
| Digital | Select the assigned scenario revision and complete its planning checklist → saved record tied to the correct source version. |
| Problem solving | Recognize a conflict between two fictional records → explanation of why the task must pause and which qualified person should resolve it. |
| Communication | Use provider-taught closed-loop communication in a role-play → clear message, accurate repeat-back and confirmation. |
| Collaboration | Rotate information checker, communicator and recorder roles → a shared issue list with individual contributions observed. |
| Adaptability | Respond to an instructor-issued change in conditions → withdraw the previous classroom plan and list what must be reassessed. |
| Creativity and innovation | Improve the readability of the training checklist → prototype that makes missing information easier to notice without altering technical rules. |

**Accessible entry / stretch:** Begin with one chart row and a terminology key; extend to reconciling several documents and explaining why an apparently complete pack is insufficient.
**Evidence bundle:** Annotated educational chart, calculation practice, issue log and observed role-play; no operational approval inferred.
**Research/provider questions:** Is the partner IUOE Local 115, and which level is offered? How do the July 2027 rules affect intake and placement? Which registration, sponsor and qualified-supervision requirements apply?
