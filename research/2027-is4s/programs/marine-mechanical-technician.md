# Marine Mechanical Technician — investigate a simulated service complaint

[All programs](README.md) · [Teaching framework](../framework.md) · [Worked numeracy starters](../numeracy-starters.md)

**Research stage:** Initial learning-design draft. Activities are proposed for ACCESS/instructor review. Cohort and public-program details come from [the supplied reference](../sources/supplied-program-reference-2026-10-22.txt), section “Marine Mechanical Technician”, and have not been independently confirmed.

**Supplied context:** July 19–September 10, 2027; Trades; BCIT. Confirm the cohort's equipment and outcomes separately from the full foundation and apprenticeship pathways.
**Integrated challenge:** Use a fictional service complaint, an approved manual extract and supplied test results to build an evidence-based troubleshooting record and customer handover.
**Inputs to request:** Equipment-specific teaching extract, fictional work order, instructor-generated readings, parts information and an approved diagnostic sequence; no independent work on live systems.

| Skill | Proposed activity → observable evidence |
|---|---|
| Reading | Locate the correct model's instructions and relevant diagram → references marked with model and document version. |
| Writing | Separate reported symptoms, supplied observations and suggested next checks → structured service note with no unsupported diagnosis. |
| Numeracy | Compare simulated readings with instructor-provided reference values → table with units, differences and clear flags for further review. |
| Digital | Navigate the assigned manual and enter a fictional service record → saved record that cites the exact source section. |
| Problem solving | Rank plausible explanations against the supplied evidence → justified next-check plan within the instructor's permitted sequence. |
| Communication | Explain findings to a fictional customer using plain language → explanation distinguishes what is known from what remains to be checked. |
| Collaboration | Divide document lookup, evidence recording and checking → shared record showing how conflicting interpretations were resolved. |
| Adaptability | Revise an explanation when new simulated evidence arrives → updated reasoning that explicitly addresses the new information. |
| Creativity and innovation | Design a clearer symptom-to-evidence recording aid → template that another learner can use accurately. |

**Accessible entry / stretch:** Supply a labelled diagram, short manual extract and one symptom; extend to multiple possible causes or comparing two approved document revisions.
**Evidence bundle:** Source annotations, readings table, troubleshooting record and customer-handover observation.
**Research/provider questions:** Which engines and electrical/mechanical systems are taught? What equipment manuals and safe diagnostic procedures are authorized? How are workshop access, environmental handling and progression assessed?
