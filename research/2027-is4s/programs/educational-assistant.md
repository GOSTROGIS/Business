# Educational Assistant — support an accessible classroom activity

[All programs](README.md) · [Teaching framework](../framework.md) · [Worked numeracy starters](../numeracy-starters.md)

**Research stage:** Initial learning-design draft. Activities are proposed for ACCESS/instructor review. Cohort and public-program details come from [the supplied reference](../sources/supplied-program-reference-2026-10-22.txt), section “Educational Assistant”, and have not been independently confirmed.

**Supplied context:** November 8–December 17, 2027; Programs; provider TBD. The supplied Douglas program is occupational context, not confirmation of the six-week cohort's credential or practicum.
**Integrated challenge:** Using a fictional learner profile and teacher-provided goal, prepare an accessible classroom activity, collect neutral observations and suggest an adjustment for teacher review.
**Inputs to request:** Fictional profile, teacher-directed goal, activity materials, observation form and approved privacy/role guidance; avoid real learner records or invented cultural information.

| Skill | Proposed activity → observable evidence |
|---|---|
| Reading | Locate the teacher's goal, learner preferences and agreed supports → plan that accurately cites those details. |
| Writing | Record observable actions and the support offered → neutral notes that avoid diagnosis, judgement or unsupported interpretations. |
| Numeracy | Plan time and materials, then summarize fictional observation counts → checked schedule or table using appropriate units and clear labels. |
| Digital | Create an accessible visual schedule or practice resource → readable file with text alternatives and sensible document structure where supported. |
| Problem solving | Identify a barrier between the activity and stated goal → proposed adjustment that stays within the teacher-directed plan. |
| Communication | Model clear instructions and check understanding in a role-play → learner choice and comprehension are explicitly checked. |
| Collaboration | Coordinate with a role-play teacher and another support worker → agreed responsibilities and a concise handover. |
| Adaptability | Adjust pacing or presentation after the fictional learner's feedback → revised activity explaining the observed reason for the change. |
| Creativity and innovation | Offer two ways to engage with the same learning goal → practical alternatives that preserve access and learner dignity. |

**Accessible entry / stretch:** Provide a completed example and short profile; extend to balancing two access needs or explaining why an appealing adaptation does not meet the stated goal.
**Evidence bundle:** Activity plan, accessible resource, neutral observation note and proposed revision for teacher review.
**Research/provider questions:** Who delivers and recognizes this cohort? What practicum, screening and role expectations apply? Which inclusive-practice resources, assistive technologies and learner-support arrangements will be used?
