# Carpentry — estimate and revise a small project

[All programs](README.md) · [Teaching framework](../framework.md) · [Worked numeracy starters](../numeracy-starters.md)

**Research stage:** Initial learning-design draft. Activities are proposed for ACCESS/instructor review. Cohort and public-program details come from [the supplied reference](../sources/supplied-program-reference-2026-10-22.txt), section “Carpentry”, and have not been independently confirmed.

**Supplied context:** March 22–May 14, 2027; Trades; BCIT. Confirm the cohort's project and credit separately from foundation or apprentice technical training.
**Integrated challenge:** Develop a cut list and materials estimate for an instructor-approved small practice project, build a paper/card model and explain the effect of a client-requested change.
**Inputs to request:** Approved drawing, stock sizes, fictional prices, cut-list template and instructor-defined allowances; any actual tool work follows the partner's teaching plan.

| Skill | Proposed activity → observable evidence |
|---|---|
| Reading | Extract sizes and notes from a project drawing and materials list → annotated plan with corresponding parts identified. |
| Writing | Write a short work sequence and clarification request → ordered steps and a question precise enough for the instructor to answer. |
| Numeracy | Calculate lengths, counts and a materials subtotal → checked cut list and estimate with units and stated allowances. |
| Digital | Use a spreadsheet to recalculate a fictional estimate → saved sheet that updates correctly when one quantity changes. |
| Problem solving | Identify why the available stock cannot satisfy the first cut plan → revised plan using approved stock and allowance rules. |
| Communication | Explain the project estimate and confirm the requested change → accurate summary of agreed dimensions and unresolved details. |
| Collaboration | Pair a planner with an independent checker, then swap → documented correction and shared final plan. |
| Adaptability | Revise the list after a change in size or available stock → traceable before-and-after quantity comparison. |
| Creativity and innovation | Compare two permitted layouts for clarity or material use → sketches and a reasoned choice within the design requirements. |

**Accessible entry / stretch:** Offer a labelled model, measurement demonstration and a partly completed cut list; extend to fractions, a second stock length or comparing total costs.
**Evidence bundle:** Cut list, calculation sheet, model, change note and explanation of a checking strategy.
