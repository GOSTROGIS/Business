# Nursing — organize a simulated care-information handover

[All programs](README.md) · [Teaching framework](../framework.md) · [Worked numeracy starters](../numeracy-starters.md)

**Research stage:** Initial learning-design draft. Activities are proposed for ACCESS/instructor review. Cohort and public-program details come from [the supplied reference](../sources/supplied-program-reference-2026-10-22.txt), section “Nursing”, and have not been independently confirmed.

**Supplied context:** Dates TBD; Programs; BCIT. The supplied reference pathway is the BSN; confirm the ACCESS cohort's dates, preparation stage and teaching scope with BCIT.
**Integrated challenge:** Use a fictional, instructor-authored patient case to organize information, check basic numerical records and provide a structured handover; identify gaps for a supervising instructor to resolve.
**Inputs to request:** Fictional case, instructor-approved documentation and handover format, simulated chart and known-answer numeracy examples appropriate to the learners' training stage.

| Skill | Proposed activity → observable evidence |
|---|---|
| Reading | Locate the case's relevant information, instructions and unknowns → annotated source and a list distinguishing known facts from missing data. |
| Writing | Draft a factual simulated note using the taught format → clear entry that avoids unsupported conclusions and preserves the source of information. |
| Numeracy | Check times, units and totals in an instructor-supplied simulated record → workings, units and independent check; flag discrepancies rather than infer treatment. |
| Digital | Enter information in a training chart or mock form → accurate record using fictional identifiers and approved privacy practices. |
| Problem solving | Notice conflicting or missing information and prioritize clarification → explicit account of the concern and appropriate instructor/supervisor escalation. |
| Communication | Give a structured handover and check understanding respectfully → concise exchange that includes the uncertainty and requested next step. |
| Collaboration | Practise complementary recorder, reviewer and communicator roles → jointly checked handover with individual contributions observed. |
| Adaptability | Revise the information summary after a new simulated observation → updated account that retains the original facts and records the change. |
| Creativity and innovation | Improve a plain-language teaching aid for the fictional case → prototype reviewed for accessibility and accuracy by the instructor. |

**Accessible entry / stretch:** Start with short records, a vocabulary aid and one numerical check; extend to multiple documents and competing information needs. Medication calculations require a separate instructor-approved teaching and assessment sequence.
**Evidence bundle:** Annotated case, simulated note, numerical checks, discrepancy log and observed handover; these do not establish clinical competence or licensure.
**Research/provider questions:** What stage of BSN preparation is the ACCESS cohort addressing? Which current prerequisites and clinical-readiness deadlines apply? Which term-specific resources, simulation tools, cultural-safety learning and accessibility supports are approved?
