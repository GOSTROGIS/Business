**Supplied context:** February 1–March 12, 2027; Trades; provider not identified. A public asphalt program is a reference, not confirmation of this six-week cohort's content.
**Integrated challenge:** Use an instructor-approved site sketch to estimate a practice paving area, reconcile fictional delivery tickets and brief a team about a weather-related scheduling change.
**Inputs to request:** Simplified site drawing, fictional delivery tickets, approved calculation assumptions, equipment vocabulary and a provider-authored weather scenario.

| Skill | Proposed activity → observable evidence |
|---|---|
| Reading | Find dimensions and delivery details across a plan and tickets → highlighted fields linked to the materials plan. |
| Writing | Record delivery discrepancies and a revised work sequence → concise log that records facts and flags unanswered questions. |
| Numeracy | Calculate area and, where taught, volume using an instructor-supplied depth → worked estimate with consistent units; use supplied factors only for further conversions. |
| Digital | Enter ticket quantities in a table and compare planned with recorded totals → saved reconciliation sheet with checked formulas. |
| Problem solving | Investigate why two tickets do not match the planned order → identify plausible causes and information needed before choosing an explanation. |
| Communication | Give a short change-of-plan briefing using plain language → listener accurately identifies the changed time and their next task. |
| Collaboration | Allocate planner, ticket checker and briefing roles → shared plan with a completed peer check. |
| Adaptability | Reorder classroom planning tasks after a weather or delivery update → revised sequence that preserves instructor-specified constraints. |
| Creativity and innovation | Design a clearer ticket-checking aid → trial checklist that helps another learner find a planted discrepancy. |

**Accessible entry / stretch:** Start with rectangular areas, colour-coded tickets and a calculator; extend to composite shapes or comparing quantities across several deliveries.
**Evidence bundle:** Site calculation, delivery log, discrepancy explanation, revised schedule and brief-back observation.
