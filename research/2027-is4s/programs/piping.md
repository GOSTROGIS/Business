# Piping — interpret a route and prepare a materials list

[All programs](README.md) · [Teaching framework](../framework.md) · [Worked numeracy starters](../numeracy-starters.md)

**Research stage:** Initial learning-design draft. Activities are proposed for ACCESS/instructor review. Cohort and public-program details come from [the supplied reference](../sources/supplied-program-reference-2026-10-22.txt), section “Piping”, and have not been independently confirmed.

**Supplied context:** May 17–July 9, 2027; Trades; UAPIC. Confirm the balance of Plumbing, Steamfitting and Sprinklerfitting and any stated credit for this cohort.
**Integrated challenge:** Interpret a simplified instructor-approved pipe route, create a tabletop model and prepare a parts list; revise it after an approved route change.
**Inputs to request:** Schematic, labelled sample fittings or cards, manufacturer/provider reference extracts and instructor-supplied measurement conventions. Use no live pressure, gas or connected systems.

| Skill | Proposed activity → observable evidence |
|---|---|
| Reading | Match symbols, fitting labels and schematic notes → annotated route showing which source identifies each component. |
| Writing | Record a parts request and a route-change note → complete identifiers, quantities and clear description of the change. |
| Numeracy | Measure route segments and count fittings; apply instructor-taught allowances only where provided → checked length and quantity schedule. |
| Digital | Find an assigned part in an approved digital catalogue → saved reference with the correct identifier and relevant specification marked. |
| Problem solving | Identify a missing specification or incompatible model component → clear stop-and-clarify question supported by the references. |
| Communication | Describe the proposed route to a partner and confirm terminology → partner reconstructs the intended tabletop route accurately. |
| Collaboration | Share schematic reading, model assembly and checking → jointly reconciled model and parts list. |
| Adaptability | Update the model after the instructor changes one route segment → revised drawing and list agree with each other. |
| Creativity and innovation | Improve a parts-identification or route-explanation aid → prototype tested for clarity by another learner. |

**Accessible entry / stretch:** Begin with colour-coded fittings and direct lengths; extend to an additional branch or instructor-selected offsets after the necessary teaching.
**Evidence bundle:** Annotated route, parts list, measurement sheet, tabletop model and change record.
**Research/provider questions:** Which trades, materials and joining methods are included? What codes/modules are supplied? What registration, supervision and assessment arrangements apply to any workplace component?
