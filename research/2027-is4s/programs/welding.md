# Welding — plan and document a practice assembly

[All programs](README.md) · [Teaching framework](../framework.md) · [Worked numeracy starters](../numeracy-starters.md)

**Research stage:** Initial learning-design draft. Activities are proposed for ACCESS/instructor review. Cohort and public-program details come from [the supplied reference](../sources/supplied-program-reference-2026-10-22.txt), section “Welding”, and have not been independently confirmed.

**Supplied context:** January 25–March 19, 2027; Trades; UAPIC. Confirm the actual eight-week scope and assessment outcomes separately from a full foundation program.
**Integrated challenge:** In pairs, prepare a materials plan and job record for an instructor-selected, non-structural practice assembly; explain the drawing, check quantities and revise after an approved design change.
**Inputs to request:** Approved drawing, sample job card, material list, measurement tools and vocabulary sheet; process and safety instructions supplied by the welding instructor.

| Skill | Proposed activity → observable evidence |
|---|---|
| Reading | Locate dimensions, drawing notes and relevant instructions → annotated drawing with the source of each required detail marked. |
| Writing | Complete a job card with sequence, observations and questions → clear, dated record distinguishing observations from assumptions. |
| Numeracy | Measure practice pieces and total cut lengths using one unit system → calculation sheet with units and a reasonableness check. |
| Digital | Enter quantities in a simple materials spreadsheet and save the correct drawing revision → named file with checked entries and revision identification. |
| Problem solving | Detect a mismatch between the drawing and materials list → explanation of the mismatch and the question that must be resolved before work. |
| Communication | Explain the planned sequence and request clarification of an unfamiliar symbol → partner can restate the task and unresolved issue. |
| Collaboration | Rotate reader, measurer and checker roles → jointly checked plan plus a note of each learner's contribution. |
| Adaptability | Respond to an instructor-issued dimension change → updated quantities and explicit identification of affected steps. |
| Creativity and innovation | Suggest a clearer job-card layout or material-identification method → small prototype and explanation of how it reduces confusion. |

**Accessible entry / stretch:** Begin with a full-size diagram, labelled sample and whole-number measurements; extend to mixed measurements, an extra component or comparing two permitted stock layouts.
**Evidence bundle:** Annotated drawing, quantity sheet, job card, revision note and learner reflection identifying one transferable skill.
**Research/provider questions:** Which process, materials, joint types and drawing conventions are in the cohort? Which texts/modules and PPE are supplied? What practical assessments and any progression credit are explicitly included?
